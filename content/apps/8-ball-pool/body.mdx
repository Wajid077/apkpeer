## What is 8 Ball Pool?

8 Ball Pool is an online pool game from Miniclip. You play one-on-one matches of eight-ball against real people: break, pot all your solids or stripes, then sink the black 8 ball to win. Each match is played for coins, and winning lets you move up to more exclusive tables in different cities with bigger stakes.

It's been on Google Play since January 2013 and has passed a billion downloads, making it one of the most popular multiplayer games on Android. Part of its appeal is how simple it is to start: drag to aim, pull back the cue to set power, release to shoot. The depth comes from spin, angles and planning your next shot.

You can play friends or strangers, collect cues with better stats, and take part in seasonal events. It's one of the most-played [sports games](/category/sports) on Android, and a natural pick for anyone who likes quick head-to-head matches.

## Key features

### One-on-one online matches

Each match pairs you with another player at a table with a set entry fee in coins. The winner takes the pot. Higher-tier tables in different cities need more coins but pay out more.

### Cues and upgrades

Cues have stats for force, aim, spin and time, and better cues make long or tricky shots easier. You can win, buy or unlock cues through events and the Pool Pass.

### Spin and aiming

Set the cue ball's spin before you shoot to control where it goes after hitting another ball. Good spin control is the difference between potting one ball and clearing the table.

### Play with friends, spectate and cheer

Challenge friends from your friends list, and with this update you can watch friends' games and cheer them on.

### Events and seasons

Seasonal events and passes bring themed tables, cues and rewards, like the Día de los Muertos and Ocean Drive Heist events in this update.

## Tips for winning more matches

Think one shot ahead: pick the ball that leaves the cue ball in a good position for your next pot, not just the easiest one now. Use less power than you think; soft shots are more accurate. Learn to use top and back spin to control where the cue ball stops. And don't move up to expensive tables too fast. Build your coins at tables where you win consistently.

## 8 Ball Pool rules in brief

Each player is assigned a group, solids or stripes, after the first ball is potted. Pot all seven of your group, then call a pocket and sink the 8 ball there to win. If you pot the 8 ball early, or in the wrong pocket, you lose. Fouls, such as potting the cue ball or hitting your opponent's ball first, give your opponent "ball in hand", letting them place the cue ball anywhere. Every match is timed, so think quickly.
