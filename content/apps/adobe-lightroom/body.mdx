## What is Adobe Lightroom?

Adobe Lightroom is a photo and video editor from Adobe, the company behind Photoshop. The mobile app brings the same editing engine that professional photographers use on computers: precise controls for light, colour, detail and lens corrections, presets, and AI tools that can select a subject or sky and remove distractions. It works with JPEGs, RAW files and videos.

Adobe released Lightroom for Android in 2015. It has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play with a 4.5-star rating, and it's one of the [photo editor apps](/category/photo-editors) we list.

## Key features

### Light and colour controls

Adjust exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks, plus white balance, vibrance and saturation. The colour mixer and colour grading let you fine-tune individual colours and give a photo a consistent mood.

### Presets

Apply one-tap looks from Adobe's presets or your own. Premium presets and recommended presets suggest styles that suit each photo, and you can import presets you've bought or made on a computer.

### AI masking

Select the subject, sky, background, people or objects automatically, then edit just that area. You can brighten a face, darken a sky or blur a background without manual brushing.

### Remove and heal

Remove distractions like power lines or people in the background. Generative Remove, a premium feature, fills the area convincingly using Adobe's AI.

### RAW and pro camera

Edit RAW photos with more detail to work with, and use Lightroom's built-in camera with manual controls and DNG capture on supported phones.

### Video editing

Apply the same light and colour adjustments and presets to videos.

## How to install Lightroom APK on Android

Install [Lightroom from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adobe.lrmobile).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 8.0 or newer.
2. Open Google Play, search for Lightroom, and check the developer is Adobe.
3. Tap **Install**, open the app and sign in with an Adobe, Google, Facebook or Apple account.
4. Add photos from your gallery to start editing.

Adobe doesn't publish its own APK. Files from other sites aren't official.

## Free vs premium

The free version covers the essential tools: light, colour, effects, curves, crop and straighten, and many presets, with no ads. Lightroom's premium plan adds AI masking, Generative Remove and healing, geometry and perspective tools, advanced RAW features, and cloud storage that syncs your photos and edits with Lightroom on your computer and the web. Adobe's photography plans that include Photoshop also include Lightroom premium.

## Tips for better edits

- **Start with Auto.** Tap Auto for a starting point, then refine.
- **Watch the highlights.** Pull highlights down to rescue bright skies before increasing exposure.
- **Edit faces carefully.** Use masking on people rather than raising saturation for the whole photo.
- **Copy and paste settings.** Edit one photo from a series, then paste the settings onto the rest for a consistent look.
- **Save your own presets.** Once you find a look you like, save it so future edits take seconds.
- **Export at full size.** For printing, export at the largest size and highest quality.

## Is Lightroom APK safe?

The official Lightroom app is safe to install from Google Play. It's published by Adobe, rated Everyone, has no ads, and offers in-app purchases for premium plans.

- **Subscriptions.** Free trials turn into paid plans automatically. Cancel them in Google Play if you subscribed there, or in your Adobe account if you subscribed with Adobe.
- **Cloud storage.** With premium, photos you add are synced to Adobe's cloud. Turn sync off for any you'd rather keep only on your phone.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified Lightroom APKs that claim to include paid features aren't from Adobe. They require you to install files from unknown sources, can carry malware, and can't sync safely with Adobe's servers. APKPeer only lists the official app.


## Lightroom presets explained

Presets are saved combinations of edits that you can apply in one tap. Lightroom groups its built-in presets into styles such as portraits, travel, food and black and white. Premium users also get recommended presets, which suggest looks based on what's in each photo. You can create your own preset from any edit: tap the three-dot menu, choose **Create Preset**, and select which settings to include. Presets you sync from Lightroom on a computer appear on your phone with a premium plan, and you can also import preset files you've downloaded. Use a preset as a starting point, then adjust exposure and white balance for each photo.
