## Key features

### Search by text, voice or camera

Type, speak, or point your camera at a product or barcode to find it on Amazon.

### Order tracking

See where every package is, get delivery notifications and photos of the delivered parcel.

### Returns

Start a return from **Your Orders**, pick a drop-off or pickup option, and track the refund.

### Deals and lists

Check Today's Deals, add items to your Wish List or Cart, and get alerts on price drops for saved items in supported countries.

### App shortcuts

Hold the app icon to open Orders, Deals, Cart or Search straight away.

## Safe shopping on Amazon

- **Check the seller:** "Ships from and sold by Amazon" or a seller with lots of recent positive ratings is the safer pick.
- **Read recent reviews**, especially the 3-star ones, which are usually the most honest.
- **Ignore texts and calls** claiming to be Amazon about a "problem with your order". Check orders only inside the app.
- **Never pay outside Amazon** for an Amazon listing.

## Is Amazon Shopping APK safe?

The official app on Google Play is safe and published by Amazon Mobile LLC. It's rated Teen because of user content in listings and reviews, and Play notes it contains ads (sponsored products). Fake "Amazon" APKs and look-alike shopping apps are a common scam, so install only the Play version.

## Amazon on PC, iPhone and tablets

You can shop at amazon.com (or your local Amazon store) in any browser, and install the Amazon Shopping app from the App Store on iPhone. On Fire tablets the app is built in. Your cart, orders and lists sync through your Amazon account.

## Who should use the Amazon app

It suits regular Amazon shoppers and Prime members who want delivery alerts and quick reorders. If you only buy once in a while, the website works just as well without installing anything.
