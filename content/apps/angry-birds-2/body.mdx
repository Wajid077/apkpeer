## What is Angry Birds 2?

Angry Birds 2 is the sequel to one of the most famous mobile games ever. You use a slingshot to launch birds at the pigs' wobbly towers of wood, ice and stone, knocking them down with physics. Each level has several stages, and you choose which bird to launch from a hand of cards, so strategy matters as much as aim.

Made by Rovio Entertainment, the Finnish studio owned by SEGA since 2023, the game launched in July 2015. It has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play, and it's one of the [casual games](/category/casual) we list.

## Key features

### Multi-stage levels

Most levels have several stages, so you need to keep enough birds for later rooms.

### Bird cards

Choose which bird to launch next from your cards. Each bird has a special ability, like Chuck's speed boost or Bomb's explosion. Destruction fills the Destructometer, earning extra cards.

### Spells

Spells like Golden Duck and Blizzard help when you're stuck.

### Hats and upgrades

Collect hats to dress up your birds and raise their power.

### Clans, Arena and events

Join a clan for clan events, compete in the Arena against other players, and play daily challenges and seasonal events.

## Tips for three-star wins

- **Aim for weak points.** Hit supports and joints rather than walls.
- **Use the right bird.** Blue birds break ice, yellow birds smash wood, and Bomb is good against stone.
- **Fill the Destructometer.** More destruction earns extra bird cards.
- **Save spells for the last stage.** They're most valuable when you're about to lose.
- **Join an active clan.** Clan events give good rewards.

## Lives, gems and how to save them

Angry Birds 2 uses lives: each failed level costs one, and they refill over time. Gems let you buy extra birds or spells mid-level, but they're better saved for chests, events and the Arena. Daily challenges, the Tower of Fortune and clan events give free gems and spells, so playing them regularly reduces the temptation to buy. Watching an optional ad sometimes gives an extra bird or spell when you're close to winning. If children play, make sure purchases need a password in Google Play.

## The Arena and clans

The Arena puts you head to head with another player on the same level, and the highest score wins. Your birds' hat sets and levels matter here, so upgrade the birds you use most. Clans give you a group to chat with, clan quests with shared rewards, and a clan leaderboard. Joining an active clan is one of the best ways to progress steadily without spending.
