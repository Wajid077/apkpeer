## What is Archero?

Archero is a roguelike action game from Habby, and the Archero APK is its Android installer. You control a lone archer who fights through rooms of monsters. Move to dodge and stop to shoot automatically. Every level-up offers a choice of skills, so each run builds differently. Clear the chapter's boss to unlock harder chapters.

## Archero tips

- **Keep moving** between enemy attacks, then stop to shoot.
- **Take Multishot and Front Arrow** early in most runs.
- **Avoid too many elemental skills** if you need raw damage.
- **Upgrade your weapon first**, then armour and rings.
