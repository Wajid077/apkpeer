## What is Asphalt Legends?

Asphalt Legends is Gameloft's arcade racing game, and the latest form of a series that's been on phones for years. You drive licensed supercars and hypercars from brands like Ferrari, Lamborghini and Bugatti through cities and deserts, boosting with nitro, launching off ramps, and knocking rivals off the road. It's about speed and spectacle rather than realistic handling.

The game launched in 2018 as Asphalt 9: Legends, was later renamed Asphalt Legends Unite, and is now listed on Google Play as "Asphalt Legends - Racing Game." It's the same app and package throughout, so your garage carries over. It has passed 100 million downloads.

It looks impressive on a good phone, needs Android 9 or newer, and plays online with cross-platform multiplayer. It's one of the best-looking [racing games](/category/racing) on Android.

## Key features

### Licensed supercars

The garage includes hundreds of real cars from top manufacturers, each with upgrades for top speed, acceleration, handling and nitro. You collect blueprints to unlock and improve them.

### TouchDrive or manual controls

**TouchDrive** steers automatically and lets you choose routes and stunts with taps, which makes the game easy to pick up. Manual controls give you full steering, drifting and nitro timing for more skilled play.

### Stunts and shortcuts

Every track has ramps, barrel rolls, 360-degree spins and hidden shortcuts. Stunts fill your nitro bar, and finding the fastest route matters as much as your car.

### Career, events and multiplayer

The career mode takes you through seasons of races, while daily and limited events offer special cars and rewards. Multiplayer races pit you against real players, including on other platforms.

### Clubs

Join a club to race with others towards shared goals and rewards, and compare times with friends. An active club is one of the steadiest sources of extra rewards in the game.

## Tips for faster races

Start with TouchDrive while you learn the tracks, then switch to manual for better control in events. Learn where the shortcuts are, since they often beat a faster car on the main road. Save nitro for straights and use the perfect nitro (a second tap at the right moment) for an extra boost. And focus upgrades on a few cars you race often rather than spreading parts across your whole garage.

## Car classes explained

Cars in Asphalt Legends are grouped into classes, from D (the entry level) through C, B and A to S (the fastest). Many races and events only allow cars from a certain class, so a fully upgraded B-class car can be more useful than a barely upgraded S-class one. Each car also has a star rank, unlocked by collecting blueprints, that raises how far it can be upgraded. When choosing what to work on, look at which classes the current events need.
