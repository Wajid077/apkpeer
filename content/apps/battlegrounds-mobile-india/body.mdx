## What is BGMI?

BGMI (Battlegrounds Mobile India) is KRAFTON's battle royale made for players in India. The BGMI APK installs it on Android: up to 100 players drop onto maps like Erangel and Miramar, loot, and fight to be the last squad standing. It was launched in 2021 after PUBG Mobile was banned in India, was blocked in 2022, and returned in 2023.

It's one of the [action games](/category/action) we cover, alongside the global <AppLink slug="pubg-mobile">PUBG Mobile</AppLink>. We read its details from the Indian Google Play store.
