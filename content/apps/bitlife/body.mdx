## What is BitLife?

BitLife is a life simulator told almost entirely in text. You're born as a random character in a random country, and each tap of the **Age** button moves you forward a year. Along the way, life happens: school, friends, relationships, jobs, illness, luck, and your own choices. You decide whether to study hard, become a doctor or a criminal, marry, travel, or spend everything at the casino.

It's made by Candywriter, LLC, and has been on Google Play since February 2019, with more than 50 million downloads. The fun is in the stories that come out of small decisions, and in trying wildly different lives one after another.

There's almost no graphics beyond simple icons, which makes it light on storage and battery. It's one of the most popular [simulation games](/category/simulation) on Android, though its age rating means it's meant for older teens and adults.

## Key features

### Live a life through choices

Each year, you can pick activities from menus: study, see a doctor, go to the gym, date, buy a house, commit a crime, or emigrate. Random events, such as getting sick or winning the lottery, keep things unpredictable.

### Careers and education

Go to school and university, pick a major, and apply for jobs from fast food to surgeon, lawyer or astronaut. Some careers need specific degrees or high stats.

### Relationships and family

Make friends, date, marry, have children, and keep in touch with parents and siblings. When your character dies, you can continue as one of your children, building a family story across generations.

### Achievements and challenges

Achievements and weekly challenges give you goals, such as becoming a famous actor or living to 100, which push you into lives you wouldn't otherwise try. Completing them also unlocks ribbons that sum up each life.

### Paid extras

Optional purchases add features: Bitizenship removes ads and adds bonus features, and God Mode lets you change your character's appearance and stats and control other characters. Special packs add careers and scenarios.

## Tips for a successful life

Keep your character's health and happiness up by visiting the gym, the doctor and friends regularly; low stats cause problems later. Study hard in school, because many of the best careers depend on education. Save money before making big purchases like houses. And try a weekly challenge when you're out of ideas: they often lead to the funniest lives.

## What BitLife's stats mean

| Stat | What it affects |
| --- | --- |
| Happiness | Mood; low happiness leads to bad events and depression |
| Health | Illness and life expectancy |
| Smarts | School, university and which careers you can get |
| Looks | Relationships and some careers, like modelling or acting |

Most activities raise one stat and sometimes lower another. Going to the gym helps health and looks; studying helps smarts; spending time with friends and family helps happiness. Keeping all four balanced makes for a longer, easier life.

## Is BitLife APK safe?

The official BitLife APK is safe to install. It's published by Candywriter, LLC on Google Play. Play marks it as containing ads and in-app purchases, from $0.99 to $89.99 per item.

The age rating is the important part: Google Play rates BitLife Mature 17+ for sexual content and drug references. It's all text, but characters can make adult choices. Parents can block it with Google Play's content filters or Family Link.

You'll find modified BitLife APKs claiming to unlock God Mode, Bitizenship and every pack for free. They aren't from Candywriter, they can contain adware, and they stop getting the new content updates. APKPeer only links to the official game.
