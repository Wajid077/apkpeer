## What is Bitwarden?

Bitwarden is an open-source password manager, and the Bitwarden APK is its Android app. It stores your passwords, passkeys, card details and secure notes in an encrypted vault, generates strong passwords, and fills logins in apps and browsers. Your vault syncs across Android, iPhone, desktop and browser extensions.

It's one of the [tools](/category/tools) we recommend for staying safe online, alongside a VPN like <AppLink slug="proton-vpn">Proton VPN</AppLink> for public Wi-Fi.

## Key features

### Encrypted vault

Everything is encrypted on your device with your master password before it syncs.

### Autofill

Fill usernames and passwords in apps and browsers using Android's autofill service or the inline keyboard suggestions.

### Password generator

Create long random passwords or passphrases, and save a new login straight from the generator.

### Passkeys

Store and use passkeys for supported sites, so you can sign in without a password.

### Send

Share text or files securely with a link that expires.

## Bitwarden autofill not working? Fixes

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| No autofill prompt | Settings → Autofill: enable the Autofill service |
| Wrong login suggested | Edit the item's URI to match the app or site |
| Autofill stops after a while | Remove battery restrictions for Bitwarden |
| Vault won't sync | Pull down to sync; check the server region (US/EU) |

## Setting up Bitwarden safely

1. Choose a **long master password**, such as four random words, and write it down somewhere safe.
2. Turn on **two-step login** for your Bitwarden account.
3. Import passwords from your browser (export them as CSV on a computer, then delete the CSV).
4. Use the generator to replace weak or reused passwords over time.

## Is Bitwarden safe?

Yes. Bitwarden is open-source, regularly audited by independent security firms, and uses end-to-end encryption, so Bitwarden can't read your vault. The official app on Google Play is published by Bitwarden Inc. and has no ads. Your biggest risk is a weak or reused master password, so make it unique.

## Bitwarden vs alternatives

- **Google Password Manager** in <AppLink slug="google-chrome">Chrome</AppLink> is built in but tied to Google.
- **1Password** is polished but has no free plan.
- **Proton Pass** comes from the makers of <AppLink slug="proton-vpn">Proton VPN</AppLink>.

## Bitwarden on PC, iPhone and browsers

Bitwarden has apps for Windows, Mac, Linux and iPhone, extensions for Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari, and a web vault at vault.bitwarden.com. Your vault syncs across them all on the free plan.

## Who should use Bitwarden

Anyone who reuses passwords. It's especially good for people who use several browsers or switch between Android and iPhone.

## Final thoughts

The Bitwarden APK from Google Play is the best free password manager for most Android users. Pick a strong master password, turn on autofill and two-step login, and replace reused passwords one site at a time.
