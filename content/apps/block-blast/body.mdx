## What is Block Blast!?

Block Blast! is a block puzzle game from HungryStudio. The Block Blast APK installs the official game on Android: you get three shapes at a time and place them on an 8x8 grid. Fill a row or column to clear it and score; the game ends when none of your shapes fits. There's no timer, so it's all about planning.

It's one of the most downloaded [puzzle games](/category/puzzle) of recent years, with more than 1 billion downloads on Google Play.

## Tips for higher Block Blast scores

- **Keep the middle open** so big squares always fit.
- **Check all three shapes** before placing the first.

- **Keep your combo going** by clearing a line every turn.

Many players warm up with an Adventure level, then switch to Classic.
