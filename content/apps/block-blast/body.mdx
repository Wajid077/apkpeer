## What is Block Blast!?

Block Blast! is a simple block puzzle game. You get three blocks at a time in different shapes, and you drag them onto an 8x8 grid. When you fill a whole row or column, it clears and you score points. The game ends when none of your three blocks fits on the board. There's no timer and no gravity: just you, the grid and some careful planning.

Made by HungryStudio, the game came to Google Play in September 2022 and has passed 1 billion downloads, making it one of the most downloaded mobile games of recent years. It has a 4.8-star rating, and it's one of the [puzzle games](/category/puzzle) we list.

## Key features

### Classic mode

The endless mode most people play. Place blocks, clear lines, and chase a new high score. Every three blocks you place, you get three new ones, so you need to keep space for awkward shapes.

### Adventure mode

Adventure mode adds levels with goals, such as clearing specific gems or reaching a target score, with themed pictures revealed as you progress. It gives the game a sense of progression beyond your high score.

### Combos and multi-line clears

Clearing lines on consecutive moves builds a combo, and clearing several lines with one block gives a big bonus. The best scores come from setting up multi-line clears on purpose.

### Offline and quick

No internet needed, no account required, and a round can last two minutes or two hours. It runs smoothly on older and low-cost phones.

## Tips for higher scores

- **Keep the board open.** Leave large empty areas rather than scattering blocks, so big shapes like the 3x3 square always fit.
- **Plan for all three blocks.** Before placing the first block, check that the other two will still have somewhere to go.
- **Save corners for awkward shapes.** L-shapes and long lines fit neatly along edges and into corners.
- **Build towards multi-line clears.** Fill two or three rows almost completely, then drop one block that finishes them together.
- **Keep the combo alive.** If you can clear a line every turn, your combo bonus grows quickly.
- **Don't rush.** There's no timer, so take a moment before each placement.

Watch out for copycat games with almost the same name and icon; many exist on app stores and are packed with more aggressive ads.
