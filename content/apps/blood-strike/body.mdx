## What is Blood Strike?

Blood Strike is a free first-person shooter from NetEase Games. It offers battle royale matches and faster multiplayer modes, with characters called Strikers who each have special abilities. It's designed to run smoothly on a wide range of Android phones, which has made it popular with players whose devices struggle with bigger shooters.

Blood Strike launched in December 2023 and has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [action games](/category/action) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="call-of-duty-mobile">Call of Duty: Mobile</AppLink>.

## Graphics settings for smooth play

In a shooter, a steady frame rate matters more than detailed graphics. In Blood Strike's settings, try lowering graphics quality and shadows while raising the frame rate as high as your phone allows. If your phone gets hot during long sessions, lower the frame rate a little; overheating causes the phone to slow itself down. Turning off other apps, notifications and background downloads also helps. On older phones, the lowest graphics preset usually gives the most responsive aiming.

## Playing with friends and squads

Blood Strike is more fun and more successful with a squad you can talk to. Add friends in the game, form a team before matchmaking, and use voice chat or quick commands to share enemy positions. In battle royale, agree on a landing spot before the drop, share loot like healing items and ammo, and revive teammates quickly. If you play alone, quick team modes are a good way to practise before taking on full battle royale matches.
