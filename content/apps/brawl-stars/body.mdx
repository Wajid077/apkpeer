## What is Brawl Stars?

Brawl Stars is a fast multiplayer action game from Supercell, the Finnish studio behind Clash of Clans. Matches last about two to three minutes. You pick a Brawler, each with its own attack, Super ability and play style, and fight in teams of three or in free-for-all Showdown. The controls are simple: one joystick to move, one to aim, and a button for your Super.

Brawl Stars launched worldwide in December 2018 and has passed 500 million downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [action games](/category/action) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="stumble-guys">Stumble Guys</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Game modes

The main 3v3 modes rotate through the day: Gem Grab (collect and hold 10 gems), Brawl Ball (score two goals), Heist (break the enemy safe), Bounty (earn stars for eliminations), Knockout (best of three rounds with no respawns), and Hot Zone (control zones). Showdown is a battle royale for 10 players, solo or in duos.

### Brawlers

Brawlers come in rarities from Rare to Legendary, plus Ultra Legendary. Each has a main attack and a Super, and you unlock Gadgets, Star Powers, Gears and Hypercharges as you upgrade them.

### Brawl Pass and Trophy Road

Earn rewards by playing through the free Trophy Road and the Brawl Pass, which has a free track and a paid Brawl Pass Plus track.

### Ranked

Ranked mode uses drafts with picks and bans, and Update 70 refreshes it for solo players with better matchmaking.

### Clubs and friends

Join a club to chat and play with others, and team up with friends using invite codes.

## Tips for new players

- **Learn a few Brawlers well.** Mastering two or three gives better results than switching every game.
- **Match Brawlers to modes.** Tanky close-range Brawlers shine in Brawl Ball; long-range snipers suit Bounty and Knockout.
- **Use bushes.** Hiding in bushes sets up ambushes, but enemies can reveal them by attacking.
- **Charge your Super, then use it well.** A well-timed Super wins more games than a quick one.
- **Play with friends.** Voice or chat coordination makes 3v3 modes far easier.
- **Don't rush pushes.** In Gem Grab, holding gems at the back wins more often than chasing kills.

## Is Brawl Stars APK safe?

The official Brawl Stars game from Google Play is safe. It's published by Supercell, rated Everyone 10+ for fantasy violence and crude humour, has no ads, and offers in-app purchases from $0.29 to $239.99 per item.

Watch out for these:

- **Free gems websites.** Sites and videos promising free gems or Brawlers are scams. They ask for your Supercell ID login or make you complete surveys and install other apps.
- **Private servers and modified APKs.** These aren't from Supercell, can carry malware, and don't connect to your real account. Supercell bans accounts caught using cheats or third-party tools.
- **Spending.** Turn on purchase authentication in Google Play, and use Supercell ID's parental settings if your child plays.
