## What is Bus Simulator Indonesia?

Bus Simulator Indonesia, known by fans as BUSSID, is a driving simulator where you drive intercity buses along routes through Indonesian cities and landscapes. You pick up passengers at terminals, follow traffic, manage your speed and fuel, and earn money to buy new buses. What makes it stand out is the community: players design their own bus liveries and drive together in online convoys.

Indonesian developer Maleo released the game in March 2017. It has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play, and it's one of the [simulation games](/category/simulation) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="car-parking-multiplayer">Car Parking Multiplayer</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Indonesian roads and cities

Drive authentic-feeling routes between Indonesian cities, with toll roads, traffic, rest areas and landmarks. Day and night cycles and weather change how each trip feels.

### Your own liveries

Design your own livery, or use one shared by the community, to make your bus look exactly how you want. The new sticker system adds more ways to decorate.

### Online convoys

Join multiplayer convoys and drive with friends or other players, chat, and follow each other along routes.

### Buses and upgrades

Earn money from trips and spend it on new buses and upgrades. Different buses handle differently, from city buses to big double-deckers.

### Authentic details

Musical "telolet" horns, working indicators, wipers, passenger announcements and realistic cockpit views make the game feel distinctly Indonesian.

### Controls

Choose between steering wheel, tilt or buttons, with automatic or manual gearbox.

## Tips for new drivers

- **Drive smoothly.** Sudden braking and crashes reduce your earnings.
- **Follow traffic rules.** Stop at red lights and use indicators to avoid fines.
- **Refuel before long trips.** Running out of fuel ends your trip early.
- **Pick the right camera.** The cockpit camera is more immersive; the chase camera makes manoeuvring easier.
- **Save money for a better bus.** A comfortable, reliable bus makes longer routes easier and more profitable.
- **Join a convoy.** Driving with others is the best part of the game.

## Is Bus Simulator Indonesia APK safe?

The official Bus Simulator Indonesia from Google Play is safe. It's published by Maleo, rated Everyone, contains ads, and has no in-app purchases listed on Play.

- **Ads.** Ads appear between trips and as optional rewards.
- **Livery and vehicle files from the community.** BUSSID supports community-made vehicles and liveries. Download them only from trusted community sources, and never install separate APK files that claim to add vehicles.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified BUSSID APKs with free money aren't from Maleo, can carry malware, and can break your saved progress or online play.
