## What is Candy Crush Saga?

Candy Crush Saga is a match-three puzzle game from King, the studio owned by Microsoft through its purchase of Activision Blizzard. You swap two neighbouring candies to line up three or more of the same color, which clears them and drops new candies from above. Each level gives you a goal, such as reaching a score, clearing jelly or bringing ingredients to the bottom, and a limited number of moves to do it.

It sounds simple, and the first levels are. The depth comes from special candies: match four in a row for a striped candy that clears a line, five in an L or T shape for a wrapped candy that explodes, and five in a row for a color bomb. Combining them is how you beat the hard levels.

Candy Crush Saga has been on Google Play since November 2012 and has passed a billion downloads. It's the best-known [puzzle game](/category/puzzle) on mobile, and King still updates it every week.

## Key features

### Thousands of levels

Levels are laid out on a map, grouped into episodes with their own themes. Each one has a goal and a move limit, and you earn up to three stars depending on your score.

### Special candies and combos

Striped, wrapped and color bomb candies are the key to high scores and hard levels. Combining two special candies, such as a striped with a wrapped candy, creates much bigger effects.

### Boosters

Boosters, such as the lollipop hammer or extra moves, help with difficult levels. You earn some through play and events, and can buy more with gold bars.

### Lives

You have up to five lives. Failing a level costs one, and they refill over time. It's a natural break point, and the main reason people spend money in the game.

### Events and rewards

Regular events and challenges give extra rewards for winning streaks and completing tasks, and you can send lives to friends who also play.

## Tips for hard levels

Look for moves that create special candies before making simple matches, since a single color bomb can do more than ten ordinary moves. Make matches near the bottom of the board when you can: falling candies create free cascades. Save your boosters for levels that are close to the target, and if you're stuck, take a break. Lives refill, and coming back fresh helps more than you'd think.

## Special candies at a glance

| Match | You get | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 in a row | Striped candy | Clears a whole row or column |
| 5 in an L or T shape | Wrapped candy | Explodes twice, clearing the area around it |
| 5 in a row | Color bomb | Clears every candy of the color you swap it with |
| Square of 4 | Fish | Swims to a random target on the board |

Swapping two special candies together creates an even bigger effect, and two color bombs clear the whole board.
