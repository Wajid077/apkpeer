## Key features

### Level goals

Clear jelly, bring down ingredients, collect orders or reach a target score, each within a move limit.

### Special candies

Match four for a striped candy, an L or T shape for a wrapped candy, and five in a row for a colour bomb.

### Boosters

Lollipop hammers, colour bombs and extra moves help on hard levels.

### Events

Daily rewards, team events and seasonal challenges give free boosters and lives.

## Tips to beat hard Candy Crush levels

- **Make matches at the bottom** to cause cascades higher up.
- **Combine special candies:** striped + wrapped clears three rows and columns; colour bomb + striped turns a colour into striped candies.
- **Focus on the level goal**, not on the score.
- **Save boosters for hard and super hard levels**, marked on the map.
- **Take a break** if a level seems impossible; the board shuffles differently each time.

## Gold bars and spending

Gold bars are Candy Crush's premium currency for boosters, extra moves and lives. You can finish levels without paying; patience and free event rewards go a long way. Set a budget if you do buy, and keep purchase authentication on.

## Level types explained

Candy Crush mixes several level types. **Jelly levels** ask you to clear every jelly square. **Ingredient levels** need you to bring cherries and hazelnuts down to the bottom. **Order levels** list specific candies or combinations to collect. **Score levels** just need a target score. Reading the goal before your first move, shown at the top of the board, is the simplest way to stop wasting moves on matches that don't help.
