## What is Canva?

Canva is a design app for people who aren't designers. You pick a template for whatever you're making, such as an Instagram post, a YouTube thumbnail, a birthday invite, a poster, a presentation or a short video, then swap in your own text, photos and colors. The Android app is listed on Google Play as "Canva: AI Photo & Video Editor" and is published by Canva, the Australian company behind [canva.com](https://www.canva.com/).

It's been on Google Play since November 2017 and has more than 500 million downloads. The phone app does most of what the website does, and your designs sync between the two, so you can start something on your commute and finish it on a laptop.

Canva has grown into one of the most popular [photo and design apps](/category/photo-editors). Alongside templates, it now includes photo editing, video editing and a set of AI tools for generating images, writing text and removing backgrounds.

## Key features

### Templates for almost everything

Search for what you're making, like "Instagram story", "menu" or "resume", and Canva shows templates already sized correctly. Tap one, then tap any text, photo or element to change it. It's the fastest way to make something that looks professionally designed.

### Photo editing

Upload photos to crop, adjust brightness and color, apply filters, and add text or stickers. The background remover, a Pro feature, cuts a person or product out of a photo in one tap, which is handy for product shots and thumbnails.

### Video editing

Canva includes a simple video editor with a timeline, transitions, music and text animations. It works well for short social videos and slideshows, especially when you start from a template.

### AI tools

Canva's AI tools can generate images from a description, write or rewrite text, and resize a design for different platforms. Some are available on the free plan with limits, and more come with Pro.

### Brand kit and teamwork

On paid plans, a brand kit stores your logos, colors and fonts so every design stays consistent. You can share designs with others to edit together or leave comments.

The free plan is genuinely enough for many people. Pro pays off if you design regularly for a business or need the background remover.

## Tips for better designs in Canva

Start from a template made for the exact format you need, so the size is right from the beginning. Limit yourself to two fonts and a few colors from the template, rather than adding more, and leave some empty space: crowded designs are harder to read on a phone screen. Before downloading, zoom in to check spelling and alignment. For social media, download as PNG for sharp text, or MP4 for anything with animation.

## Is Canva APK safe?

The official Canva app is safe. It's published by Canva on Google Play, has no ads, and is rated Everyone 10+, with a note that user content varies. Your designs are stored in your Canva account, so treat that account like any other important login and turn on two-step verification.

Canva needs an internet connection for almost everything, because templates, photos and your designs live online. Photos you upload are stored in your account until you delete them.

Modified "Canva Pro" APKs are common on download sites. They aren't made by Canva, they can steal your login, and since Pro content and AI features run on Canva's servers, they usually don't work anyway. APKPeer only links to the official app.
