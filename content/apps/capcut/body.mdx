## What is CapCut?

CapCut is a free video editor for phones made by Bytedance, the company behind TikTok. It's listed on Google Play as "CapCut - Video Editor," has been there since April 2020, and has passed a billion downloads. If you've seen a short video with bouncy captions and smooth transitions, there's a good chance it was edited in CapCut.

It sits between simple trimming apps and professional editors. You get a proper multitrack timeline, keyframes, speed curves, green screen and background removal, but also one-tap templates and automatic captions for when you want something done in a minute.

Most of it is free, and the app has no ads. CapCut Pro is an optional subscription for premium effects, AI tools and cloud storage. It's one of the most popular [video editing apps](/category/video-editors) on Android, and a good default if you make short videos for social media.

## Key features

### Templates

The **Templates** tab is full of ready-made edits, many based on trends. Pick one, swap in your own clips and photos, and CapCut handles the cuts, effects and music timing. It's the fastest way to make a polished short video.

### Auto captions

Tap **Text → Auto captions** and CapCut transcribes your speech and adds timed subtitles. You can edit the words and choose from animated caption styles. Captions matter on social media, where many people watch without sound.

### A real timeline

Below the preview is a multitrack timeline for video, overlays, text, audio and effects. You can split clips, adjust speed with curves, add keyframes to animate position or scale, and layer picture-in-picture clips.

### Background removal and green screen

**Remove background** cuts a person out of a video without a green screen. If you did film on a green screen, **Chroma key** removes the color. Both are handy for reaction videos and explainers.

### Export options

Export in different resolutions and frame rates, up to 4K on devices that support it. You can post straight to TikTok or save to your gallery.

## Tips for faster editing in CapCut

Start from a template when you can, then replace the clips; it's quicker than building transitions yourself. Use auto captions, then read through them once, since names and slang are often misheard. For social media, exporting at 1080p and 30 frames per second keeps files small without a visible quality loss on phones. And keep your original clips in your gallery: if you uninstall CapCut or clear its data, local projects can disappear with it.

Install [CapCut from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lemon.lvoverseas) if it's available in your country. Without Play Store, use the official app carefully.

1. Check your phone runs Android 6.0 or newer.

3. If you downloaded the file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We currently link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for CapCut.
4. Open the APK and allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks.
5. Tap **Install**, then open CapCut. You can edit without an account, but signing in saves projects to the cloud.
6. Turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is CapCut APK safe?

The official CapCut app is safe to install. It's published by Bytedance Pte. Ltd. on Google Play, it has no ads, and it's rated Teen.

Privacy is the bigger question. CapCut is owned by Bytedance, and like TikTok it has faced government action in some countries: India has banned CapCut with other Bytedance apps since 2020. Its terms of service give CapCut broad rights over content you upload to its services. That's worth reading before you edit client work or anything confidential, and it's a good reason to keep sensitive footage out of cloud projects.

Google Play lists in-app purchases from $0.49 to $900.00, which covers Pro subscriptions of different lengths and team plans. Check the plan and renewal terms before subscribing.

The real danger is "CapCut Pro" modified APKs on download sites. They aren't made by Bytedance, they can steal your login, and Pro AI features run on CapCut's servers, so they often don't work anyway. APKPeer only links to the official app.
