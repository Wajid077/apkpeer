## Free vs CapCut Pro

The free version includes the full timeline editor, thousands of templates, auto captions, basic effects and exports up to 4K. CapCut Pro, a subscription bought in the app, unlocks premium effects, filters, fonts and templates, extra AI tools and more cloud storage. Pro items are marked with a badge in the editor, and a project containing them can only be exported on Pro, which is the most common reason exports fail for free users.

## Who CapCut is for

CapCut suits anyone making short social videos: TikTok and Reels creators, small businesses making product clips, and students editing school projects. It's more capable than most free mobile editors, but long, complex projects with many tracks are still easier on the desktop version.

## Final thoughts

The CapCut APK from Google Play is the official, free way to edit videos on Android. Remove Pro elements if export fails, keep plenty of storage free, and update regularly for the newest editing tools.
