## What is Car Parking Multiplayer?

Car Parking Multiplayer started as a parking game and grew into an open-world driving sandbox. It's made by olzhass, a small independent developer, and has been on Google Play since March 2017, where it has passed 100 million downloads.

There are two sides to it. The single-player side has 82 parking and driving challenges, from squeezing a sedan into a tight bay to reversing a tow truck. The multiplayer side drops you into an open world with real players, where you can drive, walk around, fill up at gas stations, race, trade cars and chat by voice.

What keeps people playing is the car culture. You can tune engines, swap gearboxes and turbos, adjust suspension and wheel angle, and wrap cars in custom vinyls. It's one of the most popular [simulation games](/category/simulation) on Android.

## Key features

### Open-world multiplayer

Join a server and you're in a shared world with other players. You can leave your car and walk, use gas stations and car services, race, and trade cars with real players. There's a friend list, voice chat, and a police mode for players who want to role-play.

### Car tuning

Every car can be modified. Swap the engine, add a turbo, change the gearbox and exhaust, and adjust the suspension and wheel angle. On the visual side there are dynamic vinyls and body parts, so no two cars on a server look the same.

### 82 parking and driving challenges

The single-player campaign is where the game started: real-life parking situations that get harder as you go, with different vehicles such as tow trucks, pickups, trucks, sports cars and classics. It's the best place to learn the controls before going online.

### 100 cars with real interiors

The garage has around 100 cars, and each has a modeled interior, so first-person driving looks the part. There are also 16 player skins for when you're walking around.

### Creator's maps

Players can build and publish their own maps. Version 4.9.11 added ratings for these maps and a publication form, so good community maps are easier to find.

## Tips for new drivers

Start with the single-player parking challenges before going online. They teach you the controls, and finishing them earns money for your first upgrades. Try the different camera views until you find one that suits tight spaces; an overhead or rear view usually makes parking easier. When you join a multiplayer server, spend a few minutes driving around before trading or racing, so you know how the open world is laid out. And tune one car well rather than buying lots of cheap ones.

## Is Car Parking Multiplayer APK safe?

The official game is safe to install. It's published by olzhass on Google Play, where it's rated Everyone. Play marks it as containing ads and in-app purchases (from $0.99 to $69.99 per item), and lists "Users Interact" as an interactive element, because of chat and voice chat.

The real risks are social. Open-world servers mean voice chat with strangers, so younger players should be careful, and parents may want to check the game's chat settings. Car trading is a built-in feature, but scams are common: never share your account login for a trade, and ignore people selling accounts or "free money" on social media.

Modified versions of Car Parking Multiplayer are widely shared, promising all cars or endless money. They aren't made by olzhass, they can carry malware, and because the multiplayer servers check accounts, players who use them risk losing their account. APKPeer only links to the official game.
