## What is Carrom Pool?

Carrom Pool is an online version of carrom, the popular tabletop game from South Asia. You flick a striker to knock discs into the four corner pockets of the board. In Classic mode, the traditional rules apply, including the red queen. Disc Pool and Freestyle offer simpler, faster variations for quick matches.

Carrom Pool is made by Miniclip, the studio behind <AppLink slug="8-ball-pool">8 Ball Pool</AppLink>. It launched in 2019 and has passed 500 million downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [board games](/category/board) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="ludo-king">Ludo King</AppLink>.

## Tips for better aim

- **Use the guideline.** Line up the striker so the guide hits the disc at the right angle.
- **Control power.** Gentle shots are often better than full power.
- **Plan your next shot.** Leave discs near pockets for your next turn.
- **Cover the queen.** In Classic mode, pocket a disc of your own colour straight after the queen to claim it.
- **Practise offline.** Practice mode helps you learn angles without losing coins.

## Carrom rules in Classic mode

In Classic mode, each player is assigned white or black discs. You pocket your own discs by flicking the striker from your baseline. The red disc, called the queen, is worth extra, but only if you "cover" it by pocketing one of your own discs straight after it. If you fail to cover the queen, it returns to the centre. Pocketing the striker is a foul, which costs you a disc. The first player to pocket all their discs wins. Disc Pool and Freestyle simplify these rules, which makes them good for beginners.

## Managing coins and strikers

Matches have an entry fee in coins, with bigger rewards in higher arenas. Don't move up to an expensive arena too quickly: losing a few matches there can wipe out your coins. Upgrading a striker improves its aim, force and time, but even the starter striker is fine while you learn. Daily rewards, free gifts and the pass give coins without spending money.
