## What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is OpenAI's AI assistant, and this is its official Android app. You type or speak a question and get an answer in plain language: explaining a topic, drafting an email, summarizing a document, translating, planning a trip or helping with code. It's the same ChatGPT you use at [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com/), with the same account and chat history.

The app arrived on Google Play in July 2023 and has passed a billion downloads. The phone version adds things that make more sense on a phone than on a computer: talking to ChatGPT out loud, pointing your camera at something and asking about it, and sharing text or images into it from other apps.

It's one of the most useful [productivity apps](/category/productivity) you can install, as long as you treat its answers as a helpful first draft rather than the final word. AI assistants can be confidently wrong, so check anything important.

## Key features

### Chat by text or voice

Type in the message box, or tap the voice button to talk. In voice mode you can have a spoken back-and-forth conversation, which is handy while cooking, walking or driving. Your voice chats appear in your history afterward as text.

### Ask about photos and files

Tap the **+** button to take a photo, pick an image, or attach a file such as a PDF. You can ask ChatGPT to read a menu in another language, explain a chart, or summarize a long document.

### Web search

ChatGPT can search the web for current information and shows the sources it used, so you can check them. It's useful for recent news, prices or anything that changes often.

### Chat history and memory

Your conversations are saved so you can return to them, and ChatGPT can remember preferences you share. You can view and delete memories, or use a temporary chat that isn't saved, from the app's settings.

### Share into ChatGPT

Use Android's share menu to send text, links or images from other apps straight into a new chat. It's the quickest way to ask about something you're reading.

## How to install ChatGPT APK on Android

Install [ChatGPT from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.openai.chatgpt) if you can, and check that the developer is listed as OpenAI. Without Play Store, sideload the official app carefully.

1. Check your phone runs Android 7.1 or newer.
2. Get the app only from Google Play or links on chatgpt.com. There are many fake ChatGPT apps.
3. If you downloaded the file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We currently link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for ChatGPT.
4. Open the APK and allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks.
5. Tap **Install**, open ChatGPT, and sign in with your OpenAI, Google, Microsoft or Apple account.
6. Turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is ChatGPT APK safe?

The official ChatGPT app is safe to install. It's published by OpenAI on Google Play, rated Teen, and its permissions match its features: microphone for voice, camera and files for attachments, and notifications. You can review them under **Settings → Apps → ChatGPT → Permissions**.

Privacy deserves attention, because people tell AI assistants a lot. By default, OpenAI may use conversations from personal accounts to train its models. Turn that off under **Settings → Data Controls** if you'd rather it didn't, and avoid pasting passwords, ID numbers or other people's private information into any chatbot.

The biggest risk is fake apps. Search for ChatGPT on Android and you'll see lookalike icons and names from other developers, some charging for a thin wrapper around someone else's service. Check the package name `com.openai.chatgpt` and the developer name OpenAI. APKPeer only links to the official app.
