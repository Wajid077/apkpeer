## Key features

### Chat and writing help

Ask anything in plain language, then follow up. ChatGPT drafts emails and essays, rewrites text, summarises articles and explains topics step by step.

### Voice mode

Talk to ChatGPT out loud and hear it answer, with natural back-and-forth. It's handy for practising a language or thinking out loud.

### Photos, files and images

Upload a photo, PDF or spreadsheet and ask about it, or describe an image and have ChatGPT create it.

### Search

ChatGPT can search the web and cite the pages it used, which helps with news and anything recent.

### Projects and memory

Group chats and files into projects, and let ChatGPT remember preferences you choose. Manage or turn off memory in settings.

## Tips for getting better answers

- **Give context.** Say who it's for and what you need: "Explain this to a 12-year-old in 5 bullet points."
- **Ask it to check itself.** For maths or facts, ask ChatGPT to show its working or list what it's unsure of.
- **Use Temporary Chat** for anything you don't want saved.
- **Turn on search** for current events, then open the cited sources.

## Is ChatGPT APK safe?

The official ChatGPT app from Google Play is safe. It's published by OpenAI and rated Teen. Google Play marks it as containing ads.

- **Privacy.** Turn off **Improve the model for everyone** in **Settings → Data controls** if you don't want chats used for training.
- **Accuracy.** Check important medical, legal and financial answers against a reliable source.
- **Fake apps.** Many lookalike "AI chat" apps charge weekly subscriptions. The real app's developer is OpenAI.
- **Kids.** Under-18s need parental consent; parents can link accounts for controls.

## Free vs paid ChatGPT

The free plan covers everyday chat, writing help, photo and file questions, voice mode and web search, with limits that reset over time. When you hit a limit, ChatGPT switches to a lighter model or asks you to wait. Paid plans, bought inside the app through Google Play, raise those limits and unlock OpenAI's most capable models and extra tools. Cancel a subscription any time under **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions**. If you only use ChatGPT a few times a week, the free plan is usually enough.
