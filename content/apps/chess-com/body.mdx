## How to improve faster

- Play **Rapid** (10 minutes or more) to have time to think.
- Do **a few puzzles every day**; tactics win most beginner games.
- Read your **Game Review** after each loss, especially the first big mistake.
- Learn one opening for White and one reply for Black, not ten.

## Is Chess.com safe?

The official app on Google Play is safe and published by Chess.com. It has strong fair-play detection, so using an engine during games gets accounts closed. For children, turn off chat and messages in settings or use ChessKid, Chess.com's kids' site.
