## Beginner tips

- **Never leave builders idle**: always keep an upgrade running.
- **Upgrade resource storage and defences evenly** before rushing your Town Hall.
- **Join an active clan** early for troop donations and war rewards.
- **Learn one attack strategy** at a time, using the practice mode.
- **Spend gems on builders**, not on speeding up timers.

## Gems, Gold Pass and spending

Gems are Clash of Clans' premium currency. You earn some free from achievements and obstacles. The most valuable use is extra builder huts; speeding up timers gives the least value. The Gold Pass is good value for regular players. Turn on purchase authentication for children.

## Attacking: a simple plan for beginners

1. **Scout first.** Look for the enemy's Town Hall, air defences and Clan Castle.
2. **Clear the Clan Castle troops** with a few troops before your main push.
3. **Funnel your attack**: destroy outer buildings on two sides so troops walk into the core.
4. **Drop heroes behind the main force** and use their abilities when their health drops.
5. **Save spells** like Rage and Heal for the moment your army reaches the centre.

Practice mode teaches classic strategies for each Town Hall level without losing anything.

## Clan Wars and Clan War Leagues

In Clan Wars, two clans each get one or two attacks per member, and the clan with more stars wins bonus loot. Clan War Leagues run monthly, with seven war days and medals to spend in the League Shop. Being in an active war clan is one of the fastest ways to progress.
