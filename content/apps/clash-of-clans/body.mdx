## What is Clash of Clans?

Clash of Clans is a strategy game from Supercell, the Finnish studio behind Clash Royale and Brawl Stars. You build a village around your Town Hall, collect gold and elixir from mines and collectors, put up walls and defenses, and train an army. Then you raid other players' villages for their resources while they try to raid yours.

The other half of the game is your clan. Join one with friends or strangers, donate troops to each other, chat, and fight Clan Wars and the monthly Clan War League against other clans. That social side is why many players have stuck with it for years.

Clash of Clans has been on Google Play since September 2013 and has more than 500 million downloads. Progress is measured in Town Hall levels, each unlocking new buildings and troops. It's the best-known [strategy game](/category/strategy) on mobile.

## Key features

### Build and upgrade your village

Place buildings, walls and traps, and upgrade them to make your village stronger and produce more resources. Builders do the upgrades, and each upgrade takes time, from minutes early on to days at higher levels.

### Attack with troops, spells and heroes

Train troops like Barbarians, Archers, Giants and Dragons, add spells, and lead heroes such as the Barbarian King and Archer Queen. Planning where and in what order to deploy them is the heart of the game.

### Clans, Clan Wars and Clan War League

Clans let you donate troops to teammates and fight together. Clan Wars pit two clans against each other over two days, and Clan War League runs a week-long tournament each month with extra rewards.

### Builder Base

A second village with its own buildings, troops and battle rules. It's a change of pace from the main village, and it progresses separately.

### Gold Pass and events

The monthly Gold Pass gives extra rewards and upgrade boosts for completing challenges, and regular events bring special troops and temporary bonuses.

## Tips for new players

Keep all your builders busy, since idle builders are the biggest waste of time in Clash of Clans. Upgrade resource collectors and storage early, then defenses before walls. Don't rush to the next Town Hall level before upgrading the one you're on; a strong lower-level village is better than a weak higher one. And join an active clan early: troop donations make attacks far easier.

## Town Hall levels explained

Your Town Hall level decides what you can build and how strong your troops can get. Each new level unlocks more buildings, higher upgrade caps and new troops or heroes, and the game matches you mostly against villages at a similar level. That's why players talk about "maxing" a Town Hall: upgrading everything available at your level before moving up. Doing that keeps your defenses strong enough to protect your resources.
