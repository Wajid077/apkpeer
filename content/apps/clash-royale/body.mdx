## What is Clash Royale?

Clash Royale is a real-time strategy card game from Supercell. Two players face off in an arena, each with three towers. You build a deck of eight cards featuring troops, spells and buildings from the Clash universe, spend elixir to play them, and try to destroy more of your opponent's towers than they destroy of yours within three minutes.

The game launched globally in March 2016 and has passed 500 million downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [strategy games](/category/strategy) we list, alongside Supercell's <AppLink slug="clash-of-clans">Clash of Clans</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Real-time battles

Elixir fills up over time, and each card costs a set amount. The skill is in timing: defending efficiently, then counter-pushing when your opponent has spent their elixir.

### Deck building

There are more than 100 cards across Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Champion rarities. A good deck has a win condition (like Hog Rider or Giant), support troops, defensive options and spells.

### Evolutions

Evolved cards gain a powerful extra ability after you cycle them a set number of times. Unlocking and choosing Evolutions is a big part of the modern game.

### Trophy Road and Ranked

Climb arenas by winning trophies, then compete in Ranked for seasonal rewards.

### Clans and Clan Wars

Join a clan to request and donate cards, chat, and battle together in Clan Wars.

### Events and challenges

Special modes like draft, double elixir and mirror matches rotate regularly, with rewards for good runs.

## Tips for new players

- **Learn the elixir trade.** Use cheap cards to counter expensive ones and you'll get an elixir lead.
- **Don't play first at the start.** Let your opponent commit, then respond.
- **Know your win condition.** Every push should support the card that actually damages towers.
- **Keep a balanced deck.** Include something to stop air units and something to deal with swarms.
- **Use a deck you can upgrade.** Focusing upgrades on one deck beats spreading resources thin.
- **Watch replays.** Losses show you which cards your deck struggles against.

## Is Clash Royale APK safe?

The official Clash Royale from Google Play is safe. It's published by Supercell, rated Everyone 10+ for fantasy violence, has no ads, and offers in-app purchases from $0.29 to $239.99 per item.

- **Free gems scams.** Websites and videos offering free gems or cards are scams that steal Supercell ID logins.
- **Private servers.** "Unlimited" private-server versions aren't from Supercell, don't connect to your account, and can carry malware.
- **Account safety.** Link your progress to Supercell ID and never share your login. Supercell support never asks for your password.
- **Spending.** Use purchase authentication in Google Play and Supercell ID's parental controls for younger players.
