## What is 1.1.1.1 + WARP?

1.1.1.1 + WARP is Cloudflare's free privacy app. The 1.1.1.1 APK installs it on Android, where it can switch your phone's DNS to Cloudflare's fast 1.1.1.1 resolver, or turn on WARP to encrypt all your traffic between the phone and Cloudflare's network.

It's one of the [VPN and privacy apps](/category/vpn) we cover, with <AppLink slug="proton-vpn">Proton VPN</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="windscribe">Windscribe</AppLink>.

## DNS mode vs WARP mode

In **1.1.1.1 mode**, only your DNS lookups (the step where your phone finds a website's address) go to Cloudflare, encrypted. Your other traffic is unchanged. In **WARP mode**, all your traffic is encrypted between your phone and Cloudflare's network, similar to a VPN. WARP keeps your apparent location close to your real one, so it's for privacy on untrusted networks rather than for appearing in another country.

## Using 1.1.1.1 without the app

Android's Private DNS setting can use Cloudflare too. Open **Settings → Network → Private DNS** and enter **one.one.one.one**, or **family.cloudflare-dns.com** for malware and adult-content filtering. The app is easier and adds WARP.
