## What is Coin Master?

Coin Master is a casual game built around a slot machine. Each spin can give you coins, shields to protect your village, attacks on other players' villages, or raids to dig up their coins. You spend the coins to build and upgrade a series of themed Viking villages. Collecting cards, raising pets and playing events add more goals along the way.

## Key features

### Spin the slot machine

Each spin uses one spin from your supply. Three matching symbols give coins, attacks, raids, shields or more spins. A bet multiplier uses several spins at once for bigger rewards.

### Build villages

Spend coins on buildings in each village. Finishing a village unlocks the next, with a new theme and more expensive upgrades.

### Attacks and raids

Attack other players' villages to damage buildings and win coins, or raid their coin stash by digging in the right places. Your friends can do the same to you.

### Cards and pets

Collect cards to complete themed sets for rewards. Pets like the fox, tiger and rhino give bonuses during raids and attacks.

### Events

Regular events give bonus rewards for spinning the right symbols, completing tasks or playing with friends.

## Tips for new players

- **Don't bet high without a reason.** Use multipliers during events that reward the symbols you're spinning for.
- **Let spins refill.** Checking in a few times a day gives you free spins over time.
- **Add friends.** Friends send gifts and help with card trading.
- **Save coins before attacks.** Spend coins quickly on buildings, so raiders can't steal them.
- **Use pets wisely.** Feed them before events where their bonuses matter.
- **Set a spending limit.** Offers are frequent and tempting.
