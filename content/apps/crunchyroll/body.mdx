1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Crunchyroll**.
2. Check that the developer is **Crunchyroll, LLC**.
3. Tap **Install**, then sign in or create a Crunchyroll account.

The official listing is [Crunchyroll on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.crunchyroll.crunchyroid). Skip Crunchyroll APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Crunchyroll, LLC, they don't get updates, and they can carry malware or adware.

## Picking a Crunchyroll plan

Premium tiers differ in how many screens can stream at once and whether offline viewing is included. If you watch alone on your phone, the basic tier is usually enough; share with family or travel a lot, and the higher tiers make more sense. Check prices in the app for your country.

## Is Crunchyroll APK safe?

The official Crunchyroll app from Google Play is safe and published by Crunchyroll, LLC. Unofficial anime APKs that offer every show for free are usually pirated and packed with risky ads or malware. Crunchyroll's own app keeps your account and payments safe.
