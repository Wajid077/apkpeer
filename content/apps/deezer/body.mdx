## What is Deezer?

Deezer is a music streaming service based in France. The Deezer APK installs its Android app, with a large music catalogue, podcasts, Flow (an endless personalised mix), playlists, lyrics, and high-quality FLAC audio on paid plans.

It's one of the [music and audio apps](/category/music-audio) we cover, alongside <AppLink slug="spotify">Spotify</AppLink>.

## How to install Deezer APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Deezer**.
2. Check that the developer is **Deezer Music**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app.

The official listing is [Deezer on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=deezer.android.app). Avoid Deezer APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Deezer Music and can carry malware or adware.

## Deezer tips

- **Like and skip** songs in Flow to train it.
- **Download on Wi-Fi** with a paid plan.
- **Set audio quality** separately for mobile data and Wi-Fi.

## Is Deezer APK safe?

The official Deezer app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Deezer Music, rated Teen, contains ads on the free tier and offers in-app purchases. Unofficial Deezer copies that unlock paid features aren't from Deezer and can get your account suspended.

## Deezer plans explained

Deezer's paid plans, such as Premium, Duo, Family and Student, remove ads and add offline downloads, unlimited skips and lossless audio. A free tier with ads is available in some countries, with limits on skipping and choosing songs. Prices and availability vary by country, so check the plan screen in the app.

## Moving your playlists to Deezer

Deezer can import playlists from other services through its built-in transfer feature or third-party tools, so switching doesn't mean starting from scratch. Like a few dozen songs, and Flow quickly adapts.

## Deezer on other devices

Deezer runs on PC, Mac, iPhone, smart speakers, TVs, Wear OS and Android Auto, and your library syncs between them.

## Who should use Deezer

Deezer suits listeners who care about sound quality, love the Flow mix, or prefer a European alternative to Spotify.
