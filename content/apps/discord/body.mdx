## What is Discord?

Discord is a chat app built around communities. Each community is a server with its own text channels, voice channels and roles. You can drop into a voice channel to talk while gaming, stream your screen to friends, share memes in a text channel or run a large fan community with moderators and bots. It started in gaming but now hosts study groups, creators, open-source projects and friend groups of every kind.

Discord Inc. released the Android app in 2015. It has passed 500 million downloads on Google Play, and it's in our [communication apps](/category/communication) category.

## Key features

### Servers and channels

Join servers with an invite link or browse public ones. Inside, text channels are organised by topic, and roles control who can see and post where. Threads keep side conversations from flooding the main channel.

### Voice and video

Voice channels are always open: tap one to join and talk, and leave when you're done. You can also video call friends, and stream your screen or a game to a voice channel with Go Live.

### Direct messages and group DMs

Message friends privately or in small group DMs, with voice and video calls, file sharing, reactions and replies.

### Activities and apps

Play built-in games and watch things together inside voice channels. Servers can add bots for moderation, music, polls and games.

### Nitro

Nitro is Discord's optional subscription. It adds larger file uploads, HD streaming, custom emoji and stickers across servers, animated avatars and profile themes, and server boosts.

## How to install Discord APK on Android

Install [Discord from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.discord).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 7.0 or newer.
2. Open Google Play, search for Discord, and confirm the developer is Discord Inc.
3. Tap **Install**, open the app, and log in or create an account.
4. Allow microphone access the first time you join a voice channel.

Discord doesn't offer an official APK outside Google Play. Discord accounts are a common target for scammers, so avoid unofficial files that ask you to log in.

## Is Discord APK safe?

The official Discord app is safe to install. It's published by Discord Inc. on Google Play and rated Teen. In-app purchases are for Nitro, server boosts and Shop items. Play doesn't show an ads label, though Discord shows sponsored Quests.

Most Discord risks come from other users:

- **Fake Nitro gifts and "free" links.** A message offering free Nitro, or asking you to scan a QR code to log in, is almost always a scam to take over your account.
- **Strangers in DMs.** In **Settings → Content & Social**, limit who can message you and add you as a friend.
- **Two-factor authentication.** Turn it on in **Settings → Account**.
- **Teens.** Parents can use Family Center to see which servers and friends a teen has, and Discord applies stricter defaults for teen accounts.


## Discord settings worth changing

- **Who can DM you.** In **Settings → Content & Social**, turn off direct messages from server members you don't know, or switch it off per server.
- **Friend requests.** Choose who can send you friend requests: everyone, friends of friends, or server members.
- **Sensitive media filter.** Choose whether Discord blurs or blocks images that may be explicit in DMs and servers.
- **Notifications.** Long-press a busy server and set notifications to @mentions only, so your phone isn't buzzing all day.
- **Data saver.** Lower image quality and turn off GIF autoplay to save mobile data.
- **Two-factor authentication.** Add it in **Settings → Account**, and save the backup codes somewhere safe.

## Tips for new Discord users

Start with a few servers you actually care about, because a dozen busy ones become overwhelming fast. Read each server's rules channel before posting; many communities have specific channels for questions, media and off-topic chat. Use threads for side conversations so the main channel stays readable. In voice channels, push-to-talk or the noise suppression setting keeps background noise down. If someone you don't know messages you with a link, especially one promising free Nitro or asking you to log in, don't click it.
