## Setting up Disney+ for kids

Create a profile for each child, turn on **Junior mode** for young kids, and set a **Profile PIN** on adult profiles so children can't switch to them. Use the content rating setting to choose the highest rating each profile can see.

## Is Disney+ APK safe?

The official app from Google Play is safe and published by Disney. "Free Disney+" APKs and shared-account offers are scams or carry malware. Disney+ only needs your own login, and your plan decides what you can stream.

## Disney+ on TV, PC and iPhone

Disney+ runs on smart TVs, Android TV, Fire TV, game consoles, iPhone and iPad, and at disneyplus.com in a browser. You can cast from the Android app to a Chromecast. How many screens can stream at once depends on your plan.

## Who should use Disney+

It's best for families with children and fans of Marvel, Star Wars and Pixar. If you mostly watch dramas and reality TV, compare catalogues before subscribing.
