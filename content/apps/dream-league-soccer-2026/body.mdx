## What is Dream League Soccer 2026?

Dream League Soccer 2026, usually shortened to DLS 26, is a free football game from First Touch Games, a UK studio that has been making the series for years. You start with a small club and a squad of modest players, then sign better ones, upgrade your stadium and climb through the divisions toward the top league.

The appeal is the mix of club management and quick, arcade-style matches. You play the matches yourself with on-screen controls, and between games you handle transfers, training and your squad. It's a lighter, faster alternative to the big licensed football sims, and it runs on modest phones.

First Touch doesn't release a separate app each year. The same game on Google Play, first released in January 2020, is updated with the new season's name, players and features. So the DLS 26 APK is the latest version of the Dream League Soccer app you may already have. It's one of the most popular [sports games](/category/sports) on Android.

## Key features

### Build your dream club

Start with a basic squad and sign real players as your club grows. You can customize your kit and logo, and upgrade your stadium to earn more and host bigger crowds. Transfers are where most of your coins go, so spend them on positions that need it most.

### Fast, arcade-style matches

Matches use a virtual joystick and on-screen buttons for passing, shooting and tackling. Games are short enough to finish in a few minutes. You can change camera angles and difficulty in the settings.

### Division climb and cups

Your main career runs through a set of divisions, with promotion and relegation each season. Cup competitions run alongside, giving you extra matches and rewards.

### Live Events and online play

Live Events add limited-time challenges with their own rewards, and online multiplayer lets you play other people's clubs. These need an internet connection; most career matches don't.

### Coaching and player development

Train your players to improve their attributes, and choose formations and tactics before kick-off. A well-trained squad of lower-rated players can beat a lazy team of stars.

## Tips for your first season

Don't spend your starting coins on one expensive star. A couple of solid signings in weak positions, usually defence and central midfield, will win you more matches. Train the players you'll actually keep, and pick a formation that suits your best players rather than copying a famous team. If matches feel too easy or too hard, change the difficulty in the settings: the rewards scale with it.
