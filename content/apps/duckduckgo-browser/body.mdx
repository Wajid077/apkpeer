## What is DuckDuckGo?

DuckDuckGo is a privacy company known for its private search engine. The DuckDuckGo APK installs its Android browser, which blocks trackers on websites, upgrades connections to HTTPS, searches without building a profile of you, and clears your tabs and data with one tap of the Fire Button.

It's one of the [browsers](/category/browsers) we cover, with <AppLink slug="firefox">Firefox</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="brave-browser">Brave</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Tracker blocking

Stops hidden third-party trackers on the sites you visit.

### Fire Button

Clears tabs, history and cookies in one tap.

### Email Protection

A free @duck.com address that strips trackers from emails before forwarding them.

### Duck.ai

Optional anonymous AI chat.

### Subscription

An optional paid plan adds a VPN and identity-protection services where available.

## How to install DuckDuckGo APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **DuckDuckGo**.
2. Check that the developer is **DuckDuckGo**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app.

The official listing is [DuckDuckGo on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.duckduckgo.mobile.android). Avoid DuckDuckGo APK files from other download sites: they aren't from DuckDuckGo and can carry malware or adware.

## Tips for using DuckDuckGo

- **Make it your default browser** so links open privately.
- **Set the Fire Button** to clear data automatically when you close the app.
- **Sign up for Email Protection** to get a tracker-free email alias.
- **Hide Duck.ai** in settings if you don't use AI chat.

## DuckDuckGo App Tracking Protection

Beyond the browser, DuckDuckGo for Android can block trackers inside other apps. App Tracking Protection runs as a local VPN on your phone (your traffic doesn't go to DuckDuckGo's servers) and stops many third-party trackers that apps try to contact. It shows which companies each app tried to send data to. Turn it on in the app's settings, and switch it off for any app that stops working properly.

## DuckDuckGo search vs the browser

You can use DuckDuckGo search in any browser, but the app adds tracker blocking, the Fire Button, HTTPS upgrades and Email Protection. If you only want private search, set DuckDuckGo as the search engine in your current browser; if you want everything, install the app.

## Who should use DuckDuckGo

It suits people who want privacy with zero setup: install it, make it your default browser, and the protections are already on.
