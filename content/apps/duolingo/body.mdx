## What is Duolingo?

Duolingo is the world's most downloaded education app. It teaches languages through short lessons that feel like a game: you match words, translate sentences, speak into the microphone, listen and type. Points, streaks, leagues and a pushy green owl named Duo keep you coming back every day.

Duolingo, based in Pittsburgh, offers more than 40 languages, plus courses in maths, music and chess. The app has passed 500 million downloads on Google Play with a 4.7-star rating. It's one of the [education apps](/category/education) we list.

## Key features

### Bite-sized lessons

Each lesson takes a few minutes and mixes reading, writing, listening and speaking. A learning path guides you through units, with stories and practice sessions to review what you've learned.

### Streaks and leagues

Your streak counts how many days in a row you've practised. Leagues put you in a weekly leaderboard with other learners, where you earn XP to move up.

### Speaking and listening practice

Speaking exercises check your pronunciation with your microphone. Stories and audio lessons help you understand natural speech.

### Maths, music and chess

Beyond languages, Duolingo teaches basic to intermediate maths, reading music and playing simple songs on an on-screen piano, and chess puzzles and lessons.

### Super and Max

Super Duolingo removes ads, removes the limit on mistakes, and adds personalised practice. Duolingo Max, in some languages and countries, adds AI features such as explaining your mistakes and video calls with a Duolingo character.

## How to install Duolingo APK on Android

Install [Duolingo from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.duolingo).

1. Open Google Play, search for Duolingo, and check the developer is Duolingo.
2. Tap **Install** and open the app.
3. Choose a language and your reason for learning, take the placement test if you already know some, and set a daily goal.
4. Create a profile so your progress is saved and syncs with the web version.

Duolingo doesn't publish its own APK outside Google Play. Files from other sites aren't official, and modified versions can lose your progress.

## Tips for learning faster with Duolingo

- **Pick a realistic goal.** Ten minutes a day you'll actually do beats an hour you'll skip.
- **Do the speaking exercises.** Don't skip them; speaking out loud helps you remember.
- **Use stories and audio lessons.** They train your ear for real conversation better than single sentences.
- **Review weak words.** The practice hub brings back words and grammar you got wrong.
- **Add something outside the app.** Watch shows, read simple books, or find a conversation partner in your new language.

## Is Duolingo APK safe?

The official Duolingo app is safe to install. It's published by Duolingo on Google Play, rated Everyone, contains ads on the free tier, and lists in-app purchases from $0.99 to $239.99 per item for subscriptions and gems.

- **Kids.** Duolingo is suitable for children, though it isn't designed for under-13s without a parent. Duolingo ABC is a separate app for young children learning to read.
- **Notifications.** Duolingo's reminders can feel relentless; adjust them in **Settings → Notifications**.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified Duolingo apps promising free Super features aren't from Duolingo. They can steal your login and get your account banned. Use the official free tier or a real subscription.


## Making Duolingo fit your routine

Set a reminder at a time you're usually free, such as on your commute or after dinner. Duolingo's widget for your home screen shows your streak and nudges you without opening the app. If you're short on time, a single lesson or a quick practice session keeps your streak going. On busier weeks, turn on a Streak Freeze in advance. If leagues stress you out, you can leave the leaderboards in settings and still keep your streak and progress. Learning with a friend helps too: follow each other and use Friend Quests to stay motivated together.
