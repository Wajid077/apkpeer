## What is Facebook Lite?

Facebook Lite is a smaller, lighter version of Facebook made by Meta for phones with limited storage, older hardware or slow internet. It does the core things you'd expect, such as scrolling your feed, posting, commenting, sharing photos, joining groups and following pages, but in a much smaller download that uses less data.

It was built for places where mobile data is expensive or networks are slow, and it works even on 2G connections. It also has messaging built in, so you don't need the separate Messenger app. On Google Play it's listed by Meta Platforms, Inc. and has more than a billion downloads.

You use the same Facebook account, friends and groups as on the regular app. It's one of the [social media apps](/category/social) we cover, and the best way to use Facebook on a budget phone.

## Key features

### Small and data-friendly

Facebook Lite is a fraction of the size of the full Facebook app and loads quickly on slow networks. It keeps images and visuals simpler to save data, which makes a real difference on a limited mobile plan.

### Messages built in

Chat with friends from inside Facebook Lite, without installing Messenger. Messages sync with Messenger and Facebook on other devices.

### Feed, groups and pages

Read and react to posts, comment, share, and join groups and follow pages, just like on the full app. Notifications work the same way too.

### Photos and posts

Post status updates and photos, tag friends, and share to your profile, groups or pages.

### Works on older phones

The app is designed to run on older and low-memory Android phones that struggle with the full Facebook app, and to use less battery.

## Tips for saving data and space

Turn off video autoplay in Facebook Lite's settings, so videos only play when you tap them. Clear the app's cache now and then under **Settings → Apps → Facebook Lite → Storage** if your phone is running out of space. Download photos only when you need them, and use Wi-Fi for uploading albums. If you also have the full Facebook app installed, removing it frees up a lot of storage, since Lite gives you the same account.

## How to install Facebook Lite APK on Android

Install [Facebook Lite from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.facebook.lite) and check that the developer is Meta Platforms, Inc.

1. Open Google Play and search for Facebook Lite, or use the link above.
2. Tap **Install**, then open the app and log in to your Facebook account.
3. If your phone has no Play Store, any Facebook Lite APK comes from a third-party mirror. Android will only install it over an existing Facebook Lite if it's signed by Meta.
4. If you downloaded a file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for Facebook Lite.
5. If you installed from a file, turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is Facebook Lite APK safe?

The official Facebook Lite app is safe to install. It's published by Meta Platforms, Inc. on Google Play and rated Teen. Play marks it as containing ads and in-app purchases, from $0.29 to $179.99 per item.

Privacy works the same as on regular Facebook. Meta collects data about how you use its apps to personalize your feed and ads, as its privacy policy explains, and you can review ad settings in **Accounts Center**. Turn on two-factor authentication there too, because Facebook accounts are a common target for hijacking.

Watch out for modified Facebook Lite APKs and "Facebook Lite Pro"-style apps from other sites. They aren't made by Meta and can steal your login. Also be careful with links in messages that ask you to log in again, a common phishing trick. APKPeer only links to the official app.
