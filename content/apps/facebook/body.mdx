## What is Facebook?

Facebook is the world's largest social network, run by Meta Platforms. The Android app lets you post updates and photos, follow friends and pages, join Groups about almost any interest, watch Reels and videos, find local events, and buy and sell secondhand items on Marketplace. In some countries it also includes Facebook Dating.

The app has passed 10 billion downloads on Google Play, making it one of the most installed Android apps ever. It's in our [social apps](/category/social) category, along with Meta's lighter version, <AppLink slug="facebook-lite">Facebook Lite</AppLink>.

## What's new in the latest Facebook update

Meta publishes different builds for different phones and processors, and Play picks the right one. Play doesn't list release notes for Facebook, and Meta updates the app roughly weekly, turning features on gradually from its servers.

If something looks different from what a friend sees, that's usually a staged rollout rather than a different version.

## Key features

### Feed and Reels

Your feed mixes posts from friends, Groups and pages with recommended content. The Feeds tab can show only friends, Groups or pages in time order. Reels are short vertical videos, and you can create your own with music, effects and text.

### Groups

Groups are often the main reason people still use Facebook: local neighbourhood groups, hobby groups, buy-and-sell groups, support groups and fan communities. Each group sets its own rules, and admins can approve posts and members.

### Marketplace

Marketplace lets you browse and list items for sale near you, from furniture to phones. You chat with buyers and sellers through Messenger and arrange pickup or shipping, depending on your country.

### Events and Pages

Find local events, see which friends are going, and follow Pages for businesses, public figures and organisations.

### Accounts Center

Accounts Center links your Facebook, Instagram and Threads accounts, and holds your password, security and ad settings in one place.

## How to install Facebook APK on Android

Facebook comes preinstalled on many Android phones. If it isn't on yours, install [Facebook from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.facebook.katana).

1. Open Google Play, search for Facebook, and check the developer is Meta Platforms, Inc.
2. Tap **Install**. Play gives you the build that matches your phone.
3. Open Facebook and log in, or create a new account.
4. Allow notifications if you want alerts for messages and Group activity.

Meta doesn't publish a Facebook APK for download outside Play. Files from other sites aren't official and are a common way to steal Facebook logins. If your phone is low on storage or your connection is slow, try <AppLink slug="facebook-lite">Facebook Lite</AppLink> instead.

## Is Facebook APK safe?

The official Facebook app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Meta Platforms, Inc., rated Teen, contains ads, and lists in-app purchases from $0.05 to $10,000.00 per item for things like Stars and Meta Verified.

The main risks are to your account and privacy:

- **Phishing.** Messages claiming your page will be deleted, or that you've won something, often lead to fake login pages. Always log in through the app itself.
- **Two-factor authentication.** Turn it on in **Accounts Center → Password and security**.
- **Privacy.** Run **Privacy Checkup** to choose who sees your posts, friend list and contact details, and review **Ad preferences**.
- **Marketplace scams.** Meet in public places, don't pay deposits for items you haven't seen, and be wary of buyers who want to pay with unusual methods.


## Facebook settings worth changing

- **Privacy Checkup.** Under **Settings & privacy → Privacy Center**, run Privacy Checkup to choose who sees your future posts, friends list, email and phone number.
- **Profile lock.** In some countries you can lock your profile so only friends see your photos and posts.
- **Login alerts.** Turn on alerts for unrecognised logins in **Accounts Center → Password and security**.
- **Ad preferences.** In **Accounts Center → Ad preferences**, review which advertisers have reached you and turn off ads based on partner data.
- **Notifications.** Facebook sends a lot of notifications. Trim them in **Settings → Notifications** so you only hear about messages, comments and Groups you care about.
- **Data use.** Turn on **Data saver** if you're on a limited mobile plan. It loads lower-quality images and stops videos playing automatically.

## Facebook storage and battery

The full Facebook app is large and keeps a big cache of photos and videos. If your phone is running out of space, clear the cache in **Settings → Apps → Facebook → Storage**, which doesn't log you out. If the app still feels heavy, <AppLink slug="facebook-lite">Facebook Lite</AppLink> gives you the same account in a much smaller package.

- **<AppLink slug="facebook-lite">Facebook Lite</AppLink>** uses far less data and storage, and works well on older phones.

For Groups, Marketplace and keeping in touch with family, Facebook is still hard to replace. Spend five minutes in Privacy Checkup after installing so you share only what you mean to.
