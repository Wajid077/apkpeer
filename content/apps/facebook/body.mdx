Facebook is Meta's social network. The Facebook APK installs it on Android, where you keep up with friends and family, join Groups about almost anything, watch Reels and videos, find local events, and buy and sell on Marketplace. In some countries it also includes Facebook Dating.

It's one of the [social apps](/category/social) we cover. If your phone is old or short on storage, Meta's lighter <AppLink slug="facebook-lite">Facebook Lite</AppLink> is the better fit.

## Key features

### Feed and Reels

Posts from friends, Groups and Pages, plus recommended videos. The Feeds tab can show only friends in time order.

### Groups

Local neighbourhood groups, hobby groups, buy-and-sell groups and support groups, each with their own rules and admins.

### Marketplace

Browse and list items for sale near you, then chat with buyers and sellers through Messenger.

### Events and Pages

Find local events, see which friends are going, and follow businesses and public figures.

### Accounts Center

Manage your password, security and ad settings for Facebook, Instagram and Threads in one place.

## How to install Facebook APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone.
2. Search for **Facebook** and check that the developer is **Meta Platforms, Inc.**.
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to finish.
4. Open the app and sign in, or create an account.

If the Play Store isn't on your phone, use the [official Facebook listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.facebook.katana) from a phone or computer that has it, or the developer's own website listed below.

## Is Facebook APK safe?

The official Facebook app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Meta Platforms, Inc., rated Teen, and contains ads.

- **Phishing.** Messages saying your page will be deleted or that you've won something often lead to fake login pages. Always log in through the app.
- **Two-factor authentication.** Turn it on in **Accounts Center → Password and security**.
- **Privacy.** Run **Privacy Checkup** to choose who sees your posts, friend list and contact details.
- **Marketplace.** Meet in public places and don't pay deposits for items you haven't seen.
- **Unofficial Facebook APKs** from download sites can steal your account. Stick to Google Play.

## Tips for using Facebook on Android

- **See friends first.** Open the **Feeds** tab and choose **Friends** to see posts in time order without suggestions.
- **Trim notifications** in **Settings → Notifications** so you only hear about messages, comments and Groups you care about.
- **Turn on data saver** to stop videos playing automatically on mobile data.
- **Lock your profile** where available, so only friends see your photos and posts.
- **Clear the cache** when the app feels heavy: **Settings → Apps → Facebook → Storage → Clear cache** doesn't log you out.
- **Check login activity** in Accounts Center and log out of devices you don't recognise.

## Final thoughts

The Facebook APK from Google Play is the safe way to use Facebook on Android, with Groups, Marketplace and Reels in one app. Run Privacy Checkup after installing, turn on two-factor login, and switch to Facebook Lite if your phone struggles.
