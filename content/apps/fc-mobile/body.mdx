## What is EA SPORTS FC Mobile?

EA SPORTS FC Mobile is EA's football game for phones. You build an Ultimate Team from thousands of real, licensed players from leagues around the world, then play quick matches against the computer and other players. The game is built for mobile, with short matches, simple controls and constant events tied to the real football calendar.

The game launched as FIFA Mobile in October 2016 and was renamed EA SPORTS FC Mobile in 2023. On Google Play, the current season is listed as EA SPORTS FC Soccer Mobile 27, and it has passed 500 million downloads. It's one of the [sports games](/category/sports) we list.

## Key features

### Ultimate Team

Collect players, build a squad with good chemistry, and upgrade players through training and rank-ups. Formations and tactics change how your team plays.

### Match modes

Play VS Attack, where you control only your attacking moments in quick asynchronous matches, Head to Head for full real-time matches against other players, and Manager Mode, where you set tactics and watch.

### Events and campaigns

Themed events follow real football: league seasons, cups, team of the week and special player promotions. Completing them earns players, packs and upgrades.

### Market

Buy and sell players with coins on the transfer market, a key way to improve your team without paying.

### Leagues

Join a league with other players to chat, compete in league tournaments and earn shared rewards.

## Tips for building a strong team

- **Focus on events.** Events give the best free rewards each season.
- **Use the market wisely.** Sell duplicates and buy players when prices dip after big promotions.
- **Upgrade key positions first.** Strikers, central midfielders and centre-backs make the biggest difference.
- **Learn skill moves.** Simple skill moves and well-timed passes beat sprinting everywhere.
- **Try Game Plans.** Switching to a more defensive mentality late in a match can protect a lead.
- **Join an active league.** League rewards add up over a season.


## How to install FC Mobile APK on Android

The easiest option is [FC Mobile on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ea.gp.fifamobile).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 7.0 or newer.

3. Tap **Install**, then open the game. The game downloads extra data on first launch, so use Wi-Fi.
4. Link the game to an account in its settings, if it offers one, so your progress is saved if you change phones.

Without Google Play, install the official game carefully: get it only from Google Play or the developer, allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks, then turn that permission off again under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**. ## Is FC Mobile APK safe?

The official EA SPORTS FC Mobile from Google Play is safe. It's published by ELECTRONIC ARTS, rated Everyone, contains ads, and offers in-app purchases from $0.39 to $99.99 per item.

- **Packs and spending.** Player packs have random contents. EA shows pack probabilities in the game. Set a budget and turn on purchase authentication in Google Play.
- **Coin sellers and account sharing.** Buying coins or accounts from third parties breaks EA's rules and leads to bans; sellers often steal accounts back.
