## What is Firefox?

Firefox is the open-source web browser from Mozilla, a non-profit. The Firefox APK installs it on Android, with Enhanced Tracking Protection on by default, private browsing, add-ons, a password manager and sync with Firefox on your computer.

It's one of the [browsers](/category/browsers) we cover, alongside <AppLink slug="google-chrome">Chrome</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="brave-browser">Brave</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Enhanced Tracking Protection

Blocks social trackers, cross-site cookies, cryptominers and fingerprinters.

### Add-ons

Install extensions such as ad blockers and dark-mode tools.

### Private browsing

Private tabs leave no history on your phone, and you can lock them with your fingerprint.

### Sync

Sign in with a Mozilla account to sync tabs, bookmarks and passwords.

## How to install Firefox APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Firefox**.
2. Check that the developer is **Mozilla**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app.

The official listing is [Firefox on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.mozilla.firefox). Avoid Firefox APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Mozilla and can carry malware or adware.

## Firefox tips for Android

- **Set Firefox as your default browser** in Android settings.
- **Install an ad blocker add-on** from Menu → Add-ons.
- **Use Strict tracking protection** for even more blocking.
- **Turn on HTTPS-Only mode** in Settings.

## Is Firefox APK safe?

The official Firefox app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Mozilla, rated Everyone, and has no ads or in-app purchases. Only install add-ons from Mozilla's own add-ons site.

## Firefox on PC, iPhone and other devices

Firefox runs on Windows, Mac, Linux and iPhone. Signing in with a Mozilla account syncs your bookmarks, passwords, history and open tabs between them. Firefox Focus, a separate Mozilla app, is a minimal private browser that deletes everything when you close it.

## Best add-ons to start with

- **uBlock Origin** to block ads and trackers.
- **Dark Reader** for dark mode on every site.
- **Bitwarden** if you use that password manager.

Only install add-ons from addons.mozilla.org, and keep the list short: every add-on can see the pages you visit.

## Who should use Firefox

Firefox is the pick for people who want privacy without giving up a full-featured browser, and for anyone who wants an ad blocker on Android without switching to Brave. It's also the main independent browser engine, which matters if you'd rather not have the whole web run on Chromium.

## Final thoughts

The Firefox APK from Google Play is the best choice if you want an independent, privacy-focused browser with add-ons on Android. Install an ad blocker and switch on HTTPS-Only mode on day one.
