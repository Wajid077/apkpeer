## What is Free Fire?

Free Fire is a battle royale shooter from Garena. Fifty players parachute onto an island, scavenge for weapons and gear, and fight while a safe zone shrinks around them. The last player or team standing wins, a "Booyah!" in Free Fire's language. Matches last about ten minutes, which is a big part of its appeal on phones.

The other big reason for its popularity is that it runs on almost anything. Free Fire needs only Android 5.0 or newer, and it was designed for budget phones with limited memory, which made it hugely popular in South America, Southeast Asia and other regions where those phones are common. It's been on Google Play since December 2017 and has passed a billion downloads.

Garena also makes Free Fire MAX, a separate app with better graphics that shares the same accounts and matches. Both are among the most-played [action games](/category/action) on Android.

## Key features

### Battle Royale

The classic mode: 50 players, one island, a shrinking zone. Play solo, in pairs or in squads of four. Choose where to land, loot weapons and armor, and use vehicles to stay inside the safe zone.

### Clash Squad

A faster 4v4 mode played over several rounds. You buy weapons between rounds with money earned in the previous one, so it plays more like a tactical shooter than a survival game.

### Characters and skills

Each character has a special skill, such as healing faster or running quicker, and you can equip a combination of skills before a match. Characters are unlocked with gold, diamonds or events.

### Short matches for any phone

Matches are short, the download is light compared with most shooters, and the game runs well on phones with little memory. Graphics settings can be lowered further for older devices.

### Events and collaborations

Garena runs constant events every few weeks, each with its own login rewards and missions, from seasonal updates to crossovers like the current NARUTO SHIPPUDEN collaboration, with limited-time modes, maps and rewards.

## Is Free Fire APK safe?

The official Free Fire APK is safe. It's published by Garena on Google Play and rated Teen for violence and blood. Play marks it as having in-app purchases (from $0.09 to $99.99 per item), some of which include random items.

Account safety is the big issue in Free Fire. "Free diamonds" generators and top-up sites that ask for your login are scams that steal Garena and Google accounts. Only top up through the game itself or Garena's official top-up site.

Free Fire is also one of the most targeted games for cheat tools, such as aimbots, auto-headshot scripts and modified APKs. They aren't made by Garena, they often carry malware, and Garena's anti-cheat system bans accounts that use them, often permanently. APKPeer only links to the official game.
