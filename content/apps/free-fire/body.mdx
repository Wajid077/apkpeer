## Tips for winning more matches

- **Land away from the hot zones** at first to loot safely.
- **Always pick up a gloo wall** item; walls save lives in the open.
- **Move with the zone**, not against it, and keep vehicles for late rotations.
- **Learn one character skill combo** that suits your play style.
- **Practise in Clash Squad** to improve aim quickly.
- **Lower the graphics** for a steadier frame rate on older phones.

## Diamonds, passes and what's worth buying

Diamonds are Free Fire's premium currency. They buy the Booyah Pass, characters, pets, weapon skins and bundles. None of it is needed to win: characters and many items can also be unlocked with gold earned by playing. If you do spend, the season pass usually gives the most items for the price. Treat lucky-draw style events with care, because rewards are random and costs climb quickly.
