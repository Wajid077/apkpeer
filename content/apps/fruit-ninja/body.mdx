## What is Fruit Ninja?

Fruit Ninja is Halfbrick's swipe-to-slice arcade game, and the Fruit Ninja APK is its Android installer. Fruit flies up the screen, you slice it with your finger, and you avoid the bombs. Simple, fast and satisfying, it's been one of Android's most-played games since 2011, with blades, dojos and seasonal events added over the years.

## High-score tips

- **Wait for groups:** let several fruits rise, then slice them together for combos.
- **Watch the bombs:** in Classic and Arcade, a bomb ends the round or costs points.
- **Arcade bananas:** Frenzy, Freeze and Double Score bananas are worth chasing.
- **Use long swipes** across the screen rather than many small ones.
