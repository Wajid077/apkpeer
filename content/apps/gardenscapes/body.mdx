## Gardenscapes level tips

- **Look at the goal first** and focus on it rather than random matches.
- **Combine power-ups:** a rocket with a bomb clears big areas.
- **Work from the bottom:** pieces falling from the top can create free matches.
- **Save boosters** for hard and super-hard levels.
