## What is Genshin Impact?

Genshin Impact is an open-world action RPG from HoYoverse. You play as the Traveler, searching across the fantasy world of Teyvat for a lost sibling. You explore huge regions inspired by real-world cultures, climb almost anything, glide, swim, solve puzzles, cook and fight using a party of four characters, each wielding one of seven elements. Combining elements, like Pyro and Hydro, triggers powerful reactions.

Genshin launched in September 2020 and has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play. It's published by COGNOSPHERE PTE. LTD., HoYoverse's publishing company, and it's one of the [role-playing games](/category/role-playing) we list.

## Key features

### Open world

Explore regions such as Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan and newer areas. Each has its own story, music, puzzles and secrets.

### Elemental combat

Switch between four party members during a fight. Each uses an element, and combining them, like freezing enemies with Cryo and Hydro or burning them with Pyro and Dendro, is key to winning.

### Characters and wishes

Unlock characters through the story and through wishes, Genshin's gacha banners. Level up characters, weapons and artifacts to make your team stronger.

### Co-op

Play with up to three friends in open-world exploration, domains and bosses from Adventure Rank 16.

### Cross-save

Your HoYoverse account syncs progress between Android, iPhone, PC and PlayStation.

## Tips for new players

- **Follow the Archon Quests.** They unlock new regions, features and free characters.
- **Use your Resin wisely.** Spend it every day on talent books, weapon materials or artifacts for the characters you use most.
- **Don't spread resources thin.** Build one strong team of four before levelling others.
- **Open every chest and Oculus.** Exploration gives Primogems, the currency for wishes.
- **Save wishes for characters you want.** Thanks to pity, saving for a limited banner is more reliable than wishing on everything.
- **Lower graphics for long sessions.** Lower settings reduce heat and battery drain on phones.
