## What is Geometry Dash Lite?

Geometry Dash Lite is the free version of Geometry Dash, a rhythm-based platformer by RobTop Games, a one-person studio in Sweden. You control a small square that moves forward on its own. Tap to jump over spikes and gaps, hold to fly a ship, and keep going until you hit something, at which point you start the level again from the beginning.

What makes it addictive is the music. Every level is built around a soundtrack, and the jumps land on the beat, so once you learn a level it feels like playing a song. It's also famously hard. Expect to die dozens of times on a level before you finish it.

The Lite version has been on Google Play since September 2013, has more than 500 million downloads, and includes a selection of the official levels with ads. It's one of the best-known [arcade games](/category/arcade) on Android, and the full game, with everything unlocked, is a paid app.

## Key features

### One-touch controls

Almost everything is done with a single tap or hold. The challenge comes from timing, not complicated controls, which makes it easy to start and hard to master.

### Levels built around music

Each level has its own soundtrack, and obstacles are placed in time with it. Many players turn the sound up and play by ear as much as by sight.

### Practice mode

Practice mode lets you place checkpoints as you go, so you can learn a hard section without restarting from the beginning each time. Beat the level in practice, then try it for real.

### Different game modes inside a level

Portals switch your square between forms, such as a ship that flies, a ball that flips gravity, or a wave that zigzags. Each changes how your taps work, which keeps levels surprising.

### Unlockable icons and colors

Completing levels and achievements unlocks new icons and colors for your character. It's purely cosmetic, but it's a nice reward for clearing a hard level.

## Tips for beating hard levels

Use practice mode to learn a level in pieces before trying it in normal mode. Listen to the music: most jumps line up with the beat, and playing by ear is often easier than reacting to what you see. Take breaks when you keep failing at the same spot, since frustration ruins timing. And the new death markers in this update show exactly where you keep crashing, which tells you which section to practise.

## Is Geometry Dash Lite APK safe?

The official Geometry Dash Lite APK is safe. It's published by RobTop Games on Google Play, where it's rated Everyone 10+ for fantasy violence and mild blood. Play marks it as containing ads and doesn't list in-app purchases.

Because the full game is paid, you'll find plenty of downloads claiming to be "Geometry Dash full version free." They're unauthorized copies of a paid game, often bundled with adware, and they can't use RobTop's online level servers properly. If you enjoy the game, the full version is inexpensive and supports a solo developer. APKPeer only links to the official apps.
