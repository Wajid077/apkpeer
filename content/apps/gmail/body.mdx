## What is Gmail?

Gmail is Google's email service, and the Gmail APK is its Android app. It handles Gmail addresses and other accounts such as Outlook, Yahoo and work email, filters spam and phishing, sorts mail into tabs like Primary and Promotions, and lets you search years of email in seconds. It also includes Google Chat and Meet for teams that use Google Workspace.

Gmail is one of the [communication apps](/category/communication) we cover, next to <AppLink slug="google-meet">Google Meet</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="google-drive">Google Drive</AppLink>, which shares its storage.

## Key features

### Several accounts in one app

Add personal, work and other providers' accounts, and switch with a swipe on your profile picture.

### Smart inbox tabs

Gmail sorts mail into Primary, Social, Promotions and Updates. You can turn tabs off in settings if you prefer one inbox.

### Search and filters

Search by sender, date, attachment or words inside the message. Labels and filters (set up on the web) keep the inbox tidy automatically.

### Undo send and scheduled send

Change your mind for a few seconds after sending, or schedule an email for later from the send menu.

### Gemini help

Depending on your account and region, Gmail can summarise long threads and help draft replies with Google's AI.

## How to install Gmail APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Gmail**.
2. Check that the developer is **Google LLC**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app and sign in. If Gmail is already on your phone but missing from the home screen, Play shows **Enable** or **Open**: it was disabled, not removed.

The official listing is [Gmail on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.gm). Skip Gmail APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Google LLC, they don't update, and they can carry malware or adware.

| Problem | What to try |

| Notifications are late | Remove battery restrictions for Gmail in Android settings |
| Can't sign in | Use Google's account recovery at accounts.google.com |
| App keeps crashing | Update Gmail and Android System WebView, then clear Gmail's cache |

## Gmail tips that save time

- **Swipe actions:** set left and right swipes to archive, delete or snooze in **Settings → General settings → Swipe actions**.
- **Unsubscribe:** open a newsletter and tap Unsubscribe at the top instead of hunting for the link.
- **Free storage:** search `larger:10M` to find big attachments to delete.
- **Confidential mode:** send messages that expire and can't be forwarded.

## Is Gmail safe?

Yes. The official Gmail app is published by Google LLC, updates through Google Play and warns you about suspicious messages and dangerous links. The biggest risk is phishing, not the app: never type your password into a page reached from an email link. Turn on 2-Step Verification and check your security status at myaccount.google.com.

## Gmail vs alternatives

- **Outlook** suits people who use Microsoft 365 at work.
- **Proton Mail** focuses on end-to-end encrypted email.
- **<AppLink slug="telegram">Telegram</AppLink>** or **<AppLink slug="whatsapp">WhatsApp</AppLink>** are better for quick chats than email threads.

## Gmail on PC, iPhone and other devices

Open mail.google.com in any browser on Windows, Mac or Linux, or install Gmail from the App Store on iPhone and iPad. Gmail also works on Wear OS watches for quick replies. Everything stays in sync because your mail lives in your Google account, not on the phone.

## Who should use the Gmail app

Gmail suits almost every Android user, because it's already linked to the Google account on your phone. If you have several addresses from different providers, it's also a tidy way to keep them all in one app.

## Final thoughts

The Gmail APK from Google Play is the easiest email app for most Android users, and it handles other providers too. Keep it updated, keep an eye on your shared storage, and turn on 2-Step Verification to protect the account that unlocks everything else.
