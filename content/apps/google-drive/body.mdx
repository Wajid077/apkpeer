## What is Google Drive?

Google Drive is Google's cloud storage, and the Google Drive APK is its Android app. It stores your files online so you can open them on any phone or computer, share them with a link, and scan paper documents to PDF with the camera. Google Docs, Sheets and Slides files live in Drive too.

It's one of the [productivity apps](/category/productivity) on APKPeer. Drive storage is shared with <AppLink slug="gmail">Gmail</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="google-photos">Google Photos</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Upload and organise

Upload files from your phone, create folders, star important items and use colours to find them quickly.

### Share with control

Share a file or folder with specific people, or anyone with the link, and choose whether they can view, comment or edit.

### Scan documents

Tap **+ → Scan** to photograph receipts or forms; Drive straightens and saves them as PDFs, and makes the text searchable.

### Offline access

Mark files as available offline to open them on a plane or with no signal.

### Search inside files

Drive searches file names and the text inside documents and PDFs, so you can find a contract by a phrase you remember.

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Google Drive**.
2. Check that the developer is **Google LLC**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app and sign in.

The official listing is [Google Drive on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.docs). Skip Google Drive APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Google LLC, they don't update, and they can carry malware or adware.

## How to free up Google Drive storage

Open **Storage** in the Drive menu to sort files by size. Delete what you don't need, then empty **Trash**, because trashed files still count until they're removed. Big email attachments in Gmail and original-quality photos in Google Photos count against the same 15 GB.

## Is Google Drive safe?

The official Google Drive app is safe and published by Google LLC. Files are encrypted in transit and on Google's servers, and you control sharing for every item. Protect it like any account: use a strong password, turn on 2-Step Verification, and check **Shared with me** for unexpected files, which is a common spam trick.

## Google Drive vs alternatives

- **Dropbox** has strong file sync for desktop folders.
- **OneDrive** comes with Microsoft 365 plans.
- **<AppLink slug="google-photos">Google Photos</AppLink>** is the better place for your camera roll.

## Google Drive on PC and iPhone

On a computer, open drive.google.com, or install Google Drive for desktop to sync folders with Windows or Mac. On iPhone and iPad, install Drive from the App Store. Files you upload from Android appear everywhere you sign in.

## Who should use Google Drive

Drive is ideal for students and workers who share documents, families who want a safe copy of important papers, and anyone who wants a backup of files that aren't photos.

The Google Drive APK from Google Play is the easiest way to keep files safe and reachable on Android. Use the 15 GB free wisely, share with care, and mark important documents offline before you travel.
