## What is Google Gemini?

Gemini is Google's AI assistant. The Gemini APK is the official Android app: type or talk to it to answer questions, write and summarise, plan trips, explain photos, create images and get help with Gmail, Calendar, Maps and YouTube when you allow it. On most new Android phones it replaces Google Assistant.

It's one of the AI assistants in our [productivity apps](/category/productivity) category, alongside <AppLink slug="chatgpt">ChatGPT</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="claude">Claude</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Chat and writing

Ask anything, draft emails and messages, rewrite text and summarise long documents.

### Gemini Live

Talk naturally, interrupt, and share your camera or screen on supported phones.

### Connected Google apps

With your permission, Gemini reads Gmail, Drive, Calendar and Maps to give personal answers.

### Images

Create and edit images; generated images carry an invisible SynthID watermark.

### Phone assistant

Open Gemini from the power button or "Hey Google" to set alarms, send messages or ask about your screen.

## How to install Gemini APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone.
2. Search for **Gemini** and check that the developer is **Google LLC**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open it.
4. Sign in or link an account so your progress is saved if you change phones.

The official listing is [Gemini on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.bard). Avoid Gemini APK files on other download sites: they aren't from Google LLC and can carry adware or malware.

On many phones Gemini is preinstalled and only needs updating.

## Gemini privacy settings

- **Gemini Apps Activity:** turn off or auto-delete in settings.
- **Connected apps:** switch off any Google app you don't want Gemini to read.
- **Lock screen:** decide whether Gemini answers when your phone is locked.

## How to turn off or remove Gemini

1. Open Gemini, tap your profile picture, then **Settings → Digital assistants from Google** to switch back to Google Assistant where available.
2. Or open **Settings → Apps → Gemini** and tap **Disable** or **Uninstall updates**.

## Is Gemini APK safe?

The official Gemini app is safe. It's published by Google LLC, rated Teen, and has no ads. Check important facts, avoid sharing sensitive personal data in chats, and ignore lookalike "Gemini AI" apps from other developers that charge subscriptions.

## Free vs paid Gemini

The free Gemini app covers chat, writing help, Gemini Live voice conversations, image questions and connected Google apps, with usage limits. Google's paid AI plans, billed through your Google account, add higher limits, Google's most capable models, longer context and extra storage. Try the free version first, and only upgrade if you regularly hit limits.

## Gemini Live tips

- **Interrupt freely.** Gemini Live is built for back-and-forth conversation.
- **Share your camera** to ask about what's in front of you, such as a recipe, a sign or a broken part.
- **Share your screen** for help with an app or a form.
- **Use it hands-free** while cooking or walking.

## Gemini on PC and iPhone

Use gemini.google.com in any browser, or install the Gemini app from the App Store on iPhone. Your chats sync across devices when you're signed in.
