## What is Google Gemini?

Gemini is Google's AI assistant, and on most new Android phones it has replaced the old Google Assistant. You can type or talk to it, ask it to write or summarise something, plan a trip, explain a photo, generate an image or help with homework. Because it's made by Google, it can also work with your Gmail, Calendar, Maps, YouTube and other Google apps when you allow it.

The app launched on Google Play in February 2024, replacing the earlier Bard chatbot, and has passed 1 billion downloads. It's one of the [productivity apps](/category/productivity) we list, alongside other AI assistants such as <AppLink slug="chatgpt">ChatGPT</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Chat, write and summarise

Ask questions in plain language and follow up naturally. Gemini can draft emails and messages, rewrite text in a different tone, summarise long articles or PDFs you upload, and explain topics step by step.

### Gemini Live

Live is a spoken conversation rather than a one-off voice command. You can interrupt Gemini mid-answer, change your mind, and, on supported phones, share your camera or screen so it can talk about what you're seeing, such as a menu in another language or a settings page you're stuck on.

### Connected Google apps

With your permission, Gemini can pull information from Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Maps and YouTube. That's what lets it answer "When is my dentist appointment?" or "Find the flight confirmation email" instead of giving a generic reply.

### Images and creativity

Gemini can generate and edit images from a description. The image features use Google's Nano Banana model, and generated images carry an invisible SynthID watermark that identifies them as AI-made.

### Phone assistant

Set Gemini as your default assistant and it opens from the power button or "Hey Google". It can set alarms, send messages, control smart home devices and answer questions about whatever is on your screen.

## How to install Gemini APK on Android

Most people should install [Gemini from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.bard). On many phones it's already preinstalled and only needs updating.

1. Check that your phone runs Android 9 or newer, which is the minimum Play lists.
2. Open Google Play, search for Gemini, and make sure the developer is Google LLC.
3. Tap **Install**, open the app and sign in with your Google account.
4. When asked, choose whether Gemini should become your default assistant. You can change this later in the app's settings.

If you can't use Google Play, use the web version at gemini.google.com in Chrome instead. APKPeer doesn't host a Gemini APK, and Gemini depends on Google services, so it won't work properly on phones without them.

## Is Gemini APK safe?

The official Gemini app is safe to install. It's published by Google LLC on Google Play, rated Teen, and has no ads. The bigger questions are about privacy and accuracy.

- **Privacy.** Gemini Apps Activity is saved to your Google account by default and may be reviewed to improve Google's products. Turn it off or set auto-delete under **Settings → Gemini Apps Activity**, and avoid sharing passwords, health details or other sensitive information in chats.
- **Accuracy.** Like every AI assistant, Gemini can state wrong things confidently. Check important facts, especially medical, legal and financial ones, against a reliable source.
- **Fake apps.** Search results and ads sometimes show lookalike "Gemini AI" apps from other developers that charge subscriptions. The real app's package name is com.google.android.apps.bard and the developer is Google LLC.


## Gemini settings worth changing

A few minutes in settings makes Gemini more useful and more private.

- **Default assistant.** In the Gemini app, tap your profile picture, then **Settings → Digital assistants from Google**. Choose Gemini, or switch back to Google Assistant on phones where that's still offered.
- **Gemini Apps Activity.** Under **Settings → Gemini Apps Activity**, turn activity off, or set auto-delete to 3, 18 or 36 months. With activity off, Google still keeps chats for a short period to run the service and handle safety.
- **Connected apps.** Under **Settings → Apps**, turn off any Google app you don't want Gemini to read, such as Gmail or Drive.
- **Lock screen.** Decide whether Gemini can answer on the lock screen. Turning it off stops anyone with your phone from asking about your calendar or messages.

## Free vs paid Gemini

The free version covers everyday chat, writing help, image questions and Gemini Live. Google's paid AI plans, billed through your Google account, add higher limits, the most capable models, longer documents and extra storage. If you mainly ask quick questions and draft messages, the free tier is usually enough. Try it for a couple of weeks before paying, and check that the features you want are available in your country and language.

## Gemini vs alternatives

- **<AppLink slug="chatgpt">ChatGPT</AppLink>** is the best-known alternative, with strong writing tools and a large ecosystem of custom GPTs. It doesn't connect to your Google apps the way Gemini does.
- **<AppLink slug="claude">Claude</AppLink>** from Anthropic is popular for long documents, careful writing and coding help.
- **<AppLink slug="perplexity">Perplexity</AppLink>** focuses on search: every answer comes with links to its sources, which makes fact-checking easier.

If you already live in Google's apps, Gemini is the most convenient choice, because it can see your calendar, mail and files when you allow it. If you'd rather keep your AI assistant separate from your Google account, one of the alternatives above may suit you better.
