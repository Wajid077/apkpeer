## What is Google Lens?

Google Lens is Google's visual search tool. Instead of typing a search, you point your camera at something, or pick a photo or screenshot, and Lens tells you what it is or what it says. It can identify plants, animals and landmarks, find products to buy, translate signs and menus, copy text from paper into your phone, and help with homework problems.

The standalone Google Lens app, published by Google LLC, has been on Google Play since June 2018 and has more than a billion downloads. Lens is also built into the Google app, Google Photos, the camera on many Android phones, and Circle to Search, so many people use it without ever installing this app.

It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases, and it's one of the most useful [tools](/category/tools) on Android, whether you're traveling, shopping or studying.

## Key features

### Search what you see

Point your camera at an object, a plant, an animal, a building or a product, and Lens shows matching results from Google Search, with similar images and shopping links where relevant.

### Translate with your camera

Choose **Translate** and point Lens at text in another language. The translation appears on top of the original in your camera view, which makes menus, signs and labels easy to understand abroad.

### Copy text from the real world

Choose **Text** to select words from a photo or your camera view, then copy them, search them, or send them to your computer if you're signed in to Chrome with the same account. It's handy for notes, Wi-Fi passwords and phone numbers.

### Homework help

Point Lens at a math problem or a question, and it shows explanations, steps and related resources from the web. It's best used to understand how to solve something, not just to get the answer.

### Shopping

See a pair of shoes or a lamp you like? Lens finds similar products and where to buy them, with prices from online stores.

## Tips for better results

Hold your phone steady and fill the frame with the object or text you're interested in. For text, good light makes a big difference. When Lens finds several things in a photo, tap the one you mean, or drag the corners of the selection box to narrow it down.

## How to install Google Lens APK on Android

Install [Google Lens from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.ar.lens) and check that the developer is Google LLC.

1. Check your phone runs Android 6.0 or newer.
2. Open Google Play and search for Google Lens, or use the link above, then tap **Install**.
3. Open Lens and allow camera access. Allow photo access too if you want to search your existing photos.
4. If your phone has no Play Store, any Lens APK comes from a third-party mirror, and Lens also relies on Google services to work. Android will only install it over an existing Lens if it's signed by Google.
5. If you downloaded a file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for Google Lens.
6. If you installed from a file, turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is Google Lens APK safe?

The official Google Lens app is safe. It's published by Google LLC on Google Play, rated Everyone, with no ads or in-app purchases. It uses your camera and photos only when you open it to search.

What you search with Lens is sent to Google to find results, and it's saved to your Google account activity if Web & App Activity is turned on. You can review and delete that activity at myactivity.google.com, or turn off saving.

There's no reason to download a modified Lens. It's already completely free, and a modified app with camera access could be a privacy risk. APKPeer only links to the official app.
