Google Maps is Google's map and navigation app. The Google Maps APK installs it on Android, with turn-by-turn directions for driving, walking, cycling and public transport, live traffic, reviews and opening hours for local businesses, Street View, and location sharing.

## How to install Google Maps APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Google Maps**.
2. Check that the developer is **Google LLC**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app. Google Maps comes preinstalled on most Android phones.

The official listing is [Google Maps on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.maps). Avoid Google Maps APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Google LLC and can carry malware or adware.

## Google Maps tips

- **Download offline maps** before travelling abroad.
- **Save home and work** for one-tap directions.
- **Turn on Timeline settings** to control what location history is kept.
- **Use Live View** walking directions on supported phones.

## Is Google Maps APK safe?

The official Google Maps app is safe. It's published by Google LLC and rated Everyone. Review location sharing and Timeline settings so you only keep and share what you want.

## Google Maps on Android Auto and other devices

Google Maps works on Android Auto in compatible cars, on Wear OS watches for turn-by-turn directions on your wrist, and on iPhone. Places you save and your home and work addresses sync through your Google account.

## Navigation tips

- **Avoid tolls or highways** in route options.
- **Add stops** to plan multi-stop trips.
- **Check the speedometer and speed limits** in navigation settings where available.
- **Report incidents** like crashes or speed traps to help other drivers.

## Privacy and location history

Timeline (your location history) can be turned off or set to delete automatically. Location sharing should only be used with people you trust, and you can stop it anytime. Review these in **Settings → Location and privacy**.

## Who should use Google Maps

It's the most complete free navigation app for most countries, with transit, walking and business information in one place.
