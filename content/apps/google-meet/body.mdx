## What is Google Meet?

Google Meet is Google's video calling and meeting app, and the Google Meet APK is its Android installer. It covers quick video calls with family (it absorbed Google Duo) and scheduled meetings for school and work, with screen sharing, captions, backgrounds and chat. Anyone with a Google account can start or join a meeting.

It's one of the [communication apps](/category/communication) we cover, with <AppLink slug="zoom">Zoom</AppLink>, <AppLink slug="whatsapp">WhatsApp</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="gmail">Gmail</AppLink>, which links to Meet calendars and invites.

## Key features

### Video calls and meetings

Call contacts directly or join scheduled meetings with a link or code.

### Screen sharing

Share your phone screen to present slides or show an app.

### Captions and translation

Turn on live captions; paid plans add translated captions and real-time speech translation.

### Effects and backgrounds

Blur or replace your background, and use filters in personal calls.

### Notes and recording on paid plans

Paid plans add recording and AI note-taking, so you can focus on the conversation.

## How to install Google Meet APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Google Meet**.
2. Check that the developer is **Google LLC**.
3. Tap **Install**, then sign in with your Google account.

The official listing is [Google Meet on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.tachyon). Skip Google Meet APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Google LLC, they don't get updates, and they can carry malware or adware.

## Google Meet not working? Fixes

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| Others can't hear you | Check microphone permission and that you're not muted |
| Video freezes | Turn off your camera briefly or switch to Wi-Fi |
| Can't join a meeting | Check you're signed in with the invited account |
| Meeting ended at 60 minutes | Free group meeting limit; start a new meeting |

## Tips for better calls

- Use **headphones** to avoid echo.
- Turn on **noise cancellation** in settings.
- Face a window or lamp so your face is lit from the front.
- Mute when you're not talking in big meetings.

## Is Google Meet safe?

Yes. The official app is published by Google LLC, and calls are encrypted in transit. Meeting hosts can admit or remove guests, lock the meeting and control screen sharing. Only join meetings from links you expect, because fake meeting invites are used for phishing.

## Google Meet on PC and iPhone

On a computer, open meet.google.com in Chrome or another browser; no install is needed. On iPhone and iPad, install Google Meet from the App Store. You can also join from a Google Calendar invite on any device.

## Who should use Google Meet

It's ideal for students and teams on Google Workspace, and for families who want free video calls without adding another account.

## Final thoughts

The Google Meet APK from Google Play is the simplest free way to make video calls and join meetings on Android. Use headphones, turn on captions when it helps, and upgrade only if you need meetings longer than an hour or recording.
