## What is Google Photos?

Google Photos is Google's photo and video app. The Google Photos APK installs it on Android, where it backs up your photos and videos to your Google account, organises them by people, places and things, lets you search ("dogs at the beach"), and includes editing tools, memories, albums and sharing.

It's one of the [photo apps](/category/photo-editors) we cover, alongside <AppLink slug="snapseed">Snapseed</AppLink>.

## Key features

Back up photos and videos automatically over Wi-Fi.

### Search

Find photos by people, places, objects or text in the image.

### Editor

Crop, adjust light and colour, apply filters, and use AI editing tools on supported devices.

### Memories and albums

Revisit past moments, and create shared albums with family.

Hide private photos behind your screen lock.

## How to install Google Photos APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Google Photos**.
2. Check that the developer is **Google LLC**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app. Google Photos is preinstalled on most Android phones.

The official listing is [Google Photos on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.photos). Avoid Google Photos APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Google LLC and can carry malware or adware.

## Google Photos storage tips

- **Choose backup quality** in settings: Storage saver keeps more photos within your quota.
- **Review large videos** with the new storage tool.
- **Free up space** on the phone once photos are backed up.
- **Check your Google storage** in the Google One section of your account.

## Is Google Photos APK safe?

The official Google Photos app is safe. It's published by Google LLC and rated Everyone, with no ads. Protect your Google account with 2-Step Verification, because anyone with your password could see your photos.

## Google Photos on PC and iPhone

Open photos.google.com in any browser to view, download and organise your library, or install Google Photos from the App Store on iPhone. Everything you back up from your Android phone appears on every device signed in to the same Google account.

## Sharing photos with family

Create a shared album and invite family members, who can add their own photos too. Partner sharing can automatically share photos of chosen people with one other account. Links to albums can be turned off at any time in sharing settings.

## Downloading all your photos

To move your library elsewhere, use Google Takeout (takeout.google.com) to download all your photos and videos. It's worth doing a full download once a year as a second backup.

## Who should use Google Photos

Almost every Android user benefits from turning on backup: a lost or broken phone then doesn't mean lost memories. If you'd rather not store photos with Google, back up to a computer instead.

## Final thoughts

The Google Photos APK keeps your photos safe and searchable on Android. Turn on backup, use the Locked Folder for private photos, and run the new storage tool when space gets tight.
