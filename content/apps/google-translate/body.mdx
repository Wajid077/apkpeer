## What is Google Translate?

Google Translate is Google's translation app. Type or paste text, speak, point your camera at a sign or menu, or write with your finger, and it translates between more than 100 languages. Conversation mode lets two people talk in different languages with the phone translating out loud, and Tap to Translate works inside any other app.

The app has passed 1 billion downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [tool apps](/category/tools) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="google-lens">Google Lens</AppLink>, which shares its camera translation features.

Translation quality is improved on Google's servers, so online translations get better even between app updates. Downloaded offline language packs update separately; the app tells you when a newer pack is available.

## Key features

### Text translation

Type or paste text and see the translation instantly, with a speaker button to hear it pronounced. Google's Play description lists typed translation between 108 languages, and the app keeps adding more.

### Camera translation

Point your camera at a menu, sign or document and see the translation placed over the original text. You can also import a photo from your gallery and select the text you need.

### Conversation mode

Tap the conversation button, choose two languages, and speak in turn. Translate detects who is speaking and reads the translation aloud, which makes simple conversations possible when travelling.

### Tap to Translate

Copy text in any app, such as a chat or a web page, and a floating Translate icon appears. Tap it to see the translation without leaving that app.

### Offline translation

Download languages to translate without internet access, which is useful abroad when you don't have roaming data.

### Transcribe and handwriting

Transcribe continuous speech in some languages, which helps with lectures or longer talks, and draw characters with your finger for languages with unfamiliar scripts.

## How to install Google Translate APK on Android

Google Translate is preinstalled on many phones. Otherwise install [Google Translate from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.translate).

1. Open Google Play, search for Google Translate, and check the developer is Google LLC.
2. Tap **Install** and open the app.
3. Choose your languages and, if you're travelling, download them for offline use.

Without Google Play, you can use translate.google.com in a browser. APKPeer doesn't host a separate file.

## Tips for travelling with Google Translate

- **Download before you leave.** Offline languages are large, so download them on Wi-Fi at home, along with your own language.
- **Keep sentences short.** Short, simple sentences translate more accurately than long ones with slang.
- **Use conversation mode in quiet places.** Background noise confuses speech recognition.
- **Save phrases.** Star useful translations to keep them in your phrasebook, such as allergies, addresses and directions.
- **Show, don't just speak.** In noisy places, turn the screen around and show the translated text in large print.

## Is Google Translate APK safe?

Yes. Google Translate from Google Play is safe. It's published by Google LLC, rated Everyone, and has no ads or in-app purchases.

- **Privacy.** Text you translate online is sent to Google's servers. Don't use online translation for confidential documents, passwords or sensitive personal information. Offline translation keeps text on your phone.
- **Accuracy.** Treat machine translation as a helper, not a final version, for anything official, medical or legal.
- **Fake translator apps.** Many lookalike translator apps on app stores are filled with ads or charge weekly subscriptions. The real app is published by Google LLC.


## Translating photos and documents

To translate a document, open the camera in Google Translate and tap **Scan** to capture the whole page, then select the text you need. You can also import images from your gallery. For PDFs and Word documents, the web version at translate.google.com has a document translation option that keeps the layout. The camera works best with clear printed text in good light; handwriting and decorative fonts are harder. On menus, use instant camera translation to understand dishes at a glance, then switch to conversation mode if you need to ask the waiter a question.

## Google Translate vs alternatives

- **<AppLink slug="google-lens">Google Lens</AppLink>** is better for translating things your camera sees, and for identifying objects and copying text.
- **<AppLink slug="duolingo">Duolingo</AppLink>** teaches you the language, rather than translating for you.
- **<AppLink slug="google-gemini">Google Gemini</AppLink>** can translate and explain the nuance of a phrase, such as tone and formality.

For travel, menus and quick messages in another language, Google Translate is the app to have. Download your languages before your trip so it works even without data.
