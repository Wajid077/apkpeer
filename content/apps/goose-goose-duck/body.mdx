## What is Goose Goose Duck?

Goose Goose Duck is a social deduction game from Gaggle Studios. The Goose Goose Duck APK installs it on Android: geese complete tasks around the map while ducks secretly sabotage and eliminate them. Players talk with built-in proximity voice chat, call meetings, and vote out who they think is a duck. Dozens of special roles, like the Pelican and the Dodo, keep every game different.

It's one of the [action games](/category/action) we cover, alongside <AppLink slug="among-us">Among Us</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Geese, ducks and neutrals

Geese win by finishing tasks or voting out ducks; ducks win by sabotage; neutral roles have their own goals.

### Roles

Dozens of roles with special abilities change each round.

### Voice chat

Proximity chat lets you hear players near you on the map.

### Modes

Classic, Draft and seasonal modes such as Trick or Treat.
