## What is Granny?

Granny is a first-person horror escape game. You wake up locked inside Granny's house, and you have five days to get out. Granny hears everything: drop an item, step on a creaky floorboard or knock over a vase, and she comes looking for you. You search the house for keys, tools and items that open the exit, while hiding under beds and in cupboards when she's near.

Granny is made by DVloper, a small indie developer. Since its release in November 2017, it has passed 500 million downloads on Google Play and become a favourite of horror game streamers. It's one of the [adventure games](/category/adventure) we list.

## Key features

### Five days to escape

Each time Granny catches you, a day passes. Escape before the end of day five, or the game is over.

### Sound matters

Granny reacts to noise. Moving slowly, avoiding creaky floors and placing items carefully keeps you hidden.

### Hiding places

Hide under beds, in wardrobes and other places when she's coming.

### Items and puzzles

Find keys, tools and objects hidden around the house, then work out where they're used.

### Difficulty modes

Choose from easier modes, where Granny is slower, to harder modes, or practice mode, where Granny isn't in the house at all.

## Tips for escaping

- **Use practice mode first** to learn the house layout without Granny.
- **Move slowly.** Crouching and slow movement make less noise.
- **Learn where items spawn.** Items appear in different places each time, but in a limited set of locations.
- **Drop items on soft surfaces** like beds to avoid noise.
- **Listen for her footsteps** and hide early.
- **Find the weapon.** Some items let you stop Granny for a short time.

## Difficulty modes explained

Granny offers several difficulty levels. In easy mode, Granny moves more slowly and you have more time to react. Normal is the standard challenge, while hard and extreme make her faster and more alert, with fewer chances to hide. Practice mode removes Granny entirely, so you can explore the house, find item locations and learn routes without pressure. Many players learn the layout in practice mode, then try to escape on normal, before taking on the harder modes for a real challenge.

## Granny with headphones

Sound is everything in Granny. Her footsteps, creaking doors and the sounds of items being moved give away where she is. Playing with headphones makes it much easier to judge which direction she's coming from and when it's safe to move. Turn the game volume up and your phone's other sounds down. Remember, she hears you too: every noise you make can draw her to your location, so listen before you move.
