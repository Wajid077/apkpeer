## What is Grok?

Grok is an AI assistant you can chat with, ask questions, use for writing and coding help, and use to create images. It was built by xAI, the AI company founded by Elon Musk, and it's closely tied to X (formerly Twitter): Grok can search public posts on X to answer questions about what's happening right now. On Google Play the publisher is now listed as SpaceX Super Intelligence.

The standalone Android app arrived on Google Play in March 2025 and has passed 100 million downloads with a 4.9-star average. It's one of several AI assistants in our [productivity apps](/category/productivity) category.

## Key features

### Chat with real-time information

Ask Grok anything and it answers in a conversational style. With search enabled, it looks at the web and public posts on X, which makes it quick at summarising breaking news and online discussions. Remember that posts on X aren't fact-checked, so real-time answers can repeat rumours.

### Image creation and editing

Describe an image and Grok generates it, or upload a photo and ask for changes. The app also offers short video generation from images on supported plans.

### Voice mode

Talk to Grok out loud and hear spoken replies, with several voices to pick from. It's handy for hands-free questions or practising conversation.

### Reasoning modes

For hard problems, switch to a "think" mode where Grok spends longer working through maths, logic or code before answering.

### Free and SuperGrok tiers

Everyone gets limited free use. SuperGrok subscriptions, bought inside the app, raise message limits and unlock the newest models and extra media features. Play lists in-app purchases from $4.99 to $300.00 per item.

## How to install Grok APK on Android

Install [Grok from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.x.grok).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 9 or newer.
2. Open Google Play, search for Grok, and check the developer is SpaceX Super Intelligence.
3. Tap **Install** and open the app.
4. Sign in with X, Google, Apple or an email address.

If Play isn't available on your phone, you can use Grok in a browser at grok.com. APKPeer links to the official listing and doesn't host a separate APK.

## Is Grok APK safe?

The official Grok app is safe to install from Google Play. It has no ads and is rated Teen. Things to keep in mind:

- **Content.** Grok is intentionally less restrictive than many AI assistants, and its image tools drew strong criticism and regulatory attention after users created sexualised images of real people. The company has since added limits, but parents should still keep it away from younger children.
- **Accuracy.** Real-time answers built from posts on X can include misinformation. Check important facts against reliable news sources.
- **Privacy.** Your conversations are stored with your account. Review the data controls in settings, including whether your chats can be used to train models, and delete conversations you don't want kept.
- **Fake apps.** The real package name is ai.x.grok. Avoid "Grok AI" lookalikes from other developers.


## Grok settings worth checking

- **Data controls.** In settings, choose whether your conversations can be used to improve Grok's models, and delete chats you don't want stored.
- **Private chat.** A private or temporary chat option keeps a conversation out of your history.
- **Search sources.** You can ask Grok to show the posts and web pages it used. For news, compare those with an established news outlet before sharing.
- **Linked X account.** If you signed in with X, your Grok activity and X activity are connected. Signing in with an email address keeps them more separate.

## Free vs SuperGrok

Free use covers everyday chat, some image generation and voice mode, with daily limits that can change. SuperGrok raises those limits, gives priority access when the service is busy, and unlocks the most advanced models and media features. Subscriptions bought in the Android app are billed through Google Play, so manage or cancel them under **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions**. If you mostly use Grok to catch up on news, the free tier is usually enough. Heavy users of image and video tools are the ones most likely to hit limits.
