## What is GTA: San Andreas?

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas is one of the most celebrated open-world games ever made. Set in the early 1990s, it follows Carl "CJ" Johnson, who returns home to Los Santos after his mother's death and gets pulled back into gang life. The story takes him across the state of San Andreas, through cities inspired by Los Angeles, San Francisco and Las Vegas, with huge countryside areas in between.

Rockstar Games brought San Andreas to Android in December 2013. On Google Play it costs $6.99, has passed 5 million downloads, and is rated Mature 17+. It's one of the [action games](/category/action) we list.

## Key features

### A huge open world

Explore three cities and the countryside, deserts, forests and mountains between them. Drive cars, ride bikes, fly planes and helicopters, and swim.

### A long story

The main story has dozens of missions, from gang wars to heists, with memorable characters and a famous soundtrack of 90s hip hop, rock and country on the in-game radio.

### Customise CJ

Change CJ's clothes, hairstyle and tattoos. Exercise, eat and train to change his body and skills, which affect how he drives, shoots and fights.

### Side activities

Take on taxi, ambulance and firefighter missions, race, gamble, date, buy properties, and find hidden collectibles across the map.

### Mobile features

The Android version includes improved lighting and draw distance compared with the original, customisable touch controls, controller support, and cloud saves through a Rockstar Games Social Club account.

## Tips for playing on a phone

- **Customise the controls.** Move and resize buttons in settings so they suit your hands.
- **Try a controller.** Driving and aiming are much easier with a Bluetooth controller.
- **Save often.** Use safehouses to save before hard missions, and turn on cloud saves.
- **Lower the graphics if needed.** On older phones, lower settings keep the frame rate smooth.
- **Train CJ early.** Improving driving and weapon skills makes later missions easier.
