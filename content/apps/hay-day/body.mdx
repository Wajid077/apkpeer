## What is Hay Day?

Hay Day is a relaxing farming game from Supercell. You grow wheat, corn, carrots and more, raise chickens, cows and pigs, and turn your harvest into goods like bread, cheese and sweaters in production buildings. Then you sell them at your roadside shop, fill truck and boat orders, and expand your farm and the nearby town.

Hay Day came to Google Play in November 2013 and has passed 100 million downloads. It's one of the [simulation games](/category/simulation) we list.

## Key features

### Crops and animals

Plant crops, harvest them, and replant. Feed animals to collect eggs, milk, wool and bacon. It's simple to start but soon becomes a juggling act.

### Production buildings

Use bakeries, dairies, sugar mills and dozens of other buildings to turn raw goods into more valuable products.

### Trading

Sell goods at your roadside shop, buy from other players' shops, and use the newspaper to find bargains. Truck and boat orders give coins and experience.

### Town and fishing

Expand to the town to serve visitors, and go fishing at the lake for extra rewards.

### Neighbourhoods and Derbies

Join a neighbourhood to chat and help each other. Weekly Derbies are team competitions with great rewards.

### Farm Pass

The seasonal Farm Pass has free and paid reward tracks for completing tasks.

## Tips for new farmers

- **Keep fields busy.** Replant straight after harvesting.
- **Don't sell everything.** Keep some crops for animal feed and production.
- **Expand storage early.** Silo and barn space quickly runs out.
- **Use the newspaper.** Find cheap goods from other players' shops.
- **Join an active neighbourhood.** Derbies give some of the best rewards.
- **Save diamonds.** Spend them on expansions and production slots, not on speeding up timers.

## Is Hay Day APK safe?

The official Hay Day from Google Play is safe. It's published by Supercell, rated Everyone, contains ads, and offers in-app purchases from $0.29 to $239.99 per item.

- **Free diamonds scams.** Websites and videos offering free diamonds or coins are scams that steal Supercell IDs.
- **Account selling.** Buying farms or diamonds from third parties breaks Supercell's rules and can lead to bans.
- **Spending.** Use purchase authentication in Google Play and Supercell ID's parental settings for children.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified Hay Day APKs aren't from Supercell and can carry malware.
