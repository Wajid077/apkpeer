Hill Climb Racing 2 is Fingersoft's sequel to the classic physics driving game. ## Tips for faster times

- **Ease off the gas in the air** to control your landing.
- **Upgrade one vehicle fully** before spreading coins around.
- **Pick vehicles for each track**: grip for hills, speed for flats.
- **Use tuning parts** that suit the cup's terrain.

## Coins, gems and chests

Coins upgrade vehicles and come from every race; gems open chests and speed things up. Team chests and daily rewards help a lot without spending. Focus coins on one or two vehicles you use in cups, rather than upgrading everything a little.
