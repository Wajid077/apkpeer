## What is Hill Climb Racing?

Hill Climb Racing is a physics-based driving game from Fingersoft, a Finnish studio. You play Newton Bill, a young driver determined to climb ever-higher hills. Press the gas pedal on the right and the brake on the left, and try to get as far as you can over bumpy terrain without flipping your vehicle, landing on Bill's head, or running out of fuel.

That's the whole game, and it's surprisingly addictive. The physics make every hill a small puzzle: too much gas and you flip backwards, too little and you roll back down. Coins you collect along the way buy upgrades for your engine, suspension, tires and grip, and unlock new vehicles and stages.

It's been on Google Play since September 2012 and has passed a billion downloads. It's small, works offline and runs on almost any phone, which makes it one of the classic [racing games](/category/racing) on Android, even if it's really more about balance than racing.

## Key features

### Simple two-pedal controls

There's a gas pedal and a brake, and that's it. Gas tilts the vehicle back, brake tilts it forward, so balancing in the air is as important as driving on the ground.

### Many vehicles

Start with a basic jeep and unlock vehicles like a motocross bike, a monster truck, a tank and even a moon lander. Each handles very differently, and some are better suited to particular stages.

### Upgrades

Spend coins to upgrade each vehicle's engine, suspension, tires and four-wheel drive or equivalent. Upgrades are the main way to get further on hard stages.

### Varied stages

Stages range from the countryside and desert to the moon, where low gravity sends you flying, and many others with their own terrain and challenges.

### Daily rewards and offline play

Log in for daily rewards, or play entirely offline on a flight. Short runs make it easy to fit a few attempts into a spare minute. The game is small and easy on your phone's battery.

## Tips for going further

Upgrade the engine first, and grab every fuel can you can reach, since running out of fuel ends more runs than crashing early on. Ease off the gas when you're in the air, and use the brake to tilt your nose down before landing. Collect fuel cans whenever you can, even if it means a slower line. And switch vehicles for stages that don't suit your favorite: some climbs are much easier with a bike or a truck.

## Coins and upgrades explained

You earn coins by collecting them on the track and from distance bonuses for reaching milestones. Each upgrade level costs more than the last, so early upgrades are cheap and big gains come quickly, then progress slows. Rather than maxing one vehicle, it's often better to unlock a new vehicle that suits a stage you're stuck on. Daily rewards add a steady trickle of coins if you open the game regularly.
