## Roles explained

- **Tank:** absorbs damage and starts fights.
- **Fighter:** durable damage dealer for the side lanes.
- **Assassin:** fast jungler who picks off weak enemies.
- **Mage:** strong skill damage from range.
- **Marksman:** steady damage that grows late in the game.
- **Support:** protects allies and controls fights.

New players do well starting as a tank or marksman, which teach positioning without needing perfect timing.
