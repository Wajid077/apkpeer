## What is Hungry Shark Evolution?

Hungry Shark Evolution is an arcade game published by Ubisoft (developed by Future Games of London), and the Hungry Shark Evolution APK is its Android installer. You control a shark in an open ocean, eating fish, people and anything else to stay alive, while avoiding bigger predators. Survive, earn coins and unlock larger sharks up to prehistoric giants.
