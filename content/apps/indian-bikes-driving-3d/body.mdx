## What is Indian Bikes Driving 3D?

Indian Bikes Driving 3D is an open-world driving game. You start in a city with famous Indian motorbikes and cars, and you can ride, drive, explore, do stunts, and cause chaos however you like. There's no strict story: the fun comes from trying different vehicles, jumping ramps, and experimenting with the city's sandbox.

Published by Rohit Gaming Studio in December 2020, the game became a viral hit, especially in India, and has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [simulation games](/category/simulation) we list. We read its details from the Indian Google Play store.

## Key features

### Open-world city

Explore a city with roads, highways, ramps and buildings, on foot or by vehicle. There's no time limit, so you can play for a few minutes or an hour.

### Indian bikes and cars

Ride popular Indian motorbikes and drive cars, trucks and other vehicles, many inspired by famous real models.

### Vehicle codes

A built-in phone lets you type number codes to spawn vehicles and items instantly. Players share lists of codes online, and finding new ones is part of the fun.

### Stunts and physics

Jump ramps, flip bikes and try stunts. The physics are arcade-style rather than realistic, which makes experimenting easy.

### Offline play

The game works offline, so you can play anywhere.

## Tips for new players

- **Learn the controls first.** Practise steering and braking on quiet roads before trying stunts.
- **Try different vehicles.** Each bike and car handles differently, and some are much faster.
- **Use the map.** Find ramps and open areas for stunts.
- **Look up official codes.** Codes are a built-in feature; you only need the in-game phone.
- **Close other apps.** On older phones, closing background apps helps the game run smoothly.

## Is Indian Bikes Driving 3D APK safe?

The official Indian Bikes Driving 3D from Google Play is safe. It's published by Rohit Gaming Studio, rated 18+ in India for extreme violence, contains ads, and has no in-app purchases.

- **Copycat games.** Many games use almost the same name and icon, often with more aggressive ads. Check that the developer is Rohit Gaming Studio and the package name is com.Rohit.IndianBikes.
- **Age rating.** The 18+ rating in India is for violence. Younger players are attracted by the bright graphics, so parents should decide whether it's suitable.
- **Ads.** Ads appear during play. Turning off mobile data reduces them, though you may lose some features.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified versions claiming to remove ads or add vehicles aren't from the developer and can carry malware. The built-in codes already unlock vehicles for free.
