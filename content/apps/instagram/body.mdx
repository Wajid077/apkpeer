## Key features

### Reels

Short vertical videos with music, effects and templates. Reels are where most new followers find accounts.

### Stories and Close Friends

Stories last 24 hours. Share them with everyone, or only with your Close Friends list.

### Posts and carousels

Share up to 20 photos and videos in one post, with captions, tags and locations.

### Direct Messages

Chat one-to-one or in groups, send voice notes, share posts and video call.

### Teen Accounts

Accounts for under-16s are private by default, with limits on messages from strangers and on sensitive content.

## Is Instagram APK safe?

The official Instagram app from Google Play is safe to install. It's published by Instagram (Meta), rated Teen, and contains ads.

- **Lookalike apps.** Instagram clones that promise to show who viewed your profile or download any video aren't from Meta and can steal your password. Unofficial Instagram APKs from download sites carry the same risk.
- **Account security.** Turn on two-factor authentication in **Accounts Center → Password and security**.
- **Teens.** Keep Teen Account settings on, and use Supervision in the Family Center.
- **Privacy.** Review **Settings → Account privacy** and turn on a private account if you only want approved followers.

## Tips for using Instagram on Android

- **Save data.** In **Settings → Data usage and media quality**, turn on Data saver so Reels don't load in high quality on mobile data.
- **Clean up your feed.** Tap the three dots on a post and choose **Not interested**, or mute accounts without unfollowing them.
- **Switch accounts fast.** Add up to five accounts in Settings and switch by long-pressing your profile picture.
- **Take a break.** **Settings → Time management** sets a daily limit and reminders.
- **Archive instead of deleting.** Archived posts disappear from your profile but stay saved for you.
- **Download your data.** **Accounts Center → Your information and permissions** lets you export your photos and messages.
