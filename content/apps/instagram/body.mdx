## What is Instagram?

Instagram is Meta's photo and video sharing app. You follow friends, creators and brands, and see their posts, Stories and Reels; you share your own; and you message people directly. It launched on Android in April 2012 and has passed five billion downloads on Google Play, which makes it one of the most installed apps there.

Over the years it has moved from square photos to video. Reels, short vertical videos with music and effects, now take up much of the app. Stories (posts that disappear after 24 hours) and direct messages are where most conversations with friends happen.

The app is free and paid for by ads that appear in your feed, Stories and Reels. It's one of the [social media apps](/category/social) we cover, and like the others, the main things worth getting right are privacy settings and installing the real app.

## Key features

### Reels

Reels are short vertical videos with music, effects and text. Tap **+** and choose **Reel** to record or upload clips, add audio from Instagram's library, and trim them on a simple timeline. The Reels tab shows an endless feed of recommendations.

### Stories

Stories are photos and videos that disappear after 24 hours. You can add stickers for polls, questions, links and music, and share a Story only with your **Close Friends** list if you don't want everyone to see it.

### Posts and the feed

Regular posts can be single photos, videos, or carousels that combine several photos and videos. They stay on your profile grid until you delete or archive them.

### Direct messages

Message friends privately, send Reels and posts, make voice and video calls, and create group chats. You can choose who can message you under privacy settings.

### Privacy and teen protections

Make your account private, limit who can comment or tag you, mute or restrict people, and hide like counts. Accounts for users under 18 are set up as Teen Accounts, which are private by default with stricter messaging rules and time-limit reminders.

## Quick privacy checklist

Five settings worth checking under your profile's menu:

1. **Account privacy:** switch on Private account if only people you approve should see your posts.
2. **Close Friends:** set up a list for Stories you don't want everyone to see.
3. **Messages:** limit who can send you message requests.
4. **Two-factor authentication:** turn it on in Accounts Center under **Password and security**.
5. **Activity status:** turn it off if you don't want people to see when you're online.

## How to install Instagram APK on Android

Instagram doesn't offer its own APK download, so [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.instagram.android) is the official source for Android.

1. Open Google Play, search for Instagram, and check that the developer is listed as Instagram.
2. Tap **Install**, then open the app and log in or create an account.
3. If your phone has no Play Store, any Instagram APK you find comes from a third-party mirror, not from Instagram. If you use one, the file must be signed by Instagram, so Android will refuse to update an existing official Instagram with it if it isn't.
4. If you downloaded a file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for Instagram.
5. If you installed from a file, turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is Instagram APK safe?

The official Instagram app from Google Play is safe to install. It's published by Instagram (Meta), rated Teen, and asks for permissions that match its features: camera and microphone for creating content, photos for posting, and optional access to contacts and location.

Privacy is where to pay attention. Instagram collects a lot of data about what you view and interact with to personalize your feed and ads, and it shares data with other Meta apps, as its privacy policy explains. Reviewing **Accounts Center → Ad preferences** lets you see and adjust how ads are targeted.

The biggest risk is modified Instagram clones promising extra features like downloading Reels or viewing Stories anonymously. They aren't made by Meta, they can steal your login, and Instagram suspends accounts that use them. APKPeer only links to the official app.
