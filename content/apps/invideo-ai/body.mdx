## What is invideo?

invideo is an AI video maker from invideo INC, and its Android app (listed on Google Play as "invideo: AI Video Editor") puts the same tools from the [invideo.io](https://invideo.io/) website on your phone. You describe the video you want, and invideo writes a script, assembles footage, adds a voiceover, music and captions, and hands you an edit you can change.

That's a different starting point from most [video editors](/category/video-editors). Apps like CapCut or InShot expect you to bring your own clips and cut them. invideo starts from a prompt and builds the video for you, which suits explainer videos, ads, product clips and social posts where you don't have footage of your own.

The app arrived on Google Play in May 2024, and the current version is built around AI agents you direct in plain language, plus a timeline for when you want to take over by hand.

## Key features

### Prompt to finished video

Start a new project, type what the video is about, who it's for and where it's going (say, a 30-second Instagram ad for a bakery), and invideo produces a first draft with script, visuals, voiceover and music. It's a draft, not a final cut: expect to adjust it.

### Editing by asking

Instead of hunting through menus, you can ask for changes in plain language: "make the intro shorter," "use a female voiceover," or "swap the stock clip in scene three for something outdoors." The agent applies the change and you review it.

### A real timeline when you want control

When the AI gets something almost right, you can open the multitrack timeline and trim clips, move captions or adjust audio yourself. That's what separates invideo from simpler text-to-video apps that only let you regenerate.

### Generated media

invideo can generate images, video clips, voiceovers, sound effects and music with the AI models built into the app. That helps when stock footage doesn't fit, though generation uses your plan's credits.

### Team projects

Projects sync with your invideo account, so you can start on your phone and finish on the website. Teams can edit together and comment on individual frames, which is handy for client approvals.

## How to install invideo AI APK on Android

The easiest route is [invideo on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.invideo.ai). If your phone doesn't have Play Store, install the official APK carefully.

1. Make sure your phone runs Android 11 or newer. Older versions can't install 4.5.64.
2. Get the app only from Google Play or invideo's official site. Skip "pro" or "premium" invideo APKs on download sites.
3. If you downloaded the file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We currently link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for invideo.
4. Open the APK and allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks.
5. Tap **Install**, open invideo and sign in or create an account.
6. Go to **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps** and turn the permission off again.

## Is invideo AI APK safe?

The official invideo app is safe. It's published on Google Play by invideo INC, it has more than 10 million installs, and Play doesn't mark it as containing ads. It needs an account, because your projects and plan live on invideo's servers.

Two things are worth knowing before you pay. First, the price range on Play is wide: in-app purchases run from $9.99 to $4,608.00 per item, because the same app sells individual and team plans. Check the plan details on invideo.io before you subscribe. Second, AI generation works on credits, so long videos with lots of generated footage can use up a plan faster than you'd expect.

As with any AI tool, check the rights to what you publish. Read invideo's terms on stock media and generated content if you're making ads or client work.

Modified "premium" APKs of invideo aren't worth the risk. The AI runs on invideo's servers, so a modified app can't unlock it, but it can steal your login. APKPeer only links to the official app.
