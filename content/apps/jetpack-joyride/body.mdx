## What is Jetpack Joyride?

Jetpack Joyride is an endless runner from Halfbrick Studios, and the Jetpack Joyride APK is its Android installer. You play Barry Steakfries, who steals an experimental jetpack and flies through a secret lab. Tap to rise, let go to fall, collect coins, and dodge zappers, lasers and missiles for as long as you can. It's rated Everyone 10+ for cartoon fantasy violence.

It's one of the [arcade games](/category/arcade) we cover, alongside <AppLink slug="fruit-ninja">Fruit Ninja</AppLink> from the same studio.

## Key features

### One-touch flying

Hold to fly up, release to drop. Easy to learn, hard to master.

### Vehicles

Grab power-up vehicles like the Lil' Stomper, Crazy Freaking Teleporter and Profit Bird; each one absorbs a hit.

### Missions and ranks

Complete three missions at a time to rank up and earn rewards.

### Gadgets and jetpacks

Equip gadgets like the Coin Magnet and unlock jetpacks with different effects.

### End-of-run rewards

Tokens you pick up during a run turn into bonus rewards at the end, such as extra coins or a second chance.

## Tips for longer runs

- **Short taps** give finer control than holding.
- **Stay in the middle** of the screen so you can react to zappers above and below.
- **Buy the Coin Magnet** early; it pays for itself quickly.
- **Save headstarts** for when you're chasing a distance mission.
