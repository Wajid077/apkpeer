## What is JioHotstar?

JioHotstar is India's biggest streaming app, run by JioStar India. It was created in 2025 when Disney+ Hotstar and JioCinema merged into a single service. The app streams live cricket and other sports, Indian TV serials from Star channels, Hindi and regional films, web series, and Disney, Pixar, Marvel and Hollywood titles, in many Indian languages.

JioHotstar has passed 1 billion downloads on Google Play, inheriting Hotstar's listing that dates back to 2015. It's one of the [entertainment apps](/category/entertainment) we list. We read its details from the Indian Google Play store.

## Key features

### Live cricket and sports

Watch live cricket, including the IPL and international matches covered by JioStar's rights, plus football, kabaddi, tennis and other sports. Live matches offer multiple languages and camera options, and highlights are posted quickly.

### Indian TV shows

Catch episodes of Star channel serials, sometimes before they air on TV, plus reality shows and regional-language programming.

### Films and series

Stream Bollywood and regional films, JioHotstar originals, and international content including Disney, Pixar, Marvel and Star Wars titles.

### Many languages

The app works in multiple Indian languages, and many titles and matches offer audio in Hindi, English and regional languages.

### Downloads and profiles

Download eligible titles to watch offline, and set up profiles for family members, including kids profiles.

## How to install JioHotstar APK on Android

Install [JioHotstar from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.startv.hotstar).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 5.0 or newer.
2. Open Google Play, search for JioHotstar, and confirm the developer is JIOSTAR INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED.
3. Tap **Install**. If you already have Hotstar installed, just update it.
4. Open the app and log in with your mobile number. If your Jio plan includes JioHotstar, use the same number.

JioStar doesn't publish its own APK. Files on other sites aren't official and are often used to spread malware.

## Plans and tips

- **Plans.** JioHotstar offers several tiers, from mobile-only plans with ads to premium plans for TVs without ads. Prices change with major tournaments, so check the app's plan page.
- **Bundled plans.** Many Jio prepaid, postpaid and fibre plans include JioHotstar. Log in with your Jio number to activate it.
- **Data saving.** Lower the video quality in settings on mobile data; a live cricket match in high quality can use a lot of data.
- **Big matches.** Update the app a day before a major match. Updates and logins on match day can be slow when millions of people join at once.

## Is JioHotstar APK safe?

The official JioHotstar app from Google Play is safe. It's published by JIOSTAR INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED, rated 12+ in India, contains ads, and offers in-app purchases for subscriptions.

The main risks come from unofficial apps:

- **Modified "free premium" APKs** that promise free cricket or premium content are not from JioStar. They often carry adware or malware, stop working mid-match, and break the law in India.
- **Illegal cricket streaming apps** and websites are common during big tournaments. Besides the legal risk, they're full of aggressive ads and scams.
- **Fake recharge offers.** Messages offering free JioHotstar subscriptions through links are usually phishing. Check offers in the MyJio app or the JioHotstar app itself.


## Watching on TV and other devices

JioHotstar works on Android TV, smart TVs, Fire TV, Chromecast and web browsers as well as phones. Your plan decides how many devices can watch at the same time and whether TV viewing is included, so check before a big tournament. To cast from your phone, tap the cast icon during playback with your phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi network. For live cricket, the TV apps usually offer the best picture and multiple camera angles. Log out of devices you no longer use from the app's account settings, especially if you've shared your login with family.
