## What is KineMaster?

KineMaster is a video editor for Android, and the KineMaster APK is its installer. It's one of the longest-running mobile editors, with multiple video layers, chroma key (green screen), blending modes, keyframes, voiceovers and an asset store with music, effects and fonts. Recent versions add AI tools such as background removal and template suggestions.

## Key features

### Layers

Stack videos, images, stickers, text and handwriting, and blend them with modes like Screen and Multiply.

### Chroma key

Remove a green or blue background to place yourself anywhere.

### AI tools

Background removal, noise reduction and smart template suggestions help speed up editing.

### Audio

Record voiceovers, add music, adjust volume envelopes and use audio effects.

### Asset store

Download transitions, effects, stickers, fonts and music; some are free, many need the subscription.

## KineMaster tips

- Check **Device Capability Information** in settings to see how many layers your phone can handle.
- Record green screen footage with **even lighting** and some distance from the background.
- Export at **1080p, 30 fps** for social media to keep files small.
