## Beginner deck tips

- Fill your **curve**: cards for turns 1 to 6 so you always have a play.
- Read **location effects** before committing your big cards.
- **Retreat** when a game turns against you; losing 1 cube beats losing 4 or 8.
- Upgrade cards you use to raise your **Collection Level** and earn new cards.
