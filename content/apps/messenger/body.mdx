## What is Messenger?

Messenger is Meta's chat app for messaging your Facebook friends and contacts. You can send texts, voice messages, photos, videos and files, make voice and video calls, start group chats and share your location. Since late 2023, personal chats and calls are end-to-end encrypted by default, which means only you and the person you're talking to can read them.

Messenger has passed 5 billion downloads on Google Play and holds a 4.7-star rating. It sits in our [communication apps](/category/communication) category, alongside <AppLink slug="whatsapp">WhatsApp</AppLink>, Meta's other messaging app.

## Key features

### Encrypted chats and calls

Personal messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted by default. You can check a chat's encryption details and, for extra privacy, turn on disappearing messages so they vanish after a set time.

### Voice and video calls

Make free calls over Wi-Fi or mobile data, one-to-one or with groups. You can share your screen, use filters and effects, and switch between front and back cameras.

### Group chats

Create group chats for family or friends, name them, add nicknames and themes, and use polls to make decisions. Admins can approve new members.

### Media and files

Send high-quality photos and videos, voice messages, documents and GIFs. Replies, reactions and editing a message shortly after sending are all supported.

### Message requests and controls

Messages from people you aren't connected to go to Message requests, where you can accept or delete them. You can mute, restrict or block anyone.

## How to install Messenger APK on Android

Install [Messenger from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.facebook.orca).

1. Open Google Play, search for Messenger, and check the developer is Meta Platforms, Inc.
2. Tap **Install**. Play only shows Install when your phone is compatible.
3. Open Messenger and log in with your Facebook account.
4. Allow notifications so you don't miss messages and calls, and microphone and camera access when you first make a call.

Meta doesn't publish Messenger as a separate APK outside Play. Unofficial files can steal your Facebook login, and an old version can stop working with Meta's servers.

## Is Messenger APK safe?

The official Messenger app is safe to install from Google Play. It's published by Meta Platforms, Inc., rated Everyone, and lists in-app purchases from $0.05 to $7,999.99 per item, such as Stars for creators. Play doesn't show an ads label, but Messenger can show ads in the Chats list in many regions.

- **Scams.** Be suspicious of messages from friends asking for money, gift cards or codes. Their account may have been taken over, so call them to check.
- **Unknown links.** Don't tap links promising prizes, giveaways or "Is this you in this video?". These often lead to fake login pages.
- **Privacy settings.** In **Settings → Privacy & safety**, control who can message you and who can see when you're active.
- **Kids.** Children under 13 should use Messenger Kids, which parents manage.


## Messenger features you might miss

- **Disappearing messages.** Open a chat, tap the name at the top, and turn on disappearing messages so new messages vanish after a set time.
- **Vanish mode.** Swipe up in a chat to start vanish mode, where messages disappear once seen and the chat closes.
- **Edit and unsend.** You can edit a message for a short time after sending, and remove a message for everyone.
- **Chat lock and app lock.** In privacy settings, require your fingerprint or face to open Messenger.
- **Shared media.** Tap a chat's name to find all photos, videos, links and files shared in it.

## Saving data and storage

Messenger downloads photos and videos automatically by default. In **Settings → Data saver**, turn data saver on to load media only when you tap it, which helps on limited mobile plans. To free up space, go to **Settings → Apps → Messenger → Storage** and clear the cache. Your chats stay safe because they're stored with your account. To keep a copy of encrypted chats when you change phones, set up secure storage with a PIN in Messenger's privacy settings, so your history can be restored on the new device.

## Messenger vs alternatives

- **<AppLink slug="whatsapp">WhatsApp</AppLink>** uses your phone number instead of a Facebook account, and is the default chat app in much of the world.
- **<AppLink slug="telegram">Telegram</AppLink>** offers huge groups, channels and cloud sync across devices.
- **<AppLink slug="signal">Signal</AppLink>** is run by a non-profit and collects the least data of any major messenger.

If most of the people you talk to are on Facebook, Messenger is the easiest way to reach them. Turn on disappearing messages for sensitive chats and keep the app updated so encryption and calls keep working.
