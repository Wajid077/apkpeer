## Minecraft Bedrock vs Java

The Android game is Minecraft Bedrock Edition. Bedrock players on phones, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and iPhone can all play together. Minecraft: Java Edition is the PC-only version with its own servers and community add-ons, and Bedrock can't join Java servers. If your friends play on consoles or phones, Bedrock is what you want.

## Your first night in Minecraft

1. Punch trees for wood, then make a crafting table, pickaxe and sword.
2. Build a small shelter before dark, and light it with torches.
3. Make a bed from three wool and three planks to skip the night.
4. Mine coal and iron for better tools.
5. Don't dig straight down, because you might fall into a cave or lava.

## Realms and playing with friends

Realms is Mojang's paid subscription for a private server that's always online, so friends can join your world even when you're not playing. For free, you can play with friends on the same Wi-Fi or join each other's worlds through Xbox friends.
