## What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a sandbox game made of blocks. You explore worlds that generate endlessly, gather resources, craft tools, build anything you can imagine and survive the night against creepers, zombies and skeletons. In Creative mode you have unlimited blocks and can fly, making it a digital building set; in Survival you have to earn everything.

Mojang, owned by Microsoft, first released Minecraft on Android in 2011. The Google Play version is now Minecraft Bedrock Edition, with cross-play across consoles, PC and phones. It has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play, and it's one of the [adventure games](/category/adventure) we list, alongside the free <AppLink slug="minecraft-trial">Minecraft Trial</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Survival, Creative and Adventure

Survival is the classic challenge: gather, craft, build shelter and survive. Creative gives you every block and lets you fly. Adventure mode is for custom maps made by players and creators.

### Endless worlds

Every world is generated from a seed, with biomes like forests, deserts, oceans, snowy mountains and deep caves. Travel to the Nether and the End to face the Ender Dragon.

### Redstone and farming

Redstone works like electrical wiring, letting you build doors, traps, farms and even working computers. Automate crops, animals and resource collection.

### Multiplayer and cross-play

Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi, through Xbox friends, on featured servers, or on a Realms subscription server that's always online. Bedrock cross-play works with Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, Windows and iPhone.

### Marketplace

Buy skins, texture packs, worlds and mini-games made by creators with Minecoins. Some free content is offered too.

## Tips for your first night

- **Punch a tree straight away.** Wood makes a crafting table, tools and a shelter.
- **Make a bed.** Three wool and three planks let you skip the night and set your spawn point.
- **Light up your base.** Torches stop mobs spawning around you.
- **Don't dig straight down.** You might fall into a cave or lava.
- **Use touch controls settings.** Switch between the joystick and D-pad layouts, or connect a controller.
- **Back up worlds.** Export important worlds or use Realms so they're not lost with your phone.

## Is Minecraft APK safe?

The official Minecraft from Google Play is safe. It's published by Mojang, rated Everyone 10+ for fantasy violence, has no ads, and offers in-app purchases from $0.99 to $49.99 per item for Minecoins.

- **Pirated APKs.** "Free Minecraft" APKs are pirated copies, often outdated, and a common way to spread malware. They also can't sign in to Microsoft accounts or join most servers.
- **Kids online.** Use Microsoft Family Safety and Xbox privacy settings to control who children can play and chat with.
- **Third-party servers.** Public servers are run by other people. Choose well-known ones and never share your account password.
- **Marketplace spending.** Use Google Play's purchase authentication so Minecoin purchases need a password.
