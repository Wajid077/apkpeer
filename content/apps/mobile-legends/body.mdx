## What is Mobile Legends: Bang Bang?

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, usually shortened to MLBB, is a free multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game from MOONTON. Two teams of five players each pick a hero and fight across a map with three lanes, a jungle and two bases. You level up during the match, buy items, take objectives like the Turtle and Lord, and win by destroying the enemy base.

It's built for phones: matches usually last around ten to fifteen minutes, the controls are a virtual joystick plus skill buttons, and it runs on modest devices. That made it hugely popular in Southeast Asia, especially the Philippines and Indonesia, where it's also one of the biggest esports.

The global version on Google Play, published by MOONTON, has been there since September 2016 and has more than 500 million downloads. It's one of the most-played [action games](/category/action) on Android, celebrating its 10th anniversary this year.

## Key features

### 5v5 team battles

The classic mode is five against five on a three-lane map. Teams usually split into roles: a tank, a fighter, a mage, a marksman and a jungler (often an assassin). Coordinating with teammates matters more than individual skill.

### A large roster of heroes

MLBB has more than a hundred heroes across six roles: Tank, Fighter, Assassin, Mage, Marksman and Support. Each has a passive and three or four skills. Unlock heroes with Battle Points earned by playing, or with diamonds.

### Ranked seasons

Ranked mode runs in seasons, like S42 in this update, with ranks from Warrior up to Mythic. Each season has rewards for climbing, including a seasonal skin.

### Quick matches

Matches are short by MOBA standards, and the game offers auto-aim and target-lock options to make combat manageable on a touchscreen.

### Esports and events

MLBB has a huge esports scene, with professional leagues and international tournaments, and regular in-game events tied to them.

## Tips for new players

Start in Classic or against AI to learn one hero in each role before playing ranked. Pick heroes your team needs rather than your favorite every time. Watch the minimap often, because most deaths come from enemies you didn't see coming. And focus on objectives such as turrets, Turtle and Lord: games are won by taking the map, not by the number of kills.
