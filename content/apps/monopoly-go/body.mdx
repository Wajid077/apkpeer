## What is Monopoly GO!?

Monopoly GO! is a casual mobile game based on the classic board game, made by Scopely with Hasbro. You roll dice to move around a Monopoly board, collect cash from properties and Chance cards, and spend it on landmarks to complete each board and move on to the next. Along the way, you raid and shut down friends' boards, collect stickers, and join constant events with prizes.

Since launching in April 2023, it has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play and holds a 4.6-star rating. It's one of the [board games](/category/board) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="ludo-king">Ludo King</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Roll and build

Each roll moves your token around the board. Landing on properties earns cash, and passing Go gives a bonus. Spend cash on landmarks to finish a board and unlock the next, each with a new theme.

### Multipliers

Raise the dice multiplier to bet more rolls on a single throw for bigger rewards. It's powerful during events but drains your dice quickly.

### Shutdowns and Bank Heists

Land on a railroad to shut down a friend's landmark, or hit a Bank Heist to steal cash from their vault. Friends can do the same to you, and shields protect your landmarks.

### Sticker albums

Collect stickers to complete themed sets and albums for large rewards. Trade duplicates with friends, and use golden stickers during special trading events.

### Events and tournaments

There's almost always an event running, with milestones that give dice, cash and sticker packs. Short tournaments rank you against other players.

### Partner events

Team up with up to four friends to complete shared goals and earn extra rewards.

## Tips for new players

- **Don't roll at high multipliers without an event.** Use multipliers when an event rewards the tiles you're likely to land on.
- **Let dice refill.** Rolls refill over time; checking in a few times a day is more efficient than buying rolls.
- **Add active friends.** Friends mean trades, gifts and partner events.
- **Upgrade landmarks evenly.** Completed landmarks are harder for friends to damage.
- **Trade stickers.** Duplicates are only useful as trade material or for the vault, so swap them.
- **Set a spending limit.** Offers are frequent and tempting, so decide your budget in advance.

## Is Monopoly GO APK safe?

The official Monopoly GO! from Google Play is safe. It's published by Scopely, rated Everyone with mild fantasy violence, has no ads, and offers in-app purchases from $0.99 to $199.99 per item.

The main risk is spending. The game is designed around frequent offers, and it's easy to buy rolls during an exciting event. Turn on purchase authentication in Google Play so every purchase needs your password or fingerprint, and keep the game away from children's accounts without parental controls.

Be careful with websites and apps promising free dice or "dice generator" tools. Real free dice links only come from Monopoly GO's official social accounts and open directly in the game. Generator sites ask for your login or make you install other apps, and modified Monopoly GO APKs can get your account banned and your progress lost.
