## How floating lyrics work

Floating lyrics show a small Musixmatch window on top of apps like Spotify, YouTube Music and other players. Musixmatch reads the song name from the media notification, finds the lyrics, and scrolls them in time with the music. To use it, give Musixmatch notification access and permission to display over other apps when it asks. You can turn floating lyrics off anytime in settings.

## Musixmatch Premium
