## What is MX Player?

MX Player is one of the oldest and most popular video players on Android. It's been on Google Play since July 2011 and has passed a billion downloads, mostly thanks to two things: it played almost any file smoothly on cheap phones, and its gesture controls made watching videos easier.

The app is now published by Amazon Mobile LLC, after Amazon acquired MX Player's business in 2024. Alongside the local video player, MX Player has a streaming section with films and series in some regions, especially India, which is ad-supported.

At its core, though, it's still a player for your own files: videos you've downloaded or recorded, files on an SD card or USB drive, and network streams. It's one of the best-known [video player apps](/category/video-players), and the main thing to understand before installing it is that the free version shows ads.

## Key features

### Hardware-accelerated playback

MX Player can decode video using your phone's hardware (HW and HW+ decoders) or software (SW). If a video stutters or shows no picture, switching the decoder from the playback menu often fixes it.

### Gesture controls

Swipe up and down on the right side to change volume, on the left side to change brightness, and left or right to seek. Pinch to zoom and pan. Once you're used to them, it's hard to go back to tiny on-screen sliders.

### Subtitles

MX Player supports embedded subtitles and separate subtitle files in common formats. You can change the subtitle track, adjust timing, and change font size, color and position while watching.

### Kids lock

Kids lock blocks touches and calls while a video plays, so a child can watch without accidentally leaving the app or changing settings.

### Multi-core decoding and audio tracks

MX Player uses multiple processor cores to decode demanding files, and lets you switch audio tracks in videos with several languages.

## Tips for smoother playback

If a video won't play, try switching between the HW, HW+ and SW decoders from the playback menu. Turn on background playback for audio if you're listening to something, so it keeps going with the screen off. Use the network stream option to play a URL directly. And if you mostly watch your own files, keep an eye on data usage: the streaming sections can play video ads that use mobile data.

## How to install MX Player APK on Android

Install [MX Player from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mxtech.videoplayer.ad) if you can. Without Play Store, install the official app carefully.

1. Check your phone runs Android 7.0 or newer.
2. Get the app only from Google Play or the official MX Player site. "MX Player Pro" or ad-free files from other sites aren't official.
3. If you downloaded the file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We currently link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for MX Player.
4. Open the APK and allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks.
5. Tap **Install**, open MX Player and allow access to your videos.
6. Turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is MX Player APK safe?

The official MX Player app is safe to install. It's published by Amazon Mobile LLC on Google Play and rated Everyone. Play marks it as containing ads and in-app purchases, from $0.49 to $239.99 per item.

The ads are the main downside. They appear around playback and in the streaming sections, which some people find intrusive. MX Player also needs access to your videos to play them, which is expected for any media player.

Because people dislike the ads, modified "ad-free" MX Player APKs are widely shared. They aren't made by the developer, they're a common way to get adware or worse onto a phone, and they don't receive updates. If you want an ad-free player, the safer choice is a free open-source player like VLC. APKPeer only links to the official app.
