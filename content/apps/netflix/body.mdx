## What is Netflix?

Netflix is the world's largest subscription streaming service. The Android app gives you its full catalogue of series, films, documentaries, stand-up specials and reality shows, plus live events and games included with your membership. It works on phones and tablets, and you can cast to your TV.

Netflix, Inc. has had an Android app since 2011, and it has passed 1 billion downloads on Google Play with a 4.8-star average. It's one of the [entertainment apps](/category/entertainment) we list.

## Key features

### Huge catalogue

Watch Netflix originals and licensed series and films in many languages, with subtitles and audio in multiple languages for most titles. What's available depends on your country.

### Downloads

Download episodes and films to watch offline on planes, trains or anywhere without signal. Downloads for You can automatically keep a few recommendations ready on your phone.

### Profiles

Each person in your household gets a profile with their own recommendations, list and viewing history. Kids profiles show only content for the age you set.

### Games and live events

Netflix games, included with your membership, range from party games you control with your phone to mobile versions of popular titles. Netflix also streams live events such as sports, specials and award shows in some countries.

### Moments and Mobile Feed

Save and share Moments, short clips from shows, and browse a vertical feed of clips to find something to watch.

## How to install Netflix APK on Android

Install [Netflix from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.netflix.mediaclient).

1. Open Google Play, search for Netflix, and check the developer is Netflix, Inc.
2. Tap **Install**. If Play doesn't offer Install, your phone may not meet Netflix's requirements, often because of its video protection (DRM) support.
3. Open Netflix and sign in. If you don't have a membership, sign up on the Netflix website.

Netflix stopped offering its own APK download for most phones, so files on other sites aren't official. A phone that can't install Netflix from Play usually can't play Netflix in high quality anyway.

## Plans and settings worth knowing

- **Plans.** Netflix offers several plans that differ in video quality, how many screens can watch at once, and whether there are ads. Check the current options for your country on the Netflix website.
- **Download quality.** In **App Settings → Download video quality**, choose Standard to save space or Higher for better picture.
- **Mobile data.** In **App Settings → Mobile data usage**, choose Save Data or Wi-Fi Only if you're on a limited plan.
- **Profile lock.** Add a PIN to your profile so others in the house can't use it.
- **Parental controls.** Set maturity ratings and block specific titles for each profile in your account settings.

## Is Netflix APK safe?

The official Netflix app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Netflix, Inc., rated Teen, and has no in-app purchases listed on Play because memberships are managed with Netflix.

The common risks are scams:

- **Fake payment emails and texts.** Messages saying your payment failed and linking to a login page are a classic phishing scam. Open the app or type netflix.com yourself to check your account.
- **"Free Netflix" apps.** Apps or APKs promising free Netflix are fake. They either don't work or try to steal your details.
- **Account sharing.** Netflix limits sharing to people in your household. Extra members can be added for a fee in many countries.


## Watching Netflix on a bigger screen

Tap the cast icon in the Netflix app to send what you're watching to a smart TV, a Chromecast or a TV with Netflix built in. Your phone becomes a remote, and you can keep using it while the show plays. Netflix games designed for TVs also use your phone as a controller. If casting doesn't appear, make sure your phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network and both apps are up to date. For travel, download episodes on hotel or home Wi-Fi before you leave, because some countries' catalogues differ and downloads still play abroad until they expire.
