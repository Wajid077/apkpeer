## How to install Netflix APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone.
2. Search for **Netflix** and check that the developer is **Netflix, Inc.**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open it.
4. Sign in or link an account so your progress is saved if you change phones.

The official listing is [Netflix on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.netflix.mediaclient). Avoid Netflix APK files on other download sites: they aren't from Netflix, Inc. and can carry adware or malware.

If Play doesn't offer Install, your phone may not meet Netflix's video protection requirements. Netflix no longer offers its own APK for most phones.

## Netflix settings worth changing

- **Download quality:** Standard saves space; Higher looks better.
- **Mobile data:** choose Save Data or Wi-Fi Only.
- **Profile lock:** add a PIN to your profile.
- **Maturity ratings:** set limits per profile in your account.

## Is Netflix APK safe?

The official Netflix app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Netflix, Inc. and rated Teen. Watch out for fake "payment failed" emails and texts, which are phishing, and for apps or APKs promising free Netflix, which are fake.

## Netflix plans in short

Netflix offers several plans that differ in video quality, how many screens can watch at once, and whether ads play. In some countries the cheapest plan includes ads. Members can add extra members outside their household for a fee in many countries, because Netflix limits sharing to one household. Check the current plans and prices for your country on the Netflix website, since they change over time.

## Watching Netflix on your TV

Tap the cast icon to send a show from your phone to a smart TV or Chromecast on the same Wi-Fi network. Your phone becomes a remote. Netflix games built for TVs also use your phone as the controller.

## Netflix games

Netflix games are included with your membership, with no ads or extra purchases in most of them. Some play inside the Netflix app, and others install as separate games from Google Play under Netflix's name.

## Final thoughts

The Netflix APK from Google Play is the official way to stream Netflix on Android. Set up profiles and data settings first, and download episodes on Wi-Fi before you travel.
