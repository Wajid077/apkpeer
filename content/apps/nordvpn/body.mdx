## What is NordVPN?

NordVPN is a paid VPN service from Nord Security, and the NordVPN APK is its Android app. A VPN encrypts your internet traffic between your phone and a VPN server, which protects you on public Wi-Fi and hides your browsing from your network provider. NordVPN adds Threat Protection, which blocks known malicious sites and many trackers.

## What a VPN can and can't do

A VPN hides your traffic from the local network and your mobile provider, and makes you appear in the server's country. It doesn't make you anonymous to sites you sign in to, it won't stop you downloading malware, and streaming services may block VPN servers. Use one on public Wi-Fi and where you want more privacy, not as a cure-all.
