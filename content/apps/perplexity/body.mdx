## What is Perplexity?

Perplexity is an AI-powered answer engine. Instead of giving you ten blue links like a normal search engine, it reads current web pages and writes a direct answer, with numbered citations showing exactly where each claim came from. You can tap a citation to open the source, then ask follow-up questions in the same thread.

Perplexity AI, based in San Francisco, released the Android app in 2023, and it has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play with a 4.6-star rating. It sits in our [productivity apps](/category/productivity) category next to general AI assistants like <AppLink slug="chatgpt">ChatGPT</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Answers with sources

Ask a question in plain language and Perplexity searches the web, then writes a summary with inline citation numbers. The source list sits right below the answer, so checking a claim takes one tap. This makes it especially useful for news, product research and anything where you want to know where information came from.

### Follow-up questions

Every answer is a conversation. Ask "What about for Android?" or "Compare that with last year" and Perplexity keeps the context. Suggested follow-ups appear under each answer if you're not sure what to ask next.

### Pro searches

Pro searches spend more time on a question, run several searches, and pull together a longer, more structured answer. Free users get a small number per day; Perplexity Pro raises the limit and lets you choose between AI models from several companies.

### Voice assistant

On Android you can make Perplexity your default assistant. Ask questions by voice, get spoken answers, and use it for some everyday phone tasks without opening the app first.

### Discover and Spaces

The Discover tab shows short AI summaries of trending news with sources. Spaces let you group searches by topic or project and add your own files, which is useful for research you return to over several days.

## How to install Perplexity APK on Android

The simplest option is [Perplexity on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.perplexity.app.android).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 8.0 or newer.
2. Open Google Play, search for Perplexity, and check the developer is PerplexityAI.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app. You can search without an account, but signing in keeps your history synced.
4. To use it as your assistant, open your phone's **Settings → Apps → Default apps → Digital assistant app** and choose Perplexity.

If you can't use Google Play, you can use Perplexity in any browser at perplexity.ai. APKPeer links to the official Play listing and doesn't host a separate file.

## Is Perplexity APK safe?

The official Perplexity app is safe to install. It's published by PerplexityAI on Google Play, rated Teen, and contains no ads. In-app purchases are for the Pro subscription.

A few things are worth knowing:

- **Accuracy.** Citations make answers easier to check, but Perplexity can still misread a page or cite a weak source. Open the links for anything important.
- **Privacy.** Your searches are stored in your library if you're signed in. You can delete threads, and the settings include an option to stop your data being used to improve the AI.
- **Lookalike apps.** Several unrelated "Perplexity" or "AI search" apps exist on Play and other sites. The real package name is ai.perplexity.app.android.


## Getting better answers from Perplexity

Perplexity works best when you ask the way you'd ask a well-read friend, with the details that matter.

- **Be specific.** "Best budget Android phone under $300 with a good camera, released this year" gets a far better answer than "best phone".
- **Pick a focus.** You can narrow a search to academic papers, social discussions or video, which helps when general web results are noisy.
- **Check two sources.** For anything you'll act on, open at least two of the cited links and make sure they agree.
- **Use threads.** Keep related questions in one thread, so Perplexity remembers the context instead of starting over.

## Free vs Perplexity Pro

The free plan gives unlimited quick searches and a few Pro searches each day. Perplexity Pro adds many more Pro searches, a choice of AI models from different companies, file and image uploads for analysis, and longer research reports. Students, journalists and people who research products often find Pro worth it. For quick everyday questions, the free tier is plenty. Pro is billed through Google Play when you subscribe in the Android app, so you can cancel any time under **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions**.
