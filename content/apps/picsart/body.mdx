## What is Picsart?

Picsart is a creative app that combines a photo editor, a video editor and a design studio. You can retouch photos, remove backgrounds, add stickers and text, make collages, design social media posts from templates, and generate images with AI. It's especially popular for quick edits for Instagram, TikTok and messaging apps.

PicsArt, Inc. released the app in 2011. It has passed 1 billion downloads on Google Play, and it's one of the [photo editor apps](/category/photo-editors) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="snapseed">Snapseed</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="canva">Canva</AppLink>.

## Key features

### AI photo tools

Remove backgrounds in one tap, erase unwanted objects, enhance blurry photos, expand an image beyond its edges, and swap backgrounds. AI image generation creates pictures or stickers from a text description.

### Photo editing

Adjust brightness, contrast and colour, apply filters and effects, retouch skin, change hair colour, and add blur or depth. Layers let you combine several images.

### Templates and design

Thousands of templates for posts, stories, flyers, thumbnails and invitations. Add your own photos, change the text and fonts, and export in the right size for each platform.

### Stickers, text and collages

Use a huge library of stickers, create your own from any photo, add stylish text, and build collages with grids, frames or freestyle layouts.

### Video editing

Trim clips, add music, text and effects, and use video templates for social content.

### Drawing

A drawing tool with brushes and layers for sketches, digital art and doodling on photos.

## How to install Picsart APK on Android

Install [Picsart from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.picsart.studio).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 6.0 or newer.
2. Open Google Play, search for Picsart, and confirm the developer is PicsArt, Inc.
3. Tap **Install** and open the app.
4. Allow photo access when asked. On newer Android versions, you can allow access to selected photos only.

PicsArt, Inc. doesn't publish its own APK. Files from other sites aren't official.

## Free vs Plus and Pro

The free version includes the core editor, many filters, basic templates and some AI tools, with ads. Picsart Plus removes ads and unlocks premium content such as filters, fonts, stickers and templates. Picsart Pro adds more AI credits, brand kits and features for teams and businesses. Subscriptions are billed through Google Play in the app; cancel them under **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions** before the trial ends if you don't want to be charged.

## Tips for better edits

- **Use the right export size.** Choose the template or canvas for the platform you're posting to, so nothing gets cropped.
- **Save in high quality.** In settings, set the save quality to high for printing or sharing on large screens.
- **Don't overdo AI retouching.** Subtle skin smoothing looks better than heavy filters.
- **Work in layers.** Keep text, stickers and photo adjustments on separate layers so you can change them later.
- **Save drafts.** Picsart keeps drafts of projects so you can come back to them.

## Is Picsart APK safe?

The official Picsart app from Google Play is safe. It's published by PicsArt, Inc., rated Teen, contains ads, and offers in-app purchases.

- **Subscription trials.** Free trials turn into paid subscriptions automatically. Check the price and cancel in Google Play if you don't want to keep it.
- **Photos and privacy.** Picsart processes some AI edits in the cloud. Read the privacy settings and avoid uploading images of ID cards or sensitive documents.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified Picsart APKs that claim to include paid features aren't from Picsart. They can carry malware or adware and may stop working at any time. APKPeer only lists the official app.


## Picsart on a budget

You can do a lot without paying. Stick to free filters and stickers, which are marked differently from premium items, and use the free templates as a starting point. Free AI tools have limits, so save them for photos that really need them, like removing a messy background from a product photo. Save your work as a draft before trying premium items, so you can remove them if you decide not to subscribe. If you only need Picsart for a single project, a short trial can be enough; just set a reminder to cancel it in Google Play before it renews.
