## What is Plants vs. Zombies 2?

Plants vs. Zombies 2 is a tower defense game from PopCap Games, published by EA. Zombies shuffle across your lawn in lanes, and you plant defences to stop them: Peashooters to shoot, Sunflowers to produce sun (the currency for planting), Wall-nuts to block, and hundreds of other plants with special powers. The sequel takes the action through time, with worlds like Ancient Egypt, Pirate Seas, the Wild West, the Far Future and more.

Plants vs. Zombies 2 has passed 100 million downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [strategy games](/category/strategy) we list.

## Key features

### Time-travel adventure

Travel through many themed worlds, each with its own zombies, plants and special mechanics, like rising tides in Big Wave Beach or minecarts in the Wild West.

### Hundreds of plants

Unlock and level up plants with different roles: attackers, defenders, sun producers and support. Plant Food gives any plant a powerful temporary boost.

### Challenges and events

Penny's Pursuit, Thymed events and seasonal events offer special levels with unique rules and rewards.

### Arena

Compete weekly for the highest score on a special level and climb leagues for prizes.

### Upgrades

Level up plants with seed packets and coins to make them stronger in every world.

## Tips for new players

- **Plant Sunflowers early.** More sun at the start means stronger defences later.
- **Fill the back first.** Put attackers at the back and blockers in front.
- **Save Plant Food.** Use it when a big wave or a tough zombie arrives.
- **Mix damage types.** Lobbed attacks hit zombies behind shields; fire and ice help in different situations.
- **Level up your favourites.** A few strong plants work better than many weak ones.
- **Replay levels for stars.** Star challenges teach new strategies and give rewards.
