## What is Pokémon TCG Pocket?

Pokémon TCG Pocket is a mobile game about collecting Pokémon cards and playing fast card battles. Every day you can open free booster packs with a satisfying swipe, building a digital collection that includes beautifully illustrated cards and "immersive" cards you can zoom into. When you want to play, you build a 20-card deck and battle the computer or other players in matches that take a few minutes.

The game is from The Pokémon Company, developed by Creatures Inc. and DeNA. It launched worldwide at the end of October 2024 and has passed 50 million downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [card games](/category/card) we list.

## Key features

### Free daily packs

Open free booster packs as the pack timer refills, about twice a day. Each new expansion brings themed packs based on different Pokémon.

### Wonder Pick

Wonder Pick lets you choose one card from another player's recently opened pack, shuffled face-down. It costs Wonder Stamina, which refills over time.

### Simplified battles

Decks have 20 cards. Instead of Energy cards, an Energy Zone gives you one Energy each turn automatically. First to score three points, earned by knocking out Pokémon, wins. Battles are short enough to play on a commute.

### Immersive and rare cards

Rare cards include alternative art, full art and immersive cards that turn into animated scenes when you open them.

### Trading and friends

Add friends, share your collection in a binder, and trade cards within the game's trading rules.

### Events and battles

Events offer missions, special Wonder Picks and promo cards. Ranked matches and solo battles reward you with items.

## Tips for collectors and players

- **Open packs on time.** Don't let your pack timer sit full; open packs as soon as they're ready.
- **Do daily missions.** They give Pack Hourglasses and Shop Tickets.
- **Use Wonder Pick on rare cards.** Spend Wonder Stamina on picks containing cards you really want.
- **Build around strong ex Pokémon.** Two copies of a strong ex Pokémon plus support cards make an easy starter deck.
- **Play solo battles first.** They teach the rules and give rewards before you try ranked play.
- **Save Shinedust.** It's used for trading and flairs, so don't spend it all at once.

## Is Pokémon TCG Pocket APK safe?

The official Pokémon TCG Pocket from Google Play is safe. It's published by The Pokémon Company, rated Everyone, has no ads, and offers in-app purchases from $0.99 to $99.99 per item.

- **Pack spending.** Opening packs is exciting, and buying extra packs to chase a rare card adds up fast. Set a budget and use purchase authentication in Google Play.
- **Account linking.** Link your game to a Nintendo Account or Google account so your collection isn't lost if you change phones.
- **Account selling and boosting.** Buying accounts with rare cards breaks the terms of service, and they're often recovered by the original owner.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified APKs and "free Poké Gold" generators aren't from The Pokémon Company. They can steal accounts and get them banned.
