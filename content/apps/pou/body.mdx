## Pou care tips

- Keep the four bars (hunger, health, energy, fun) green and Pou stays happy.
- Use **Sleep** when energy is low; Pou rests faster with the light off.
- Play the mini-game you're best at for steady coins.
- Don't overfeed; Pou gets sick and needs medicine.

## Pou for children: what parents should know

Pou is rated Everyone and has no chat. It shows ads, some of which may advertise other games, and it sells coins from $0.99 to $24.99. Turn on **purchase authentication** in Google Play settings, and consider playing in airplane mode to cut down ads for younger kids.
