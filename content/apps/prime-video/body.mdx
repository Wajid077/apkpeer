## What is Prime Video?

Prime Video is Amazon's streaming service, and the Prime Video APK is its Android app. It streams Amazon Originals such as *The Boys* and *Reacher*, films and series from other studios, and live sport in some countries. You can also rent or buy titles not included in your plan, and subscribe to extra channels.

## How to install Prime Video APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Prime Video**.
2. Check that the developer is **Amazon Mobile LLC**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app and sign in.

The official listing is [Prime Video on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amazon.avod.thirdpartyclient). Skip Prime Video APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Amazon Mobile LLC, they don't update, and they can carry malware or adware.

## Saving mobile data with Prime Video

Open **Settings → Stream & download** and choose a lower quality on mobile data. Download episodes on Wi-Fi before a trip, and turn on **Wi-Fi only** for downloads to avoid surprises on your bill.

## Is Prime Video APK safe?

The official Prime Video app on Google Play is safe and published by Amazon Mobile LLC. Unofficial "free Prime Video" APKs often carry malware and break Amazon's terms, and they can't access your account properly anyway. Use the Play version and your own Amazon login.

Prime Video has apps for smart TVs, Fire TV, Android TV, Google TV, game consoles and iPhone, and you can watch at primevideo.com in a browser. Downloads are only available in the mobile and tablet apps. Use the same Amazon account everywhere to keep your watch history in sync.
