## Tips for new players

- **Land at quieter spots** until you're comfortable with fights.
- **Always carry a level 2 helmet and vest** at least.
- **Adjust sensitivity and the HUD** in settings; it makes a bigger difference than skins.
- **Use the training ground** to learn recoil.
- **Play the zone:** reposition early rather than racing the circle.

## Game modes beyond battle royale

Besides the classic 100-player matches, PUBG Mobile has Arena modes such as Team Deathmatch for quick fights, Metro Royale with extraction-style looting, and rotating themed modes like this season's Arctic Chronicles. The new PvE mode lets you fight computer-controlled enemies, which is a lower-pressure way to learn weapons and maps.

## UC, Royale Pass and spending

UC is PUBG Mobile's premium currency, used for the Royale Pass, outfits, weapon finishes and crates. Nothing you buy changes weapon stats in classic matches, so skill decides who wins. The Royale Pass is the best value if you play regularly. Crate openings are random, so set a budget before you spend.

## PUBG Mobile and BGMI

In India, the game is Battlegrounds Mobile India (BGMI), a separate KRAFTON game with its own servers and account. Players outside India use PUBG Mobile.
