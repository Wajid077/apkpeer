## What is PUBG Mobile?

PUBG Mobile is the official mobile version of PUBG: Battlegrounds, the game that made battle royale a genre. Up to 100 players parachute onto a large map, loot weapons, armor and vehicles, and fight while the play zone shrinks. The last player or squad alive wins, earning the famous "Winner Winner Chicken Dinner."

It's developed by Tencent's Lightspeed Studios under license from Krafton, which owns PUBG, and published on Google Play by Level Infinite. It launched there in March 2018 and has passed a billion downloads.

Compared with lighter shooters, PUBG Mobile leans toward realism: bullet drop, recoil, attachments and big maps where positioning matters. Matches take longer, and the game asks more of your phone, but it remains one of the most-played [action games](/category/action) on Android.

## Key features

### Classic battle royale

The main mode drops up to 100 players onto maps such as Erangel, Miramar, Sanhok and Livik. Play solo, duo or in squads of four. Choose your landing spot, gear up, and use vehicles to rotate with the zone.

### Arcade and Team Deathmatch

For quicker games, Arcade modes and Team Deathmatch offer short, action-focused matches on smaller maps. They're also good for practising aim without the long build-up of a full battle royale.

### Themed modes

Limited-time modes, like Midnight Hunters in this update, change the rules with special abilities, enemies or map changes. They rotate with each major version. Check the events area in the game lobby to see how long each mode stays available before it rotates out.

### Royale Pass and cosmetics

Each season has a Royale Pass with free and paid tracks that unlock outfits, weapon skins and emotes. Items are cosmetic and don't change weapon damage.

### Customizable controls and graphics

Move and resize every on-screen button, enable gyroscope aiming, and pick graphics and frame-rate settings to balance looks and smoothness on your phone.

## Tips for new players

Land somewhere quieter than the big named towns on your first games, so you have time to loot. Learn the recoil of one or two rifles in Training Grounds before switching between many weapons. Keep an eye on the zone timer and move early rather than late. And turn on the sound: footsteps and gunshots tell you a lot about where enemies are, especially with headphones.

## Storage and performance

PUBG Mobile is one of the bigger games on Android once its resource packs are downloaded. In the game's settings you can choose which packs to keep, so delete maps and modes you never play. Lower the graphics quality or frame rate if your phone heats up or the battery drains quickly; smoother frames usually matter more than detail in a shooter.
