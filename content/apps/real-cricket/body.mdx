## What is Real Cricket?

Real Cricket is a 3D cricket game for Android, and one of the most popular in India and other cricket-loving countries. You bat with timing-based shots, bowl with a choice of deliveries, set fields, and play full matches in formats from T20 to Test, against the computer or other players online.

It was created by Nautilus Mobile, an Indian studio, and used to include the year in its name, so many people still search for it as Real Cricket 24. On Google Play it's now listed as "Real Cricket™: Cricket Games" and published by KRAFTON, Inc., the company behind PUBG. This listing has been on Play since August 2022 and has more than 50 million downloads.

If you're looking for the official version of the game you knew as Real Cricket 24, this is it. It's one of the leading cricket titles among the [sports games](/category/sports) on Android.

## Key features

### Realistic batting and bowling

Batting uses timing and shot selection: choose a direction and shot type, and time your tap to the ball. Bowlers pick pace or spin, line and length, and variations. Getting the timing right is the core skill.

### Online multiplayer

Play real-time matches against other players, and join online leagues. Online play is the heart of the game, with rankings and rewards.

### Tournaments and formats

Play tournaments and formats including T20, ODI and Test matches, against the computer when you want something offline-style and slower-paced.

### Commentary and presentation

Matches have TV-style camera angles, replays and commentary, which make big moments feel like a broadcast.

### Teams and players

Pick from international and franchise-style teams, manage your lineup, and build your squad over time.

## Tips for new players

Start with easier difficulty against the computer to learn shot timing before playing online. Rotate the strike with ones and twos instead of trying a boundary every ball, especially early in an innings. When bowling, mix pace and length: the same delivery every ball gets punished. And watch replays of your dismissals to see whether you were early or late.

## Cricket formats explained

| Format | Overs per side | What it's like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T20 | 20 | Fast and aggressive; boundaries matter most |
| ODI | 50 | Balanced; you build an innings, then accelerate |
| Test | Two innings each, no over limit | Patient; taking wickets matters more than run rate |

In a mobile game, matches are shortened to fit your time, but the same thinking applies: attack in T20, pace yourself in ODIs, and protect your wicket in Tests.

## Getting the most from online play

Online matches pair you with players of a similar level, so expect early games to be closer than later ones. Learn one batting approach that works for you before trying fancy shots, and practise bowling variations offline first. A stable Wi-Fi connection matters: lag in a real-time match can cost you a wicket.
