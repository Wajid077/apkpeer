## What is Remini?

Remini is an AI photo enhancer. It takes blurry, pixelated, damaged or old photos and turns them into sharper, clearer, higher-resolution images, with particularly impressive results on faces. It can also colourise black-and-white photos, enhance videos, and generate AI portraits of you in different styles from a set of selfies.

Remini is owned by Bending Spoons, the Milan-based company behind several popular apps. It has passed 500 million downloads on Google Play since 2019, and it's one of the [photo editor apps](/category/photo-editors) we list.

## Key features

### Photo enhancement

Pick a photo and Remini sharpens details, reduces blur and noise, and increases resolution. It's especially good with faces in old family photos, group shots taken from far away and screenshots of video calls.

### Restore old photos

Scan or photograph an old print, and Remini repairs scratches, fades and blur. Colourise turns black-and-white photos into colour.

### AI photos

Upload several selfies and Remini generates portraits of you in different styles and settings, such as professional headshots or creative themes.

### Video enhancement

Enhance short videos to make them sharper and clearer, useful for old phone clips.

### Editing tools

Adjust the strength of enhancements, compare before and after, and use extra tools such as background blur and retouching.

## How to install Remini APK on Android

Install [Remini from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bigwinepot.nwdn.international).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 7.0 or newer.
2. Open Google Play, search for Remini, and confirm the developer is Bending Spoons.
3. Tap **Install** and open the app.
4. Allow photo access when asked. On recent Android versions, you can allow access to selected photos only.

Bending Spoons doesn't publish its own APK. Files on other sites aren't official.

## Free vs paid Remini

The free version lets you enhance a limited number of photos each day and shows ads, often before you can save a result. Paid subscriptions remove ads, raise or remove the daily limit, and unlock AI photo packs and video enhancement. Remini commonly offers weekly subscriptions, which add up quickly. Check the exact price in the subscription screen, and cancel trials under **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions** if you don't want to continue.

## Getting the best results

- **Start with the best copy.** A scan or a well-lit photo of a print enhances better than a blurry photo of a photo.
- **Crop first.** Crop to the faces or area you care about before enhancing.
- **Adjust the strength.** Strong enhancement can make faces look plastic; a lighter touch often looks more natural.
- **Keep the original.** Always save the enhanced version as a copy, and keep the original for your family archive.
- **Check details.** AI can invent details, such as teeth, eyes or text, that weren't in the original. Compare carefully before sharing.

## Is Remini APK safe?

The official Remini app from Google Play is safe to install. It's published by Bending Spoons, rated Everyone, contains ads, and offers in-app purchases.

Things to keep in mind:

- **Your photos go to the cloud.** Processing happens on Bending Spoons' servers, so read the privacy policy, especially for AI photos that use multiple selfies.
- **Other people's faces.** Get permission before enhancing or generating images of friends and family.
- **Subscriptions.** Weekly plans renew automatically. Set a reminder to cancel if you only need a few edits.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified "pro" Remini APKs aren't from Bending Spoons, can't use Remini's servers legitimately, and are a common source of malware. APKPeer only lists the official app.


## Restoring a family photo step by step

1. Take the print out of its frame and lay it flat in soft daylight, avoiding glare.
2. Photograph or scan it straight on, filling the frame.
3. Crop to the photo itself in your gallery.
4. Enhance it in Remini, compare before and after, and adjust the strength if faces look too smooth.
5. Use Colourise if it's black and white, but keep a black-and-white copy too.
6. Save the result and share it with family, noting that it's an AI-enhanced restoration rather than the original.
