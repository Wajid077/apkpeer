## What is Roblox?

Roblox is an online platform where players make and play games, called experiences. The Roblox APK installs the official Android app, where you can jump into obbies, role-play towns, tycoons, simulators, horror games and shooters, customise an avatar, and play with friends across phone, PC, Xbox and PlayStation.

It's one of the [adventure games](/category/adventure) we cover, alongside <AppLink slug="minecraft">Minecraft</AppLink>.

## How to install Roblox APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone.
2. Search for **Roblox** and check that the developer is **Roblox Corporation**.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. Open the game and link it to an account in settings, so your progress is saved if you change phones.

The official listing is [Roblox on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.roblox.client). Skip Roblox APK files on other download sites: they aren't from Roblox Corporation, they can carry malware, and online games ban accounts that use altered files.

### Roblox for Android: requirements

Android 8.0 or newer and a stable connection. Big experiences run best on phones with at least 4 GB of RAM.

## Parental controls for kids

1. Create the child's account with their real birthday.
2. In **Settings → Parental Controls**, link a parent account.
3. Set **content maturity** and **chat** limits to suit their age.
4. Set a **monthly spending limit** for Robux.
5. Turn on Google Play's purchase authentication too.

## Is Roblox APK safe?

The official Roblox app from Google Play is safe to install. It's published by Roblox Corporation and has in-app purchases for Robux.

- **Free Robux** websites, apps and videos are scams that steal accounts.
- **Altered Roblox APKs and exploit scripts** aren't from Roblox; they carry malware and lead to bans.
- **Strangers.** Talk to kids about not sharing personal information and reporting anyone who makes them uncomfortable.

## Robux and Premium explained

Robux is bought in the app through Google Play, or with Roblox gift cards. It's spent on avatar items and on game passes inside experiences, which can unlock abilities, areas or items. Roblox Premium is a monthly subscription that gives a monthly Robux allowance and lets players trade items. Set a spending limit in Parental Controls, because children can spend Robux quickly in experiences with lots of upgrades.

## Finding good experiences

Use the Charts and the search bar, read the experience's description and age guidance, and check its rating before playing. Popular genres include obbies (obstacle courses), tycoons, simulators, role-play towns and team shooters. Favourite the ones you like so they're easy to find again.

## Can you play Roblox on other devices?

Yes. Your Roblox account works on Windows, Mac, Xbox, PlayStation, iPhone and Meta Quest, so progress and Robux follow you between devices.

## Final thoughts

The Roblox APK from Google Play is the official way to play Roblox on Android. For children, set up parental controls before the first game and keep Robux spending capped.
