## What is Roblox?

Roblox isn't one game. It's a platform made by Roblox Corporation where millions of games, called experiences, are built by players and small studios, then played by everyone else for free. One minute you're running an obby (obstacle course), the next you're role-playing in a city, defending a tower or surviving a horror map.

You keep the same avatar, friends list and currency across all of them. That currency is Robux, which you spend on avatar clothing, accessories and in-game extras. Creators earn Robux when people buy what they've made, and some turn it into real income.

The Android app does everything the computer version does, apart from building experiences, which happens in Roblox Studio on a computer. Your account, friends and purchases sync across phone, tablet, PC and console. If you like open-ended [adventure games](/category/adventure), Roblox is the biggest of them all.

## Key features

### Millions of free experiences

The **Home** and **Charts** tabs show what's popular, and search finds any experience by name. Each one has a page with its description, player count, content maturity label and a **Play** button. Almost all of them are free.

### Your avatar, everywhere

Customize your avatar in the **Avatar** tab with free and paid items from the Marketplace. The avatar follows you into most experiences, although some games use their own characters.

### Friends, chat and parties

Add friends, join the same server, and chat in text or (for verified users aged 13 and older) by voice. Party features let you move between experiences as a group.

### Parental controls

Parents can link their own Roblox account to a child's account and manage it from there: set content maturity limits, turn chat off or restrict it, set monthly spending limits, and see screen time. Settings are under **Settings → Parental Controls**.

### Cross-play

Roblox runs on phones, tablets, PCs, Macs, Xbox and PlayStation, and players on all of them meet in the same servers. Progress and Robux are tied to your account, not the device.

## Tips for smoother play on older phones

Roblox experiences vary a lot in how demanding they are. If a game stutters, open the Roblox menu during play, go to **Settings**, switch **Graphics Mode** to Manual and lower the **Graphics Quality** slider. Closing other apps and keeping a few gigabytes of storage free also helps, since Roblox caches game assets as you play.

## How to install Roblox APK on Android

Get [Roblox from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.roblox.client) if you can: it updates often, and Play handles that for you. If your device has no Play Store, install with care.

1. Check that your device runs Android 8.0 or newer.
2. Download Roblox only from Google Play or links on roblox.com. Never install files promising free Robux or extra features.
3. If you downloaded the file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We currently link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for Roblox.
4. Open the APK and allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks.
5. Tap **Install**, then open Roblox and log in or sign up.
6. Turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

Without Play Store, you'll have to repeat this when Roblox updates, because the app stops connecting when it's too far out of date.

## Is Roblox APK safe?

The official Roblox APK is safe. It's published by Roblox Corporation on Google Play, where it has more than a billion installs, and it doesn't contain ads.

The risks on Roblox are about people and scams, not the app. Players chat with strangers, so younger kids should use an account with parental controls and restricted chat. Google Play rates Roblox Teen and labels it with in-game purchases that include random items.

Scams are the biggest problem. "Free Robux" sites, Discord links and APKs are designed to steal accounts or install malware. Roblox never gives away Robux through third-party sites.

You'll also see unofficial "Roblox APK" files and cheat scripts that promise to fly, teleport or auto-farm. They're not made by Roblox. Many carry malware or steal your login, and Roblox's anti-cheat system detects them and bans accounts. APKPeer only lists the official app.
