## What is Rope Hero: Vice Town?

Rope Hero: Vice Town is an open-world action game from Naxeex Action & RPG Games. You play a blue-suited superhero in a city overrun by gangs. Your signature move is the super rope: shoot it at a building and swing across the rooftops, then drop down to fight, drive cars, and take on missions to free the city's districts from criminals.

It's in the spirit of console open-world games, with a story campaign, side missions, vehicles, weapons and character upgrades, all free on a phone. It has been on Google Play since July 2016, has more than 100 million downloads, and is still updated.

Google Play rates it Mature 17+ for intense violence and blood, so it's aimed at older players. It's one of the most-downloaded open-world [action games](/category/action) on Android.

## Key features

### Swing through the city with the super rope

The rope is how you get around. Aim at a building and swing, chaining swings to cross the city quickly, or pull yourself up to rooftops. It's the most fun part of the game, and faster than driving once you get the hang of it.

### Open world and story missions

The city is split into districts controlled by gangs. Story missions push the plot forward, and side missions and random events give you more to do. Freeing districts opens up more of the map.

### Vehicles and weapons

Drive cars, bikes and other vehicles, and use a range of melee weapons and guns against gangs. Your hero also has special abilities that you unlock and upgrade as you progress.

### Upgrade your hero

Earn money and experience to improve your hero's health, strength and abilities, and buy new suits and gear from the in-game shops. Upgrades make later missions much easier, so it pays to spend money as soon as you earn it.

### Plays offline

Rope Hero is mostly a single-player game, so you can play much of it without a connection, which is handy on a commute. Ads and some rewards need the internet.

## Tips for getting started

Practise swinging in a quiet part of the city before taking on missions; it's the best way to escape a fight that's going badly. Spend your first upgrades on health and strength. Use vehicles to travel long distances on the ground, and the rope for climbing and crossing rooftops. And save often by completing missions, since a big gang fight can go wrong quickly.

## Who Rope Hero is for

Rope Hero: Vice Town suits players who like open-world freedom more than tight missions: swinging around the city, causing chaos and upgrading a hero at their own pace. It's a single-player game, so there's no pressure from other players. Because of its Mature 17+ rating, it's aimed at older teens and adults. It's also a big, detailed game, so it runs best on a reasonably recent phone with free storage.
