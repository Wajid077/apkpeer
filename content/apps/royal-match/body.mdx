## What is Royal Match?

Royal Match is a match-3 puzzle game. Swap neighbouring tiles to line up three or more of the same colour, clear obstacles like boxes, curtains and potions, and finish each level's goals within a limited number of moves. Each level you complete earns stars, which you spend to restore and decorate rooms in King Robert's castle.

The game is made by Dream Games, a studio based in Istanbul. It launched on Google Play in February 2021 and has passed 100 million downloads with a 4.6-star rating. It's one of the [puzzle games](/category/puzzle) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="candy-crush-saga">Candy Crush Saga</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Match-3 levels

Each level has a goal, such as collecting a number of items or clearing all the obstacles, and a move limit. Matching four or five tiles creates power-ups: rockets clear a row or column, TNT blasts an area, propellers fly to a target, and light balls clear every tile of one colour.

### Restore the castle

Stars from finished levels pay for tasks that restore the castle area by area. Each area tells a short, light-hearted story with King Robert.

### Boosters

Pre-level boosters give you a head start, and in-level boosters like the Royal Hammer or Arrow help when you're one move away. You earn them from events and chests, or buy them with coins.

### Events and King's Cup

Regular events give extra rewards, and competitions like King's Cup rank you against other players. The Royal Pass adds a reward track you progress through by playing.

### Teams

Join a team to ask for and give lives, chat, and take part in team events.

## Tips for beating hard levels

- **Look before you swipe.** Scan the board for matches that create power-ups instead of taking the first match you see.
- **Combine power-ups.** Swapping two power-ups together creates a much stronger effect, such as a rocket plus TNT clearing three rows and columns.
- **Work from the bottom.** Matches near the bottom cause cascades that make free matches above.
- **Focus on the goal.** Ignore tiles that don't help you reach the objective.
- **Save boosters for super hard levels.** Levels marked hard or super hard are where pre-level boosters pay off.
- **Ask your team for lives** instead of buying them.
