## What is Shadow Fight 2?

Shadow Fight 2 is a fighting game with an RPG twist. Fighters appear as black silhouettes, but their movements are animated with impressive realism, using martial arts, weapons and magic. You play a warrior who opened the Gate of Shadows, battling through seven demon bosses and their bodyguards to close it again. Between fights, you buy and upgrade weapons, armour and magic.

Made by Nekki, the game came to Google Play in July 2014 and has passed 100 million downloads with a 4.6-star rating. It's one of the [action games](/category/action) we list.

## Key features

### Physics-based fighting

Punches, kicks, combos and weapon attacks use physics-based animation, so every fight feels different. Timing and distance matter more than button mashing.

### Weapons and armour

Collect swords, nunchaku, axes, staves and more, plus helmets, armour and ranged weapons. Upgrade them to keep up with tougher opponents.

### Magic

Charge magic during a fight by landing hits, then release it for big damage.

### Bosses and bodyguards

Each act ends with a demon boss and their bodyguards, each with a unique style.

### Tournaments, duels and survival

Beyond the story, take on tournaments, challenges, duels and survival mode for rewards.

## Tips for beating tough opponents

- **Don't spam attacks.** Block, then counter-attack when your opponent misses.
- **Learn combos.** Direction plus punch or kick creates different moves.
- **Upgrade one weapon.** A fully upgraded weapon beats several weak ones.
- **Use ranged weapons** to open fights and interrupt opponents.
- **Save magic for openings.** Use it when your opponent can't block.
- **Replay earlier fights** to earn coins for upgrades.

The free Shadow Fight 2 uses an energy system: each fight costs energy, which refills over time, and gems speed things up. It also shows ads. Shadow Fight 2 Special Edition is a separate paid app from Nekki on Google Play. It removes energy and ads, gives you more in-game rewards, and adds an extra story chapter. Progress doesn't transfer between the two apps, so decide early if you think you'll play a lot. If you just want to try the game, the free version is a good start.

## Understanding the controls

The left side of the screen moves your fighter; the right side has punch and kick buttons, plus buttons for ranged weapons and magic once unlocked. Holding a direction while attacking creates different moves: forward and punch for a lunging strike, down and kick for a sweep, and so on. Blocking happens automatically when you move away from your opponent. Jumping over attacks and rolling are useful escapes. Practise combos against easier opponents before facing bosses.
