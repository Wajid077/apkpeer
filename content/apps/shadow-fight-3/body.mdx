## Combat tips

- **Block, then counter:** most enemies leave an opening after a combo.
- **Save Shadow Form** for when the enemy is low or you're in trouble.
- **Use ranged weapons** to interrupt charging opponents.
- **Upgrade one set** of gear rather than spreading resources.
