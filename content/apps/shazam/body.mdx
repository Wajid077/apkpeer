## What is Shazam?

Shazam is the app that tells you what song is playing. Hear something in a café, a shop or a video, tap the big Shazam button, and a few seconds later you see the title and artist, with lyrics, the music video and links to play it on a streaming service. It's listed on Google Play as "Shazam: Find Music & Concerts" and published by Apple Inc., which bought Shazam in 2018.

Shazam works by recording a short sample and matching its "fingerprint" against a huge database of songs. That's why it works through background noise and even on songs you only hear a few seconds of.

It's free on Android, with no ads and no in-app purchases, and has more than 500 million downloads on Google Play. It's one of the most useful [music and audio apps](/category/music-audio) to keep on your phone, even if you only open it now and then.

## Key features

### Identify songs in seconds

Open the app and tap the Shazam button, and it listens and shows a match, usually within a few seconds. Results include the song, artist and album, plus lyrics and the music video where available.

### Pop-up Shazam

Pop-up Shazam lets you identify music playing in other apps on your phone, such as a song in a video or social media post, without leaving that app. Turn it on in settings, then tap the floating button or notification.

### Auto Shazam

Auto Shazam keeps identifying songs in the background, which is handy at a party or while watching a film. It runs until you turn it off, and Android shows a notification while it's active.

### Your library

Every song you Shazam is saved to your library, so you can find that song you heard last week, along with when you heard it. With an account, your library is backed up and syncs across devices.

### Concerts

Shazam shows upcoming concerts for artists you've Shazamed, and can suggest shows near you if you allow location access.

## Tips for better results

Hold your phone closer to the speaker in a loud room, and give Shazam a few seconds of the song rather than the first second. If you're offline, Shazam anyway: it saves the recording and identifies the song when you reconnect. And add the Shazam widget to your home screen, so you can start it before the song ends.

## Shazam and your music apps

Shazam can connect to music services so the songs you identify are easy to play. Each result has buttons to open the song in a streaming app, and in settings you can link Spotify or Apple Music so your Shazams are added to a playlist automatically. That turns Shazam into a running list of everything you've discovered, ready to listen to properly later.

## How to install Shazam APK on Android

Install [Shazam from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shazam.android) and check that the developer is Apple Inc.

1. Open Google Play and search for Shazam, or use the link above.
2. Tap **Install**, open Shazam and allow microphone access when you first tap the button.
3. If your phone has no Play Store, any Shazam APK comes from a third-party mirror. Android will only install it over an existing Shazam if it's signed by Apple.
4. If you downloaded a file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for Shazam.
5. If you installed from a file, turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is Shazam APK safe?

The official Shazam app is safe. It's published by Apple Inc. on Google Play and has no ads or in-app purchases. Play rates it Teen, with a note that content varies, because results can include lyrics and videos with explicit content.

Shazam needs the microphone, but it only listens when you tap the button or while Auto Shazam is switched on. Location is optional and only used for concert suggestions. You can review both under **Settings → Apps → Shazam → Permissions**.

Since Shazam is already completely free, there's no reason to download a "Pro" or modified Shazam from another site. Those files aren't from Apple, and an unofficial app with microphone access is a real privacy risk. APKPeer only links to the official app.
