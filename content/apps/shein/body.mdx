## What is SHEIN?

SHEIN is an online retailer focused on fast fashion. Its app sells women's, men's and children's clothing, shoes, accessories, beauty products, and home, pet and electronics items, with thousands of new styles added every day. Prices are low, and promotions, points and coupons are a big part of the experience.

On Google Play, the app is published by Roadget Business PTE. LTD., the Singapore company that operates SHEIN. It has passed 1 billion downloads, and it's one of the [shopping apps](/category/shopping) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="temu">Temu</AppLink>.

## Tips for shopping on SHEIN

- **Measure yourself.** Compare your measurements with each item's size chart, not just the size label.
- **Read reviews with photos.** They show real colour, fabric and fit.
- **Check fabric.** The product details list materials; natural fabrics are often more comfortable.
- **Watch the total.** Shipping and import fees can change the final price.
- **Buy less, choose better.** Fast fashion is cheap but wears out quickly; choosing carefully saves money and waste.

## Is SHEIN APK safe?

The official SHEIN app is a genuine shopping app. It's published by Roadget Business PTE. LTD. on Google Play, rated Teen, and Play shows no ads or in-app purchases.

- **Ethics.** SHEIN has faced criticism over labour conditions in its supply chain and the environmental impact of fast fashion. Consider this when deciding how much to buy.
- **Product safety.** Some regulators have found problems in low-cost products sold online. Take care with children's items and cosmetics.
- **Scams.** Messages offering free SHEIN gift cards or "winner" prizes are phishing. Log in only through the app.
- **Privacy.** Review app permissions and notification settings, and use a strong unique password.


## Making returns easy

Before you order, check whether each item can be returned; the product page and the return policy in the app list exceptions such as underwear, swimwear and some jewellery. When something doesn't fit, start a return from the order page within the return window, choose a drop-off or pickup option, and pack the items with their tags. Refunds go back to your original payment method or as store credit, depending on what you choose. Take photos of anything damaged or wrong before you send it back, in case you need to contact support.

## Earning and using points

SHEIN gives points for actions such as daily check-ins, writing reviews with photos, and completing orders. Points can be used to reduce the price of future orders, within limits set in the app. Reviews with photos and measurements earn more points and help other shoppers. Points expire after a set time, so check the balance in your account before they run out. Coupons and flash sales stack in some cases, so compare the final price at checkout before deciding to wait for a better deal.
