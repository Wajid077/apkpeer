## What is Snapchat?

Snapchat is a camera and messaging app from Snap Inc. It opens straight to the camera, because the main thing you do is take a photo or video, called a Snap, and send it to friends. By default, Snaps disappear once they've been opened, which gives Snapchat a more casual, in-the-moment feel than apps where everything stays on a profile forever.

Around that core, Snapchat has added Stories (Snaps shared with all your friends for 24 hours), chat, voice and video calls, the Snap Map for seeing where friends are, and Spotlight, a feed of short videos from the wider community. Lenses, the augmented-reality filters that put dog ears on your face or change the scene around you, are still one of its signatures.

Snapchat has been on Google Play since October 2012 and has more than a billion downloads. It's one of the [social media apps](/category/social) most popular with teenagers and young adults.

## Key features

### Snaps and Lenses

Tap the shutter to take a photo or hold it to record video, then add text, drawings, stickers or a Lens before sending. Lenses range from silly face filters to world effects that change your surroundings. Swipe on the camera screen to browse them.

### Chat and calls

Swipe right from the camera to open chats. Messages disappear after viewing by default, though you can change how long chats stay. You can also make voice and video calls, one-to-one or in groups.

### Stories and Spotlight

Post Snaps to your Story so all your friends can see them for 24 hours. Spotlight is a public feed of short videos from across Snapchat, similar to Reels or TikTok.

### Snap Map

Pinch the camera screen to open the map and see where friends who share their location are. Ghost Mode hides your location from everyone, and you can share with only selected friends instead.

### My AI

My AI is Snapchat's built-in chatbot, which appears in your chat list. You can ask it questions or for suggestions. Treat it like any AI chatbot: don't share private details with it, and remember its answers can be wrong.

### Streaks and memories

Streaks count how many days in a row you and a friend have sent each other Snaps. Memories saves Snaps you choose to keep, privately, so you can look back at them or share them later.

## Privacy settings to check

Snapchat is designed for sharing, so a few settings are worth a look under your profile's gear icon:

1. **Contact Me:** choose whether anyone or only friends can message you.
2. **See My Location:** turn on Ghost Mode, or share only with chosen friends.
3. **View My Story:** limit Stories to friends or a custom list.
4. **Two-factor authentication:** turn it on to protect your account.
5. **Family Center:** parents and teens can link accounts so parents see who their teen talks to, without seeing the messages.

## Is Snapchat APK safe?

The official Snapchat app is safe to install. It's published by Snap Inc on Google Play and rated Teen, with users required to be at least 13. Play marks it as containing ads and in-app purchases, from $0.49 to $399.99 per item, mostly for Snapchat+.

"Disappearing" doesn't mean private. Anyone can screenshot a Snap or film their screen, and Snapchat can only tell you a screenshot happened. Location sharing on the Snap Map is the setting parents most often want to check. Turning on Ghost Mode keeps your location hidden.

Snapchat actively detects third-party and modified versions of its app, and accounts using them get locked or banned. Those apps can also steal your login. APKPeer only links to the official app.
