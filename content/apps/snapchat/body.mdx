## Tips for Snapchat privacy

- **Turn on Ghost Mode** on Snap Map so friends can't see where you are.
- **Set Contact Me** to My Friends.
- **Use Family Center** if you're a parent of a teen.
- **Export important Memories** to your gallery as a backup.
- **Turn on two-factor authentication** in settings.

## Snapchat Streaks explained

A Snapstreak counts how many days in a row you and a friend have sent each other Snaps (chats don't count). A fire emoji and number appear next to the friend's name. If one of you misses a day, the streak ends, though Snapchat sometimes lets you restore a streak shortly afterwards. Streaks are a big reason teens open Snapchat daily, so parents may want to talk about them. You can turn streak reminders off in notification settings.
