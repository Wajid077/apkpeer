## What is Snapseed?

Snapseed is a free photo editor from Google, and one of the most capable you can get on a phone without paying. It started life at Nik Software, the company Google bought in 2012 (which is why its package name is still `com.niksoftware.snapseed`), and has been on Google Play since December 2012 with more than 100 million downloads.

It's aimed at people who care about how their photos look rather than at quick filters. You get precise tools such as curves, white balance, selective adjustments, healing, perspective correction and RAW editing, all controlled with simple swipes. You can still apply a one-tap look if you want to, but the real strength is fine control.

There are no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases: every tool is free. That makes it stand out among [photo and design apps](/category/photo-editors), and a natural first choice if you want to edit photos properly on Android.

## Key features

### Tune Image and Details

Start with **Tune Image** to set brightness, contrast, saturation, highlights, shadows and warmth. Swipe up and down to pick a setting, left and right to change it. **Details** sharpens and adds structure for crisper textures.

### Curves and White Balance

**Curves** gives precise control over tones and individual color channels, like in desktop editors. **White Balance** fixes color casts from indoor lighting with a temperature slider or an eyedropper.

### Selective and Brush

**Selective** lets you tap a point on the photo and change brightness, contrast or saturation in just that area. **Brush** paints adjustments onto specific parts of the image, such as brightening a face.

### Healing and Perspective

**Healing** removes small distractions like spots or a stray object by painting over them. **Perspective** straightens buildings and tilted horizons.

### Non-destructive edit stack

Every edit is saved as a step you can go back to. Open the edit history to change or remove an earlier adjustment without redoing everything after it, and copy a set of edits to apply to other photos.

## Tips for better edits

Fix exposure and white balance first, then work on color and contrast. Use **Selective** for small corrections instead of pushing global sliders too far. Zoom in before applying **Details** to check you're not adding noise. And export at full size: Snapseed keeps the original resolution, so there's no reason to shrink a photo before sharing unless the app you're sharing to requires it.

## How to install Snapseed APK on Android

Install [Snapseed from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.niksoftware.snapseed). Google publishes Snapseed only through Play, so there's no separate official APK.

1. Open Google Play, search for Snapseed, and check that the developer is Google LLC.
2. Tap **Install** and open the app. Allow access to your photos so you can edit them.
3. If your phone has no Play Store, any Snapseed APK comes from a third-party mirror. Android will only install it over an existing Snapseed if it's signed by Google.
4. If you downloaded a file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for Snapseed.
5. If you installed from a file, turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is Snapseed APK safe?

The official Snapseed app is safe. It's published by Google LLC on Google Play, rated Everyone, and has no ads or in-app purchases. It needs access to your photos to edit them, and edits happen on your phone.

Because Snapseed is already completely free, there's no reason to download a "Pro" or "premium" Snapseed from another site. Those files aren't from Google, and a modified photo app with access to your whole gallery is a real privacy risk. APKPeer only links to the official app.
