## What is Sonic Dash?

Sonic Dash is SEGA's endless runner starring Sonic the Hedgehog. The Sonic Dash APK installs it on Android, where the current Play title is **Sonic Dash Run**: you race through loops and ramps at top speed, swipe to jump, roll and switch lanes, collect rings, and battle classic bosses like Dr. Eggman.

## How to install Sonic Dash APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Sonic Dash**.
2. Check that the developer is **SEGA**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the game.
4. Link the game to an account in its settings so your progress is saved if you change phones.

The official listing is [Sonic Dash on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sega.sonicdash). Avoid Sonic Dash APK files from other download sites: they aren't from SEGA, they can carry malware, and online games ban accounts that use altered files.

## Tips for longer runs

- **Collect rings** to survive hits; with zero rings, one hit ends the run.
- **Use the Dash meter** to speed through danger.
- **Upgrade characters** and use buddies for bonuses.
- **Complete missions** to raise your score multiplier.

## Rings, red rings and characters

Gold rings protect you and pay for upgrades; red rings are the premium currency for characters and revives. Events and daily challenges give red rings for free over time. Unlock characters you enjoy playing rather than collecting everything.
