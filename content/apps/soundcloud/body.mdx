SoundCloud is a music platform built around creators. The SoundCloud APK installs its Android app, where you stream tracks, remixes, DJ mixes and podcasts from independent artists and big names, follow creators, repost, comment on waveforms and upload your own music.

## How to install SoundCloud APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **SoundCloud**.
2. Check that the developer is **SoundCloud**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app.

The official listing is [SoundCloud on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.soundcloud.android). Avoid SoundCloud APK files from other download sites: they aren't from SoundCloud and can carry malware or adware.

## Is SoundCloud APK safe?

The official SoundCloud app from Google Play is safe. It's published by SoundCloud, rated Teen, contains ads and offers in-app purchases. Be careful with links in comments or messages promising promotion or followers; they're often scams.

## SoundCloud for artists

Artists can upload tracks from the app or website, add artwork and descriptions, and share private links before release. Free accounts have a limited upload allowance, while Artist plans add more upload time, detailed stats, scheduling and distribution to other streaming services. Real followers come from regular uploads, collaborations and sharing tracks in your community, not from paid "promotion" offers that arrive in your messages.

## SoundCloud on other devices

SoundCloud works on the web, iPhone, Android TV and smart speakers, and your likes and playlists sync across them.

## Free vs Go+

The free plan plays most tracks with ads. Go+ removes ads, unlocks offline listening and gives access to the full catalogue, including major-label releases that are limited to previews for free listeners.

## Who should use SoundCloud

It's the best app for remixes, DJ sets, bedroom producers and early tracks from artists before they're on other services.

The SoundCloud APK from Google Play is the best app on Android for discovering independent music, remixes and DJ mixes, and for sharing your own tracks.
