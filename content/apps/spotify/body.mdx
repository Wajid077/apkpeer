### Personalised mixes

Discover Weekly, Release Radar, Daily Mixes and Daylist refresh based on what you play.

Follow shows, including video podcasts, and listen to audiobooks where they're offered.

### Jam

Listen together: friends add songs to a shared queue in real time.

### Spotify Connect

Control playback on speakers, TVs and consoles from your phone.

### Offline listening (Premium)

Download music and podcasts to play without a connection.

## How to install Spotify APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone.
2. Search for **Spotify** and check that the developer is **Spotify AB**.
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to finish.
4. Open the app and sign in, or create an account.

The official listing is [Spotify on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spotify.music). Avoid Spotify APK files on other download sites: they aren't from Spotify AB and can carry malware or steal your login.

### Spotify for Android TV and cars

Spotify has apps for Android TV, Wear OS and Android Auto, all installed from Google Play.

## Tips for Spotify on Android

- **Set data saver** for mobile networks in **Settings → Data saving and offline**.
- **Hide songs** you're tired of to train recommendations.
- **Use a private session** so guest listening doesn't change your mixes.
- **Download on Wi-Fi only** with Premium.

## Is Spotify APK safe?

The official Spotify app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Spotify AB and contains ads on the free tier. Unofficial Spotify copies that unlock Premium for free aren't from Spotify; they break its terms, can carry malware, and Spotify regularly suspends accounts that use them.

## Free vs Premium on Android

The free tier plays music with ads and has playback limits on mobile, such as a limited number of skips. Spotify Premium removes ads, lets you play any song in any order, skip freely, download music for offline listening and stream at higher quality. Individual, Duo, Family and Student plans exist in many countries, and prices vary by country. Premium bought inside the Android app is billed through Google Play or Spotify's own checkout, depending on your region.

## Moving your music to Spotify

Coming from another service? Rebuild your favourites by searching and saving songs, or use a playlist transfer tool. Follow your favourite artists and like songs for a week, and Spotify's recommendations quickly catch up with your taste.

## Final thoughts

The Spotify APK from Google Play is the official way to stream Spotify on Android. Start on the free tier, set data saver, and consider Premium if you want offline listening and no ads.
