## What is Spotify?

Spotify is a music and podcast streaming service from Spotify AB, the Swedish company that popularized streaming instead of buying songs. The Android app, listed on Google Play as "Spotify: Music and Podcasts," gives you a huge catalogue of songs, albums, podcasts and audiobooks in some regions, plus playlists made for you based on what you listen to.

There are two ways to use it. The free tier is supported by ads and has some limits on how you can play music. Premium, a paid subscription, removes ads, lets you play anything in any order and download music to listen offline. Both use the same app.

Spotify has been on Google Play since May 2014 and has more than a billion downloads. It's the most popular of the [music and audio apps](/category/music-audio), and for many people the free tier is enough.

## Key features

### Music and podcasts in one app

Search for any artist, album, song or podcast, follow artists to see their new releases, and save what you like to **Your Library**. Podcasts sit next to music, so you don't need a separate podcast app.

### Personalized playlists

Spotify builds playlists for you, such as Discover Weekly and Daily Mixes, based on your listening. The more you listen, the better the recommendations get.

### Spotify Connect

Play Spotify on speakers, TVs, game consoles and cars from your phone. Tap the device icon on the player to switch where the music plays, and use your phone as the remote.

### Offline listening (Premium)

Premium users can download playlists, albums and podcasts to listen without a connection, which saves mobile data and works on flights.

### Sharing and social features

Share songs and playlists to other apps, make collaborative playlists with friends, and see what friends are listening to on desktop.

## Spotify Free vs Premium

| | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free | In-app purchases from $4.99 to $203.88 on Google Play |
| Ads | Yes | No |
| Music on demand | With some limits | Any song, any order |
| Offline music downloads | No | Yes |
| Audio quality | Standard | Higher quality options |

The free tier is the honest, legal way to listen without paying, and it's the version this page is about.

## Tips for the free tier

Follow artists and save songs to **Your Library** so Spotify learns your taste quickly and builds better playlists for you. Use the personalized mixes to discover music without searching. Podcasts work well on the free tier, so it's worth using Spotify for both. And if you mostly listen at home, connect to speakers or a TV with Spotify Connect so your phone stays free for other things.

## How to install Spotify APK on Android

Install [Spotify from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.spotify.music) and check that the developer is Spotify AB.

1. Open Google Play and search for Spotify, or use the link above.
2. Tap **Install**, open Spotify, and log in or sign up for a free account.
3. If your phone has no Play Store, any Spotify APK comes from a third-party mirror. Android will only install it over an existing Spotify if it's signed by Spotify.
4. If you downloaded a file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for Spotify.
5. If you installed from a file, turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is Spotify APK safe?

The official Spotify app is safe. It's published by Spotify AB on Google Play and rated Teen, because some songs and podcasts contain explicit content. You can block explicit content in Spotify's settings, and families can manage this through a Premium Family plan.

Play marks the app as containing ads and in-app purchases. Spotify uses your listening history to personalize recommendations and ads on the free tier, as described in its privacy policy.

The biggest risk is modified Spotify APKs that claim to unlock Premium for free. They aren't from Spotify, they break its terms of service, and Spotify regularly detects them and suspends the accounts that use them. They can also carry malware or steal your login.
