## What is Stardew Valley?

Stardew Valley is a farming and life simulation game. You leave a dull office job to take over your grandfather's overgrown farm near Pelican Town. From there, you clear the land, plant crops, raise animals, fish, mine for ores, make friends with the townspeople, and maybe find love. There's no rush and no wrong way to play: every season brings new crops, festivals and secrets.

Stardew Valley was created by one developer, Eric Barone, known as ConcernedApe. It came to Android in March 2019, costs $4.99, and has a 4.6-star rating. It's one of the [simulation games](/category/simulation) we list.

## Key features

### Farming

Plant and harvest seasonal crops, build sprinklers to water them, and turn produce into wine, cheese and preserves for more money.

### Animals

Raise chickens, cows, goats, pigs and more. Look after them and they produce better goods.

### Mining and combat

Explore the mines for ores, gems and monsters. Light combat with swords and slingshots keeps it interesting.

### Townspeople and relationships

Get to know more than 30 villagers, each with their own story and schedule. Give gifts, attend festivals, and you can marry and start a family.

### Fishing and foraging

Catch fish in rivers, lakes and the ocean with a fishing minigame, and forage wild plants in every season.

### The Community Center

Restore the old Community Center by completing bundles of items, which unlocks new areas and rewards.

## Tips for your first season

- **Plant parsnips first.** They're cheap and grow quickly in spring.
- **Check the TV.** The weather forecast and luck shows help you plan your day.
- **Save energy.** Eat food when your energy runs low, and go to bed before 2 a.m.
- **Fish early.** Fishing is a great money-maker in your first weeks.
- **Talk to everyone.** Friendship unlocks recipes, gifts and story events.
- **Use the controls that suit you.** Choose tap-to-move or a virtual joystick in the options.
