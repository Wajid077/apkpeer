## What is Subway Surfers?

Subway Surfers is an endless runner from SYBO. The Subway Surfers APK installs the official game on Android: you run along subway tracks, jump, roll and switch lanes to dodge trains, collect coins and power-ups, and escape the Inspector and his dog. A new season every few weeks takes the crew to a new city.

## Coins, keys and what to buy

Coins come from runs, missions and daily challenges, and buy power-up upgrades, characters and boards. Keys let you continue a run after crashing and are used in some events. Spend coins on power-up upgrades first, especially the jetpack, magnet and super sneakers, because they help every single run. Characters and boards are mostly cosmetic, apart from some boards with special abilities.
