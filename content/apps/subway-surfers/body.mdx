## What is Subway Surfers?

Subway Surfers is a free endless runner from SYBO Games, a studio in Copenhagen. You play a young graffiti artist caught tagging a train, and you run along the tracks to escape a grumpy Inspector and his dog. Swipe left and right to change lanes, up to jump, and down to roll under barriers, all while dodging oncoming trains.

The run never ends until you crash. Along the way you collect coins, grab power-ups like the jetpack and super sneakers, and hop on hoverboards that save you from one crash. It's simple enough for kids and fast enough to stay fun for adults.

The game has been on Google Play since September 2012 and has more than a billion downloads. It stays fresh with the World Tour: every few weeks the game moves to a new city with new scenery and characters. It's one of the best-known [arcade games](/category/arcade) on Android.

## Key features

### Swipe controls

Four swipes do everything: left and right to switch lanes, up to jump, and down to roll or drop quickly back to the ground. The game speeds up the longer you run, so reactions matter more and more.

### Power-ups

Pick up a jetpack to fly above the trains and collect coins, super sneakers to jump higher, a coin magnet, or a score multiplier. You can upgrade power-ups with coins so they last longer.

### Hoverboards

Activate a hoverboard by double-tapping the screen, and it absorbs one crash before breaking. Some boards have special abilities, such as higher jumps or speed boosts.

### The World Tour

Each update takes the game to a new city, from Shenzhen in this update to dozens of others before it. Each location brings its own visuals, characters, boards and challenges. Characters and boards you unlocked in earlier cities stay in your collection, so nothing is lost when the tour moves on.

### Characters and season hunts

Unlock new surfers by collecting tokens or completing challenges, like the Season Hunt in this update. Many characters have alternative outfits to unlock too.

## Tips for higher scores

Complete missions to raise your score multiplier. It's the biggest boost to your score over time. Spend coins on upgrading the magnet and jetpack first, because longer power-ups mean more coins on every run. Save hoverboards for later in a run, when speed makes crashes more likely. And run on top of the trains whenever you can: there are fewer obstacles up there, and it's easier to see what's coming.

## Is Subway Surfers APK safe?

The official Subway Surfers APK is safe. It's published by SYBO Games on Google Play, where it's rated Everyone 10+ for fantasy violence and has more than a billion downloads.

The game shows ads, including rewarded ones that give you extra coins or a second chance. In-app purchases run from $0.99 to $99.99 per item, and Google Play notes that some include random items, such as mystery boxes. On a child's phone, set up purchase authentication in Google Play or use Family Link.

Subway Surfers is one of the most copied games on Android, and modified APKs promising endless coins and keys are everywhere. They aren't made by SYBO, they're a common source of adware, and they don't get the World Tour updates. APKPeer only lists the official game.

## Subway Surfers vs alternatives

- **<AppLink slug="temple-run-2">Temple Run 2</AppLink>** is the classic turn-and-swipe runner. It has corners and cliffs instead of lanes, and a darker, adventure feel.
- **<AppLink slug="sonic-dash">Sonic Dash</AppLink>** puts Sonic in a lane-based runner with boss fights. It's faster, but busier with menus and ads.

If you want a bright, easy-going runner that keeps getting new places to explore, Subway Surfers is the one. It's light on storage, plays offline, and a run takes only a minute or two, which makes it an easy game to keep on any phone.
