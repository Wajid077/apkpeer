## What is Super Mario Run?

Super Mario Run is Nintendo's Mario platformer made for phones, and you play it with one hand. Mario runs forward on his own, and you tap to jump: a short tap for a small hop, a longer press for a high jump, and well-timed taps for spins and wall jumps. The challenge is collecting coins, finding the special pink, purple and black coins, and reaching the flagpole in style.

It's published by Nintendo Co., Ltd. on Google Play, where it arrived in March 2017 and has passed 100 million downloads. It looks and feels like a classic Mario game, with Goombas, Koopas, Bowser and familiar music, adapted for tapping on a phone.

Super Mario Run works differently from most free games: it's free to download and try, but the full game is a one-time purchase, with no ads. It's one of the most polished [arcade games](/category/arcade) on Android, and that pricing model is the main thing to understand before you install it.

## Key features

### World Tour

The main mode: run through courses across six worlds, from grasslands and haunted houses to airships and Bowser's castle. Each course has three sets of special coins to find, which adds a lot of replay value.

### Toad Rally

Race against "ghosts" of other players' runs. Stylish moves earn more coins and impress Toads, who join your kingdom if you win.

### Remix 10

Short, randomly mixed sections of courses played back to back. It's quick to play and good for short breaks.

### Kingdom Builder

Use the coins and Toads you collect to build and decorate your own Mushroom Kingdom, unlocking new characters and buildings.

### Several playable characters

Unlock characters such as Luigi, Peach, Toad, Toadette and Yoshi, each with slightly different moves, like Luigi's high jump or Peach's float.

## Tips for collecting special coins

Each World Tour course has pink coins to collect first, then harder purple and black coin sets that unlock after you finish the earlier ones. Use the pause blocks and bubbles to slow down or go back when you miss a coin. Watch where coins sit relative to walls, since many need a wall jump or a well-timed spin. And replay courses as different characters, as some moves make certain coins easier.

## Is Super Mario Run APK safe?

The official Super Mario Run APK is safe. It's published by Nintendo Co., Ltd. on Google Play and rated Everyone for mild fantasy violence. Google Play lists a single in-app purchase of $9.99 and no ads.

That makes it one of the more child-friendly games on Android: no ads, no random loot boxes, and a single clear price. If a child plays, the only purchase prompt is the full-game unlock, which you can protect with Google Play's purchase authentication.

Because the full game costs money, you'll see downloads claiming to be "Super Mario Run unlocked" or the full version for free. They aren't from Nintendo, they're unauthorized copies of a paid game, often bundled with adware, and they can't connect to Nintendo's servers properly, which the game needs to run.
