## What is Telegram?

Telegram is a cloud-based messaging app. The Telegram APK installs it on Android, where you chat one-to-one, join groups of up to 200,000 members, follow channels, use bots, and make voice and video calls. Because messages live in Telegram's cloud, they sync instantly across your phone, tablet and computer.

It's one of the [communication apps](/category/communication) we cover, with <AppLink slug="whatsapp">WhatsApp</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="signal">Signal</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Cloud chats

Messages, photos and files up to 2 GB sync across all your devices.

### Groups and channels

Groups hold up to 200,000 members; channels broadcast to unlimited subscribers.

### Secret Chats

End-to-end encrypted chats on one device, with self-destruct timers.

### Bots and mini apps

Bots handle reminders, polls, translations and more, right inside chats.

### Usernames and privacy

Share a username instead of your number, and control who sees your number, last seen and profile photo.

## How to install Telegram APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone.
2. Search for **Telegram** and check that the developer is **Telegram FZ-LLC**.
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to finish.
4. Open the app and sign in, or create an account.

The official listing is [Telegram on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.telegram.messenger). Avoid Telegram APK files on other download sites: they aren't from Telegram FZ-LLC and can carry malware or steal your login.

### Telegram for Android without Google Play

Telegram publishes its own APK at **telegram.org/android**. That version updates itself from Telegram. Allow your browser to install unknown apps when Android asks, then turn the permission off afterwards.

## Tips for using Telegram safely

- **Turn on two-step verification** in **Settings → Privacy and Security**.
- **Hide your number:** set **Phone number** to Nobody.
- **Limit group invites** to your contacts to avoid spam groups.
- **Use Secret Chats** for sensitive conversations.
- **Check active sessions** and end ones you don't recognise.

## Is Telegram APK safe?

## Telegram Premium: is it worth it?

Telegram Premium is an optional monthly subscription. It doubles many limits, such as the number of channels you can join and the size of files you can upload (up to 4 GB), adds faster downloads, voice-message transcription, extra reactions and stickers, and removes sponsored messages in public channels. Everything important, including groups, channels, calls and Secret Chats, is free. Most people never need Premium. It's mainly useful if you share very large files or follow hundreds of channels.

## Setting up Telegram on a new phone

Log in with your phone number and the code Telegram sends to your other devices or by SMS. Because chats live in the cloud, everything except Secret Chats appears automatically. Secret Chats stay on the old device, so save anything important from them first.

The Telegram APK from Google Play or telegram.org is the official way to get Telegram on Android. Hide your phone number, turn on two-step verification, and use Secret Chats when privacy really matters.
