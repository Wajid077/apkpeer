## What is Temu?

Temu is an online marketplace known for extremely low prices. Its app sells clothing, home and kitchen items, beauty products, toys, phone accessories, gadgets and much more. Many products ship directly from manufacturers and sellers in China, though Temu also uses local warehouses in some countries. Constant deals, games and coupons are a big part of the shopping experience.

Temu launched in 2022 and has passed 1 billion downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [shopping apps](/category/shopping) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="shein">SHEIN</AppLink>.

## Tips for shopping safely on Temu

- **Read reviews with photos.** They show the real product, size and quality.
- **Check sizes carefully.** Clothing sizes often run smaller than in Western shops.
- **Be careful with electronics.** Cheap chargers, cables and electrical items may not meet safety standards. Buy those from trusted brands.
- **Use a secure payment method.** Credit cards or PayPal offer extra protection.
- **Ignore pressure tactics.** Countdown timers and "only a few left" messages are designed to make you buy quickly.
- **Limit notifications.** Temu sends a lot; turn them down in settings.

## Is Temu APK safe?

The official Temu app is a genuine shopping app. It's published by Temu on Google Play, rated Teen, and Play shows no ads or in-app purchases. Like any shopping app, the important thing is how you use it.

- **Data concerns.** Temu has faced scrutiny and legal challenges over the amount of data its app collects. Grant only the permissions you need, and avoid linking other accounts unnecessarily.
- **Product safety.** Some regulators and consumer groups have found unsafe or poor-quality items on low-cost marketplaces. Be extra careful with toys for young children, electrical goods and cosmetics.
- **Scams.** Fake "free gift" links and copycat Temu sites are common. Only log in through the official app.


## Getting the delivery and refund you expect

Before paying, check the estimated delivery window and whether the item ships from a local warehouse, which is usually faster. After ordering, track the parcel in **Orders**. If something arrives damaged, different from the listing or not at all, open a request from the order page with photos; Temu's policies include refunds for missing items and credits for some late deliveries. Keep your communication inside the app rather than replying to emails or texts, since fake delivery messages are a common scam. When returning, use the return label and drop-off options the app gives you, and keep the receipt until the refund arrives.

## Managing notifications and permissions

Temu can send a lot of promotional notifications. In the app's settings, turn off the categories you don't want, or limit notifications in **Settings → Apps → Temu → Notifications** on your phone. Review permissions there too: a shopping app doesn't need access to your contacts or precise location for normal use. Turn off personalised ads in the app's privacy settings if you'd rather not have your browsing used to target you.
