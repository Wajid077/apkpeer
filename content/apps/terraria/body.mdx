## What is Terraria?

Terraria is a 2D sandbox adventure. Each new world is generated randomly, with forests, deserts, snow, jungles, oceans and a vast underground full of caves, treasure and danger. You dig, build a base, craft hundreds of weapons, tools, armour and items, attract townspeople to move in, and take on dozens of bosses, each harder than the last.

Terraria was created by Re-Logic, and the Android version is published by 505 Games. It arrived on Google Play in 2013, costs $4.99, and has passed 5 million downloads with a 4.6-star rating. It's one of the [adventure games](/category/adventure) we list.

## Key features

### Dig and explore

The world is huge and layered. Dig down through caves, the underground, caverns and the fiery Underworld, finding ores, chests and hidden biomes.

### Craft and build

Craft over 5,000 items, from tools and weapons to furniture and decorations. Build houses for non-player characters who move in and sell items or offer services.

### Bosses and events

Fight bosses like the Eye of Cthulhu, Skeletron and the Wall of Flesh, which changes the world into Hardmode. Invasions and events like the Blood Moon bring new enemies.

### Character progression

Your progress comes from equipment rather than levels. Better armour, accessories and weapons let you explore deeper and fight harder bosses.

### Multiplayer

Explore and fight bosses with friends, which makes tough fights much easier.

## Tips for beginners

- **Talk to the Guide.** He explains crafting recipes for any item you show him.
- **Build a shelter on day one.** Monsters get more dangerous at night.
- **Make a proper house.** NPCs only move into houses with walls, a door, a light, a table and a chair.
- **Collect Fallen Stars and Life Crystals.** They increase your mana and health.
- **Use a grappling hook.** It makes moving through caves much safer.
- **Prepare for bosses.** Build an arena with platforms and campfires before summoning a boss.
