## What is TikTok Lite?

TikTok Lite is a lighter version of TikTok, the short-video app. It's made for phones with limited storage and memory and for people on slow or expensive mobile data. You get the For You feed of short videos, can follow creators, like, comment and share, using the same TikTok account as the full app.

TikTok Lite is published by TikTok Pte. Ltd. and has passed 1 billion downloads on Google Play. It isn't available in every country, including the US, so we read its details from Google Play in Indonesia. It's one of the [social apps](/category/social) we list, alongside the full <AppLink slug="tiktok">TikTok</AppLink> app.

## Key features

### Smaller app

TikTok Lite takes up much less space than the full TikTok app and runs well on phones with less memory.

### Data saving

Videos are optimised to use less mobile data, which helps on limited or slow connections.

### For You feed

Swipe through personalised short videos, just like on TikTok.

### Same account

Log in with your TikTok account to keep your followers, likes and the people you follow.

### Basic creation

Post simple videos and interact with comments, with fewer advanced editing tools than the full app.

## Tips for using TikTok Lite

- **Turn on data saver** in settings if you're on mobile data.
- **Clear the cache** regularly to keep storage free.
- **Set screen time limits.** TikTok's digital wellbeing tools let you set daily limits.
- **Review privacy settings.** Choose who can comment, message you and see your videos.
- **Use the full app for editing.** If you create a lot, the full TikTok app has more tools.

## How to install TikTok Lite APK on Android

Install [TikTok Lite from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zhiliaoapp.musically.go).

1. Check that TikTok Lite appears in your country's Google Play. If it doesn't, use the full TikTok app instead.
2. Open Google Play, search for TikTok Lite, and check the developer is TikTok Pte. Ltd..
3. Tap **Install** and open the app.
4. Log in with your TikTok account or create a new one.

TikTok doesn't publish TikTok Lite as a separate APK outside Google Play. Installing it in a country where it isn't offered isn't supported, and unofficial files can carry malware.

## Is TikTok Lite APK safe?

The official TikTok Lite from Google Play is safe in the countries where it's offered. It's published by TikTok Pte. Ltd., rated 12+ in Indonesia, contains ads, and lists in-app purchases for coins used to send gifts to creators.

- **Privacy.** TikTok collects a lot of data about what you watch. Review your privacy and ad settings.
- **Kids and teens.** Use Family Pairing to manage screen time, content and messaging for teen accounts.
- **Rewards programs.** In some countries TikTok Lite has offered rewards for watching videos. Regulators in the EU questioned these programs in 2024. Don't treat them as a reliable income.
- **Unofficial versions.** Modified TikTok Lite APKs with download or ad-free features aren't from TikTok and can steal accounts.


## Controlling what you see

TikTok Lite's For You feed learns from what you watch, so a few minutes of tidying makes it much better. Long-press a video and choose **Not interested** to see less of that kind of content, and use keyword filters in settings to hide videos with words you'd rather avoid. Following creators you enjoy and liking their videos helps the feed show more of them. If the feed starts to feel repetitive, you can refresh it in content preferences, which resets recommendations as if you were new.

## Screen time and wellbeing

Short-video feeds are designed to keep you scrolling. In **Settings → Digital wellbeing**, set a daily screen time limit, schedule a break reminder, and turn on sleep reminders. Teen accounts have a daily limit on by default. Turning off notifications for likes and recommendations reduces the urge to open the app. For families, Family Pairing lets a parent link to a teen's account and manage screen time, content filters and messaging from their own phone.
