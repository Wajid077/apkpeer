## What is TikTok?

TikTok is a short-video app. The TikTok APK installs it on Android, where you scroll a personalised For You feed, follow creators, make your own videos with music and effects, go LIVE and shop in TikTok Shop. Its recommendation system is the reason people spend hours on it: it learns what you like very quickly.

It's one of the [social apps](/category/social) we cover, along with <AppLink slug="instagram">Instagram</AppLink>, and there's a lighter version, <AppLink slug="tiktok-lite">TikTok Lite</AppLink>, in some countries.

## Key features

### For You feed

Swipe through endless short videos picked for you. Long-press a video and tap **Not interested** to train the feed.

### Video editor

Record or upload clips, then add music, text, filters, effects and voiceovers. Templates make trends easy to copy.

### LIVE

Go live to your followers, take part in LIVE battles with other creators, and receive gifts.

### TikTok Shop

Buy products shown in videos and LIVE streams, in countries where Shop is available.

### Screen time tools

Set daily limits, sleep reminders and break reminders in **Settings → Screen time**.

## Is TikTok APK safe?

The official TikTok app from Google Play is safe to install. It's published by TikTok Pte. Ltd., rated Teen, and contains ads.

- **Lookalike apps.** Apps promising free followers, coins or extra download features aren't from TikTok and can take over your account. So can unofficial TikTok APKs from download sites.
- **Privacy.** TikTok collects data about what you watch. Review **Settings → Privacy**, and set your account to private if you only want approved followers.
- **Kids and teens.** Use Family Pairing to manage screen time, search and direct messages.
- **Coins and gifts.** LIVE gifts cost real money. Turn on Google Play's purchase authentication for younger users.

## Tips for using TikTok on Android

- **Train your feed.** Long-press videos you don't like and tap **Not interested**, and add keywords to filter in **Content preferences**.
- **Save data.** Turn on **Data saver** in Settings to load lower-quality video on mobile data.
- **Download your own videos.** Save drafts and posts to your phone before editing elsewhere.
- **Refresh the feed.** If recommendations feel stuck, refresh your For You feed in content preferences.
- **Keep your account private** if you only want approved followers, and limit who can duet or stitch your videos.
- **Clear the cache monthly.** **Settings → Free up space** removes cached videos without deleting drafts.
