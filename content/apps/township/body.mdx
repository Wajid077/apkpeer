## What is Township?

Township is a farming and town-building game by Playrix, and the Township APK is its Android installer. You grow crops, process them in factories, fill orders from trains, planes and helicopters, and use the profits to build homes, shops and attractions. Over time your village becomes a busy town with a zoo, mine and island trading.

Township is in our [casual games](/category/casual) section, along with <AppLink slug="hay-day">Hay Day</AppLink> and Playrix's <AppLink slug="gardenscapes">Gardenscapes</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Farming and factories

Grow wheat, corn and more, and turn them into bread, clothes and other goods.

### Orders

Fill helicopter, train and plane orders for coins and experience.

### Town building

Build houses to grow your population, community buildings to expand, and decorations to make it yours.

### Zoo, mine and islands

Unlock a zoo, a mine for ores and islands for expeditions.

### Co-ops and regattas

Join a co-op to trade, help others and compete in regatta seasons.

## How to install Township APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Township**.
2. Check that the developer is **Playrix**.
3. Tap **Install**, then start playing.

The official listing is [Township on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.playrix.township). Skip Township APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Playrix, they don't get updates, and they can carry malware or adware.

## Township beginner tips

- **Keep fields busy** with crops that match your online time: short crops while playing, long crops overnight.
- **Build factories early** and keep their queues full.
- **Prioritise community buildings** to expand your town.
- **Join an active co-op** for help and regatta rewards.

## Is Township APK safe?

The official Township from Google Play is safe and published by Playrix. Avoid "free cash" copies from other sites; they're unofficial and can break or steal your save. Turn on **purchase authentication** in Google Play settings if a child plays: purchases here range from $0.99 to $149.99.
