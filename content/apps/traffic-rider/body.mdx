## What is Traffic Rider?

Traffic Rider is a motorbike game from skgames, and the Traffic Rider APK is its Android installer. It's the follow-up to Traffic Racer.

## Riding tips

- **Overtake close** (under 100 km/h doesn't count) for bonus points.
- **Two-way roads** pay extra for riding in the opposite lane, but they're riskier.
- **Use the brake** before tight gaps instead of swerving.
- **Upgrade one bike** fully rather than many bikes a little.
