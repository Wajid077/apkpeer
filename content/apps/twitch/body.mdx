## What is Twitch?

Twitch is a live streaming platform owned by Amazon, and the Twitch APK is its Android app. Millions of streamers broadcast games, music, art, talk shows and esports, and viewers chat live with them. The app lets you watch, chat, follow channels, clip moments and go live yourself.

It's one of the [entertainment apps](/category/entertainment) we cover. Many communities pair Twitch with <AppLink slug="discord">Discord</AppLink> for chat between streams.

## Using less data on Twitch

Live video at "Source" quality can use several gigabytes an hour. Set quality to 480p or lower on mobile data, or use **audio only** for talk streams and music.

## Is Twitch APK safe?

The official Twitch app from Google Play is safe, published by Twitch Interactive. It's rated Teen because streams and chat are user-generated. For younger viewers, review the content on followed channels and use Twitch's chat filters. Never share login details through links in chat or messages, and ignore "free Bits" offers.

## Twitch on PC, TV and iPhone

Watch at twitch.tv in any browser, or use the Twitch apps for iPhone, smart TVs, game consoles and Android TV. Follows and subscriptions sync across devices through your Twitch account. To stream from a PC, most creators use OBS Studio or Twitch's own desktop tools.
