## What is UNO!?

UNO! is the official mobile version of Mattel's classic card game. The UNO! APK installs it on Android: match cards by colour or number, play action cards like Skip, Reverse and Draw Two, and shout "UNO!" when you have one card left. You can play quick matches with players worldwide, private rooms with friends, and 2v2 team games.

It's one of the [card games](/category/card) we cover, alongside board games like <AppLink slug="ludo-king">Ludo King</AppLink>.

## UNO! rules in brief

Match the top card by colour, number or symbol. Skip misses the next player's turn, Reverse changes direction, Draw Two makes the next player draw two, and Wild lets you pick the colour. Wild Draw Four can only be played when you have no other playable card in some rule sets. Call UNO! when you have one card left, or you'll draw a penalty.
