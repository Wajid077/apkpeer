## What is Viber?

Rakuten Viber Messenger is a free messaging and calling app, and the Viber APK is its Android installer. It sends texts, voice and video calls, voice notes, photos and stickers over the internet, and supports large group chats and public Communities. Viber is especially popular in Eastern Europe, the Middle East and parts of Asia.

## How to install Viber APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** and search for **Viber**.
2. Check that the developer is **Viber Media**.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app and sign in. Viber verifies your phone number with an SMS or a call, so keep your SIM in the phone during setup.

The official listing is [Viber on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.viber.voip). Skip Viber APK files from other download sites: they aren't from Viber Media, they don't update, and they can carry malware or adware.

## Viber privacy settings to check

- **Settings → Privacy:** hide your online status and "seen" receipts.
- **Who can add you to groups:** limit it to contacts to cut spam.
- **Disappearing messages:** turn them on for sensitive chats.
- **Block and report** unknown business accounts that message you.

## Is Viber APK safe?

The official Viber app from Google Play is safe, published by Viber Media (Rakuten). Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Scammers still use Viber, so never share your activation code with anyone: it's how they take over accounts.
