## What is VLC for Android?

VLC for Android is the phone version of VLC media player, the free, open-source player made by the VideoLAN project. If you've used VLC on a computer, you already know its reputation: it plays practically anything you give it, without hunting for codecs and without ads. The Android app brings that to your phone.

It's published on Google Play by Videolabs, the company that works on VLC alongside the non-profit VideoLAN, and has been there since February 2015 with more than 500 million downloads. It's also on F-Droid, built from the same open-source code.

VLC isn't a streaming service. It plays your own media: videos you've downloaded, music on your SD card, files on a USB drive, or streams from your home server. For that job, it's one of the best [video player apps](/category/video-players) you can install, and it doesn't cost anything.

## Key features

### Plays almost any format

MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, WebM, FLAC, MP3, OGG and many more play without extra downloads. VLC also opens disc images and network streams. If a file won't play in your phone's gallery app, try VLC first.

### Subtitles done properly

VLC reads subtitles embedded in a file or in a separate file next to it. During playback, tap the subtitle button to switch tracks, change the delay if they're out of sync, or adjust the size, which this update gives more options for.

### Network playback

Browse shared folders on your home network (SMB), media servers (UPnP/DLNA) and other network locations straight from VLC's **Browse** tab. You can also paste a stream URL to play it directly.

### Audio player

VLC is a solid music player too, with a library sorted by artist, album and genre, an equalizer, and playback that continues with the screen off.

### Remote access

Remote access lets you control VLC and browse its library from a web browser on another device on the same network. Version 3.7.2 adds playing audio files in that browser view.

## Tips for getting more out of VLC

Turn on background playback for audio, so podcasts and music keep playing with the screen off. Use the pop-up (picture-in-picture) button to keep a video playing while you use other apps. If a file stutters, try changing hardware acceleration in the video settings. And if subtitles are missing, VLC can search for and download them from inside the player.

## How to install VLC APK on Android

The easiest option is [VLC on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.videolan.vlc). Without Play Store, use F-Droid or VideoLAN's own [download page](https://www.videolan.org/vlc/download-android.html).

1. Choose one official source: Google Play, F-Droid, or videolan.org.
2. For a direct APK, pick the build that matches your phone's processor. Most phones from recent years use **arm64-v8a**.
3. If you downloaded the file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We link to VideoLAN's and F-Droid's own downloads, so there's no APKPeer file for VLC.
4. Open the APK and allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks.
5. Tap **Install**, open VLC, and allow access to your media so it can build your library.
6. Turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

## Is VLC APK safe?

The official VLC APK is safe, and it's one of the most trustworthy apps you can install. It's open source, made by a non-profit project, and has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Google Play rates it Everyone.

Its permissions are what a media player needs: access to your photos, videos, music and files to play them, network access for streaming and remote access, and notifications for background playback controls.

The risk is fake copies. Because VLC is so well known, unofficial sites have bundled lookalike "VLC" downloads with adware. VideoLAN only distributes VLC through its own site, Google Play, F-Droid and a few other official stores. APKPeer only links to those official sources.
