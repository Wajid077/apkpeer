## What is VN Video Editor?

VN (originally VlogNow) is a free photo and video editor from Ubiquiti Labs, and the VN APK is its Android installer. It gives you a multi-track timeline, keyframe animation, speed curves, colour filters, text, music and transitions, and it exports without a watermark. Many creators use it as a step up from simple editors.

VN is one of the [video editors](/category/video-editors) we cover, along with <AppLink slug="capcut">CapCut</AppLink>, <AppLink slug="kinemaster">KineMaster</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="inshot">InShot</AppLink>.

## Editing tips in VN

- Set the **project ratio** first (9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube).
- Use **Beat** markers to cut clips on the music.
- Apply a **speed curve** for smooth ramps instead of abrupt speed changes.
- Keep a **draft backup** before big changes; VN saves projects on the phone only.

## Is VN APK safe?

The official VN app from Google Play is safe and published by Ubiquiti Labs. It's rated Teen and contains ads and optional purchases. Avoid "VN Pro unlocked" APKs from other sites: they're unofficial, can contain malware, and can't be updated.
