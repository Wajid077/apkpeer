## What is World Cricket Championship 3?

World Cricket Championship 3, usually called WCC3, is a 3D cricket game from Nextwave Multimedia, a studio based in India. It's known for its detailed player animations, wide range of shots and deliveries, and commentary, which together make matches feel closer to a TV broadcast than most mobile cricket games.

You can play quick matches, full tournaments and a career mode where you create a player and build their career, plus online matches against other players. Formats range from short T20 games to longer matches, with a large choice of teams.

WCC3 has been on Google Play since September 2020 and has more than 10 million downloads. The September 2026 update added NPL 2026, the game's franchise-style T20 league. It's one of the most detailed cricket games among the [sports games](/category/sports) on Android.

## Key features

### Detailed batting and bowling

WCC3 offers a large set of batting shots, chosen by swiping in a direction and timing the stroke, and bowling options including pace and spin variations, line and length. The animations are what the game is best known for.

### Career mode

Create your own player, choose a role, and build a career from domestic cricket upward, with stats that improve as you perform. It's a different experience from playing as a whole team.

### Tournaments and NPL

Play world tournaments and the NPL franchise league, with schedules, points tables and knockouts. NPL 2026 is the current season after this update.

### Commentary

Matches have commentary in several languages, adding a lot of atmosphere to big chases and close finishes. You can pick the commentary language in the game's settings, and turn it off if you prefer playing to the crowd noise alone.

### Online multiplayer

Play real-time matches against other players online, with rankings and rewards for winning. A stable Wi-Fi connection helps avoid lag at the crease in online matches.

## Tips for batting better

Watch the bowler's line before choosing a shot direction: leave or defend balls outside off stump early in your innings. Time your swipe as the ball arrives rather than as it's released. Play the ball along the ground to start with; lofted shots are riskier until you've settled. And check the field placement before each over, aiming for gaps rather than fielders.

## Cricket formats explained

| Format | Overs per side | What it's like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T20 | 20 | Fast and aggressive; boundaries matter most |
| ODI | 50 | Balanced; build an innings, then accelerate |
| Test | Two innings each, no over limit | Patient; wickets matter more than run rate |

WCC3 lets you shorten matches to fit the time you have, but the same thinking applies: attack in T20, pace yourself in ODIs, and protect your wicket in longer formats.

## Settings for older phones

WCC3's detailed graphics can strain older phones. In the game's settings, lower the graphics quality, and turn off extras you don't need, such as some visual effects. Closing other apps before a match and keeping free storage helps too. If matches still stutter, shorter formats like T20 load and play more smoothly than long matches.
