## What is WhatsApp Business?

WhatsApp Business is the free version of WhatsApp made for small businesses: shops, salons, tutors, restaurants and anyone who sells to customers over chat. It's published by WhatsApp LLC, the same company behind <AppLink slug="whatsapp">WhatsApp</AppLink>, and it's been on Google Play since January 2018 with more than a billion downloads.

It works exactly like WhatsApp for messaging, with the same end-to-end encrypted chats, calls, groups and Status. What it adds are tools for running a business on chat: a business profile, a product catalog, quick replies, automatic greeting and away messages, and labels to keep track of orders and customers.

Your customers don't need anything special. They message you from regular WhatsApp and see your business profile. It's one of the [messaging apps](/category/communication) we cover, and the only one here built for selling.

## Key features

### Business profile

Add your business name, category, description, address, opening hours, email and website. Customers see this when they open your chat, so they don't have to ask when you're open. Set it up under **Settings → Business tools → Business profile**.

### Catalog

Show your products or services with photos, prices and descriptions. Customers can browse the catalog inside the chat and send you an item to ask about it. It's an easy shop window for businesses without a website.

### Quick replies

Save answers you send often, like delivery times or payment details, and insert them by typing "/" in a chat. It saves a lot of typing when the same questions come in every day.

### Greeting and away messages

Send an automatic greeting to new customers, and an away message outside business hours. Both are under **Settings → Business tools**.

### Labels

Tag chats with labels such as "New customer", "Pending payment" or "Order complete", or create your own. Filtering chats by label turns WhatsApp into a basic order tracker.

## How to install WhatsApp Business APK on Android

The easiest way is [WhatsApp Business on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.whatsapp.w4b), which keeps it updated automatically.

1. Check your phone runs Android 6.0 or newer.
2. Get the app only from Google Play or WhatsApp's official site. Modified "business" versions of WhatsApp put your account at risk.
3. If you downloaded the file from APKPeer, check that the SHA-256 matches the one on this page. We currently link to Google Play only, so there's no APKPeer file for WhatsApp Business.
4. Open the APK and allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks.
5. Tap **Install**, open WhatsApp Business, and verify the phone number you'll use for the business.
6. Turn off the install permission under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

If the number is already on personal WhatsApp, the app offers to move your chats over. Back them up first.

## Is WhatsApp Business APK safe?

The official WhatsApp Business app is safe. It's published by WhatsApp LLC, signed with WhatsApp's key, and rated Everyone on Google Play. Chats with customers are end-to-end encrypted, just like regular WhatsApp.

There are a few things to understand. WhatsApp Business shares some data with other Meta companies, as described in WhatsApp's [privacy policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy). If you link your business to Facebook or Instagram, more data is shared between them. And Google Play lists in-app purchases from $0.29 to $4,789.99, covering optional paid business features, so check the price before buying anything.

Modified WhatsApp apps that promise extra business features are a real risk. They aren't made by WhatsApp, they can read your customers' messages, and WhatsApp bans numbers that use them, which could cut your business off from its customers overnight. APKPeer only links to the official app.

## WhatsApp Business vs alternatives

- **<AppLink slug="whatsapp">WhatsApp</AppLink>** is the same app without the business tools. If you're not selling anything, it's all you need.
- **<AppLink slug="telegram">Telegram</AppLink>** offers channels, bots and huge groups, which suit broadcasting to a big audience. Most customers in many countries are on WhatsApp, though.
- **<AppLink slug="instagram">Instagram</AppLink>** is better for being discovered by new customers through photos and reels. Many businesses use it to attract customers, then handle orders on WhatsApp Business.

For a small business whose customers already use WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business is the obvious free choice. Set up your profile, catalog and away message on day one, and customers will get answers even when you're busy.
