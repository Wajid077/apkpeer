## What is WhatsApp?

WhatsApp is a free messaging app owned by Meta. The WhatsApp APK installs it on Android phones and tablets, where you can send texts, voice notes, photos, videos and documents, make voice and video calls, join groups and share Status updates. It uses your phone number as your account, so anyone saved in your contacts who uses WhatsApp shows up automatically.

It's the default chat app in much of the world, and it's one of the [communication apps](/category/communication) we cover alongside <AppLink slug="telegram">Telegram</AppLink> and <AppLink slug="signal">Signal</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Chats and voice notes

Send messages, photos, videos, documents up to 2 GB, stickers and voice notes. You can edit a message shortly after sending and react with any emoji.

### Voice and video calls

Make free one-to-one and group calls. The latest update lets you move a group call between your phone and computer, add a waiting room for call links, and pin one person to a bigger tile.

### Groups and Communities

Groups hold up to 1,024 people. Communities collect several related groups, such as a school or neighbourhood, under one announcement channel.

### Status and Channels

Status shares photos, videos and text for 24 hours. Channels let you follow creators, brands and news outlets in one-way broadcasts.

### Privacy tools

Disappearing messages, view-once media, chat lock with your fingerprint, and controls for who sees your profile photo, last seen and online status.

## How to install WhatsApp APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone.
2. Search for **WhatsApp** and check that the developer is **WhatsApp LLC**.
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to finish.
4. Open the app and sign in, or create an account.

If the Play Store isn't on your phone, use the [official WhatsApp listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.whatsapp) from a phone or computer that has it, or the developer's own website listed below.

### WhatsApp for Android without Google Play

WhatsApp publishes its own official APK at **whatsapp.com/android** for phones without Google Play. Download it there, open the file, allow your browser to **Install unknown apps** when Android asks, and tap **Install**. Turn that permission off again afterwards under **Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps**.

### Moving to a new phone

Back up your chats first in **Settings → Chats → Chat backup** (to Google Drive), or use **Settings → Chats → Transfer chats** to move them straight to your new Android phone.

## WhatsApp on PC, Mac and iPhone

- **PC and Mac:** use web.whatsapp.com or the desktop app, then scan the QR code in **Settings → Linked devices** on your phone.
- **iPhone:** install WhatsApp from the App Store. Moving chats between Android and iPhone uses the built-in transfer tool during setup.
- **Tablets:** WhatsApp works on Android tablets as a linked device or as a primary account.

## Is WhatsApp APK safe?

The official WhatsApp APK is safe, whether it comes from Google Play or whatsapp.com/android. Both are signed by WhatsApp LLC. Personal messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted, so even Meta can't read them.

The real risks are scams and lookalike apps:

- **Never share your six-digit code.** Anyone asking for it is trying to take over your account. Turn on **two-step verification** in Settings → Account.
- **Avoid unofficial WhatsApp clones** that promise extra features. They aren't from Meta, they can read your messages, and WhatsApp bans accounts that use them.
- **Check links in messages** promising prizes or "your account will be closed". They're phishing.
- **For kids,** set groups and profile visibility to My contacts.
