## What a VPN can and can't do

A VPN encrypts traffic between your phone and the VPN server, so public Wi-Fi operators and your mobile network can't see which sites you visit. It can also make you appear in another location. It doesn't make you anonymous, it won't stop phishing or malware you download yourself, and the VPN provider can see your traffic instead of your network. Choose a provider whose privacy policy you trust.
