## Wordscapes tips

- **Shuffle** the wheel when you're stuck; new letter positions reveal words.
- **Start with short words** (three letters) to fill crossings.
- **Look for endings** like -ED, -ER, -ING and -S.
- **Save coins** for the hard levels instead of using hints early.
