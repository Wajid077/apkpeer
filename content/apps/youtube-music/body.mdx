## What is YouTube Music?

YouTube Music is Google's music streaming service. It combines official studio releases, albums and artist playlists with YouTube's huge catalogue of music videos, live performances, remixes and covers you won't find on other streaming services. It learns your taste from what you play and builds mixes, radio stations and recommendations for you.

The app launched in 2015 and has passed 5 billion downloads on Google Play, partly because it comes preinstalled on many Android phones. It's one of the [music and audio apps](/category/music-audio) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="spotify">Spotify</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Huge catalogue

You get official albums and singles alongside YouTube-only content: live sessions, covers, remixes, and older or regional music that never made it to other services. Switch between the song and its music video with one tap.

### Personalised mixes

Supermixes, My Mix playlists, Discover Mix and mood or activity mixes refresh regularly based on your listening. Start radio from any song or artist for an endless queue of similar music.

### Lyrics and samples

Most songs show time-synced lyrics. The Samples tab offers short clips of new music to discover artists quickly.

### Podcasts

YouTube Music also plays podcasts, including video podcasts from YouTube, so you can follow shows in the same app.

### Library and uploads

Save songs, albums and playlists to your library, and upload your own music files on the web to stream them on your phone.

## How to install YouTube Music APK on Android

YouTube Music is preinstalled on many phones. Otherwise install [YouTube Music from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.youtube.music).

1. Open Google Play, search for YouTube Music, and check the developer is Google LLC.
2. Tap **Install** or **Update**.
3. Open the app, sign in with your Google account, and pick a few artists you like so recommendations start in the right place.

YouTube Music needs Google services to sign in. Without Google Play, use music.youtube.com in a browser. APKPeer doesn't host a separate file.

## Free vs Premium

The free tier lets you search and play music with ads. On phones, most music won't keep playing in the background or with the screen off, and you can't download.

YouTube Music Premium removes ads, plays in the background, and lets you download music for offline listening. YouTube Premium includes all of that plus ad-free YouTube videos. Family and student plans are available in many countries. Prices vary by country, so check the app for yours.

## Tips for better recommendations

- **Like and dislike songs.** Thumbs up and down shape your mixes quickly.
- **Pause history for a guest.** If someone else is using your phone, pause watch history so their music doesn't change your recommendations.
- **Set audio quality.** In **Settings → Data saving**, choose lower quality on mobile data and high quality on Wi-Fi.
- **Smart downloads.** With Premium, turn on Smart Downloads to keep fresh music ready for flights and commutes.

## Is YouTube Music APK safe?

The official YouTube Music app is safe to install from Google Play. It's published by Google LLC, rated Teen, contains ads on the free tier, and lists in-app purchases from $0.05 to $999.99 per item.

Avoid modified YouTube Music apps that claim to unlock Premium features for free. They aren't from Google, they need your Google account login, and they can steal it or stop working without warning. They also break YouTube's terms, which can put your account at risk. The official Premium subscription is the only safe way to get background play and downloads.


## Moving your music to YouTube Music

If you're coming from another service, you can import playlists using third-party transfer tools, or rebuild your favourites by liking songs as you play them; YouTube Music learns quickly. Songs you've watched as music videos on YouTube already count towards your taste, so recommendations are often good from day one. To keep your library tidy, use the filters in the Library tab to show only songs, albums or artists, and create playlists for different moods or activities. Collaborative playlists let friends add songs too, which is handy for parties and road trips.

## YouTube Music vs alternatives

- **<AppLink slug="spotify">Spotify</AppLink>** has the most polished playlists and the biggest podcast library, and a free tier with background play.
- **<AppLink slug="youtube">YouTube</AppLink>** is better for watching music videos and everything else on YouTube.
- **<AppLink slug="shazam">Shazam</AppLink>** identifies a song playing around you and can send it to your music app.

If you already pay for YouTube Premium, YouTube Music is included and hard to beat. If you want to listen in the background for free, Spotify's free tier is more generous.
