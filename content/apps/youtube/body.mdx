## What is YouTube?

YouTube is the world's biggest video platform, owned by Google. The Android app lets you watch videos from creators, news outlets, musicians and brands, scroll short vertical videos in Shorts, join live streams, subscribe to channels and comment. You can also upload your own videos and Shorts, edit them with basic tools, and go live from your phone.

YouTube comes preinstalled on almost every Android phone and has passed 10 billion downloads on Google Play. It's one of the [entertainment apps](/category/entertainment) we list, alongside <AppLink slug="netflix">Netflix</AppLink>.

## Key features

### Home, subscriptions and search

Your Home feed recommends videos based on what you watch. The Subscriptions tab shows new uploads from channels you follow. Search finds videos, channels, playlists and specific moments inside long videos.

### Shorts

Shorts are vertical videos up to three minutes long. Swipe through them like other short-video apps, or create your own with music, effects, timers and remix tools.

### Live and premieres

Watch live streams with live chat, and join premieres where creators release a new video and chat with viewers at the same time.

### Library and playlists

Save videos to Watch Later, build playlists, and see your history. You can pause or delete watch history to change what YouTube recommends.

### Premium features

YouTube Premium removes ads, lets you download videos for offline viewing, plays in the background and with your screen off, and includes YouTube Music Premium.

## How to install YouTube APK on Android

YouTube is usually preinstalled. If it's missing or disabled, install [YouTube from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.youtube).

1. Open Google Play, search for YouTube, and check the developer is Google LLC.
2. Tap **Install** or **Update**. If it shows **Enable**, YouTube was disabled on your phone; tap it to turn it back on.
3. Open YouTube and sign in with your Google account to see your subscriptions and history.

YouTube needs Google Play services to sign in. Without Google Play, use YouTube in your browser at youtube.com. APKPeer doesn't host a YouTube APK.

## Settings that save data and battery

- **Video quality on mobile.** In **Settings → Video quality preferences**, choose "Data saver" on mobile networks and "Higher picture quality" on Wi-Fi.
- **Autoplay.** Turn off autoplay so the next video doesn't start on its own.
- **Uploads on Wi-Fi only.** In **Settings → Background & downloads**, upload only over Wi-Fi.
- **Take a break and bedtime reminders.** In **Settings → General**, YouTube can remind you to stop after a set time or at bedtime.
- **Restricted Mode.** Turn this on to hide potentially mature videos. It isn't perfect, but helps on shared devices.

## Is YouTube APK safe?

The official YouTube app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Google LLC, rated Teen, contains ads, and lists in-app purchases from $0.05 to $999.99 per item for things like Premium, channel memberships and Super Chat.

Be careful with modified YouTube apps that promise to block ads or enable background play for free. They aren't from Google, they need your Google login to work, and some versions have carried malware. They can also stop working without warning when YouTube changes its systems. If you want an ad-free experience, YouTube Premium or Premium Lite is the official route.

For children, use YouTube Kids or a supervised account through Google Family Link rather than the main app.


## YouTube for families

YouTube's main app is rated Teen, so set things up before handing a phone to a child. For under-13s, Google Family Link lets parents create a supervised YouTube account with three content levels: Explore, Explore More and Most of YouTube. For young children, the separate YouTube Kids app offers an even more limited catalogue, timers, and the option to approve every video or channel. On a shared family phone, sign out of your own account or use a separate Google profile, so your recommendations and history stay yours and your child's viewing doesn't shape your feed. Turning on Restricted Mode adds another layer, though no filter catches everything.
