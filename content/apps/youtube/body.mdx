## What is YouTube?

YouTube is Google's video platform. The YouTube APK is the Android app for watching videos from creators, musicians, news channels and brands, scrolling Shorts, joining live streams and premieres, subscribing to channels, and uploading your own videos. It comes preinstalled on almost every Android phone.

It's one of the [entertainment apps](/category/entertainment) we cover, alongside <AppLink slug="netflix">Netflix</AppLink>. For music, Google's separate <AppLink slug="youtube-music">YouTube Music</AppLink> app is better.

## Key features

### Home, Subscriptions and Search

Your Home feed recommends videos. The Subscriptions tab shows new uploads from channels you follow, and search can jump to key moments in long videos.

### Shorts

Vertical videos up to three minutes, with music, remix and green-screen tools for making your own.

### Live and premieres

Watch live streams with live chat, and join premieres where creators release a video together with viewers.

### Library and playlists

Save videos to Watch Later, build playlists and see your history. Pausing history changes what YouTube recommends.

### YouTube Premium

Removes ads, plays in the background and with the screen off, allows downloads, and includes YouTube Music Premium.

## How to install YouTube APK on Android

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your Android phone.
2. Search for **YouTube** and check that the developer is **Google LLC**.
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to finish.
4. Open the app and sign in, or create an account.

If the Play Store isn't on your phone, use the [official YouTube listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.youtube) from a phone or computer that has it, or the developer's own website listed below.

### If YouTube is disabled or missing

If Play shows **Enable** instead of Install, YouTube was turned off on your phone. Tap **Enable**, then **Update**. You can also turn it on under **Settings → Apps → YouTube**.

## Is YouTube APK safe?

The official YouTube app from Google Play is safe. It's published by Google LLC, rated Teen, and contains ads.

- **Unofficial YouTube copies** that promise free background play or no ads aren't from Google. They need your Google login, and some have carried malware. YouTube Premium and Premium Lite are the official way to remove ads.
- **Kids.** Use YouTube Kids or a supervised account through Google Family Link rather than the main app.
- **Data and battery.** In **Settings → Video quality preferences**, choose data saver on mobile networks.

## Tips for using YouTube on Android

- **Pause or delete history** to reset recommendations, in **Settings → Manage all history**.
- **Use picture-in-picture.** In countries where it's available, swipe home while a video plays to keep watching in a small window.
- **Double-tap to skip.** Double-tap the left or right side of a video to jump back or forward 10 seconds.
- **Turn on captions.** Tap CC for subtitles, including auto-translated captions on many videos.
- **Upload on Wi-Fi only** in **Settings → Background & downloads**.
- **Set a bedtime reminder** in **Settings → General** so late-night scrolling doesn't run on.

## Final thoughts

The YouTube APK on Google Play is the official way to watch YouTube on Android, and it's probably already on your phone. Keep it updated, set data saver for mobile networks, and use YouTube Kids for children.
