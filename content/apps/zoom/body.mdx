## What is Zoom Workplace?

Zoom Workplace is Zoom's all-in-one communication app. It's best known for video meetings, from one-to-one calls to large webinars, but it also includes team chat, phone calls on paid plans, whiteboards, docs, mail and calendar. Zoom's AI Companion, included with paid plans, can summarise meetings and help with follow-ups.

Zoom launched its Android app in 2013, and it has passed 1 billion downloads on Google Play. It's published by zoom.com, and it's one of the [productivity apps](/category/productivity) we list.

## Key features

### Video meetings

Join or host meetings with video and audio, chat, reactions, breakout rooms and virtual backgrounds. Large gallery views show many participants at once.

### Screen sharing

Share your screen, photos, documents or a whiteboard from your phone.

### Team chat

Message colleagues one-to-one or in channels, share files and start meetings from chat.

### Whiteboards and docs

Collaborate on digital whiteboards and documents during or outside meetings.

### Calendar and scheduling

Connect your calendar to see and join upcoming meetings in one tap.

### AI Companion

On paid plans, AI Companion can summarise meetings, answer questions about what was discussed and draft messages.

## Tips for better Zoom calls

- **Test before important meetings.** Use Zoom's test meeting to check your camera and microphone.
- **Mute when you're not speaking.** It cuts background noise for everyone.
- **Use Wi-Fi.** Video uses a lot of mobile data; turn off video if your connection is weak.
- **Turn on original sound or noise suppression** depending on whether you're playing music or just talking.
- **Keep the app updated.** Some meetings require the latest version.

## How to install Zoom APK on Android

Install [Zoom from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=us.zoom.videomeetings).

1. Check that your phone runs Android 9 or newer.
2. Open Google Play, search for Zoom, and check the developer is zoom.com.
3. Tap **Install** and open the app.
4. Join a meeting with a link or meeting ID, or sign in to host meetings.

Zoom also offers downloads from zoom.com, but on Android, Google Play is the recommended and safest way to install and update it.

## Is Zoom APK safe?

The official Zoom Workplace app is safe to install from Google Play. It's published by zoom.com, rated Everyone, has no ads, and offers in-app purchases for paid plans.

- **Meeting security.** When hosting, use passcodes and waiting rooms, and don't share meeting links publicly, to avoid uninvited guests.
- **Phishing.** Fake meeting invites and "Zoom update" emails can lead to malware or fake login pages. Join meetings only from trusted invites, and update Zoom through Google Play.
- **Permissions.** Allow camera, microphone and screen recording only when you need them.


## Hosting your first meeting

To host, sign in and tap **New Meeting**, or schedule one with a date and time so you can send the link in advance. For safety, keep the waiting room and passcode turned on, and admit people you recognise. As host, you can mute participants, turn off their video, remove someone, lock the meeting once everyone has arrived, and stop screen sharing by others. On the free Basic plan, group meetings have a time limit, so schedule a new link if you need longer. Recording to the cloud requires a paid plan, while local recording isn't available on phones, so check what you need before an important call.

## Saving data and battery

Video calls use a lot of data. In meeting settings, turn off HD video and, if your connection is weak, switch your camera off and use audio only. Turning off other participants' video in gallery view also helps on slow connections. Calls can drain the battery quickly, so plug in for long meetings and close other apps running in the background.
