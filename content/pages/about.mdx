## Why APKPeer exists

Search for almost any popular Android app followed by "APK" and you'll find pages offering "mod", "premium unlocked" or "unlimited coins" builds. Many of those files are repackaged with adware or worse, and online games ban accounts that use them. People still search for APKs for good reasons: their phone has no Play Store, an update isn't available in their country yet, or they want to roll back a version that broke something.

APKPeer is for those people. We point you to the original app from its real developer, give you the facts you need to check it, and say plainly when something isn't safe.

## What we list, and what we don't

We list free and paid Android apps and games in their original form. Download buttons go to Google Play or the developer's own website. When we host an APK file ourselves, it's an unmodified copy whose signing certificate matches the developer's official build, and the page shows both fingerprints so you can check it.

We never list or link to modified APKs, cracked paid apps, cheats or mod menus, apps built to stream pirated films or sport, or unofficial clones of apps such as WhatsApp and Instagram. Our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) explains these rules in detail.

## How we choose apps

We cover apps that a lot of people search for, starting with the ones readers ask about most. Before publishing, we check that the app is still available from its developer and that we can verify the basic facts from an official source. If we can't confirm a fact, such as the minimum Android version, we leave it off the page rather than guess.
