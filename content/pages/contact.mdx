## What to include

- **Corrections or outdated versions:** the page URL, what's wrong, and a link to the official source that shows the right information (the developer's site or the Google Play listing).
- **App requests:** the app's name and its Google Play link or official website. We only cover original apps, so we can't add modified builds.
- **Copyright or trademark requests:** follow the steps on our [DMCA page](/dmca) so we have everything we need to act quickly.
- **Privacy and data requests:** say what you're asking for (access, correction or deletion). Our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) explains what we hold.

## What we can't help with

We don't make the apps we list, so we can't fix bugs, recover accounts or process refunds. For those, contact the app's developer. Their official website is linked at the top of each app page.
