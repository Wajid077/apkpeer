## How to calculate your age

1. Enter your **date of birth**.
2. The **Age on this date** field is set to today. Change it to work out your age on any past or future date.
3. Your exact age appears in years, months and days, along with totals and your next birthday.

## How exact age is worked out

The calculator counts on the calendar, the way people do. It first counts complete years, then complete months, then the remaining days, using the real length of each month. That's why someone born on 31 January is considered one month older on 28 February in a normal year, and why the "days" part can change size from month to month.

The total number of days is exact, including leap years.

## Birthdays on 29 February

People born on 29 February only have a real birthday every four years. In other years, the calculator counts the birthday as 1 March, which is how many countries treat it for legal purposes, such as reaching the age to drive or vote. Some people prefer to celebrate on 28 February instead.

## Age on a specific date

Many forms ask for your age "as of" a certain date, such as the start of a school year, a competition entry deadline or a job application closing date. Set **Age on this date** to that date for an exact answer.
