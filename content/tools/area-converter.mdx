## How to convert area

1. Type an area in the **Value** box.
2. Choose its unit, such as square feet.
3. See the same area in square centimetres, square metres, hectares, square kilometres, square inches, square feet, square yards, acres and square miles.

## Why area conversions catch people out

Area grows with the square of length. A metre is about 3.28 feet, but a square metre is about 10.76 square feet, not 3.28. That's why simply converting the length factor gives wrong answers for floors, rooms and plots.

## Floors, rooms and property

Property listings use square feet in some countries and square metres in others. To estimate flooring, tiles or paint, measure the room's length and width, multiply them to get the area, then convert here if your materials are sold in the other unit. Add 5–10% extra for cutting and waste.

## Land and plots

Farmland and large plots are measured in acres or hectares. A football pitch is about 0.7 hectares, or roughly 1.75 acres.

## Local land units

Many regions use traditional land units such as the marla, kanal, bigha or guntha. Their sizes vary from place to place, sometimes even between neighbouring districts, so this converter doesn't include them. Convert to square feet or square metres here, then apply the definition used in your area or on your property documents.
