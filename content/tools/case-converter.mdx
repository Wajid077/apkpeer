## How to change text case

1. Paste or type your text into the **Input** box.
2. Tap the style you want: **UPPERCASE**, **lowercase**, **Title Case**, **Sentence case** or **aLtErNaTiNg**.
3. The result appears instantly. Tap **Copy result** and paste it wherever you need it.

You can switch between styles as often as you like; the input never changes, so you won't lose your original text.

## Which case should you use?

| Style | Example | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UPPERCASE | NEW APP UPDATE | Short labels, warnings, acronyms |
| lowercase | new app update | Tags, usernames, code |
| Title Case | New App Update | Headings, titles, names |
| Sentence case | New app update | Normal writing, captions, emails |
| aLtErNaTiNg | nEw aPp uPdAtE | Jokes and memes |

Sentence case is the most readable choice for most writing, including headings on websites. All-capital text is harder to read in long passages and can come across as shouting in messages.

## Common fixes

- **Caps Lock accidents.** If you typed a paragraph with Caps Lock on, choose Sentence case to turn it back into normal text in one step.
- **Inconsistent headings.** Paste a list of headings, one per line, and convert them all to the same style.
- **Copied titles in capitals.** Some websites display titles in capitals; converting them to Title Case or Sentence case makes them ready for documents.

After converting, check proper nouns, brand names and acronyms such as APK, VPN or WhatsApp. The converter follows simple rules and can't know which words should keep special capitals. You can tidy the result further with the [find and replace](/tools/find-and-replace) tool.

## Private by design

Conversion happens in your browser as you type. Your text is never uploaded or stored, so it's safe to use with private notes and documents.
