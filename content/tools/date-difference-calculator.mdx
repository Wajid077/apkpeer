## How to count days between dates

1. The **start date** is set to today. Change it if you need to.
2. Choose the **end date**.
3. Tick **Include the end date** if both the first and last days should count.
4. See the difference in days, weeks and days, years, months and days, weekdays and hours.

If the end date is before the start date, the calculator swaps them, so you always get a positive answer.

## Should you include the end date?

| Question | Include end date? | Example (Mon to Fri) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How many days until an event? | No | 4 days |
| How many days is a holiday or booking? | Yes | 5 days |
| How long is a notice period? | Check your contract | 4 or 5 days |

Including the end date counts both the first and last day, which is how hotel stays, leave requests and event durations are often counted.

## Weekdays and working days

The calculator counts weekdays (Monday to Friday) in the period. It doesn't know about public holidays, which differ by country and region. To get working days, subtract any holidays that fall on weekdays in your period.

## Years, months and days

The years-months-days figure counts on the calendar. Because months have different lengths, "1 month" might be 28, 30 or 31 days, depending on which months the period covers. For precise durations, the total days figure is the most reliable.

- **Countdowns** to exams, weddings, trips or app launches.
- **Deadlines** for assignments, warranties and returns.
- **Notice periods** and probation periods at work.
- **Pregnancy and baby milestones**, together with the [age calculator](/tools/age-calculator).
- **Project planning**, counting working days available.
