## How to calculate a discount

1. Enter the **original price**.
2. Enter the **discount** percentage.
3. If there's a second offer, such as an extra coupon, enter it under **Extra discount**.
4. See the final price, how much you save and the true total discount.

## The formula

Final price = original price × (1 − discount ÷ 100)

For a 20% discount on 2,500: 2,500 × 0.80 = **2,000**, saving **500**.

## Stacked discounts

Shops often advertise "an extra 10% off sale prices". That second discount applies to the already-reduced price, so the savings don't simply add up:

| Offer | You might expect | Actual total discount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20% + 10% | 30% | 28% |
| 30% + 20% | 50% | 44% |
| 50% + 50% | 100% (free!) | 75% |

The calculator applies discounts one after the other, the way shops do, and shows the real overall percentage.

## Shopping tips

- **Compare final prices, not percentages.** 30% off an expensive item can still cost more than a cheaper item at full price.
- **Check the "original" price.** Some sales inflate the original price to make the discount look bigger. Price history tools and comparing several shops help.
- **Remember tax and delivery.** Discounts usually apply before sales tax, and delivery charges can cancel out a small saving. Add tax to the final price with the [sales tax calculator](/tools/sales-tax-calculator).
- **App and game sales.** Paid apps and in-game items go on sale regularly on Google Play. Knowing the real saving helps decide whether a deal is worth it.
