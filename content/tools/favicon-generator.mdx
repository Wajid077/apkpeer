## How to make a favicon

1. Tap **Choose a logo or image** and select a square image, ideally at least 512 × 512 pixels.
2. The generator creates a **favicon.ico** and PNG icons in six sizes.
3. Download the files you need and upload them to your website's root folder.
4. Copy the HTML snippet into your page's `<head>`.

If your image isn't square, it's cropped from the center, so keep the important part of your logo in the middle.

## What each file is for

| File | Size | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| favicon.ico | 16, 32, 48 px | Browser tabs, bookmarks, Windows |
| favicon-16x16.png | 16 px | Browser tabs |
| favicon-32x32.png | 32 px | Browser tabs on high-resolution screens |
| favicon-48x48.png | 48 px | Google search results, Windows |
| apple-touch-icon.png | 180 px | iPhone and iPad home screens |
| icon-192.png | 192 px | Android home screens, web app manifests |
| icon-512.png | 512 px | Splash screens, web app manifests, install prompts |

The ICO file holds several sizes inside one file, and browsers pick the best one for the screen.

## Design tips for small icons

A favicon is shown as small as 16 pixels, where detail disappears:

- **Simplify.** Use your logo mark or first letter, not the full logo with text.
- **Use strong contrast** so the icon is visible on both light and dark browser tabs.
- **Fill the square.** Thin icons with lots of empty space look tiny in tabs.
- **Check it small.** Look at the 16 px preview before you publish.

## Favicons in Google search

Google shows your favicon next to your site in mobile search results. Google asks for a square favicon that's a multiple of 48 px and clearly represents your brand, and it can take some time for a new favicon to appear after Google recrawls your site.
