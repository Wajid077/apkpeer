## How to find and replace text

1. Paste your text into the **Input** box.
2. Type what to look for in **Find** and the new text in **Replace with**.
3. Choose options: **Match case**, **Whole words** or **Regular expression**.
4. Check the replacement count, then copy the result.

The result updates as you type, so you can see exactly what will change before you copy it.

## Options explained

| Option | Effect | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Match case | "Apple" won't match "apple" | Replacing a brand name only where it's capitalized |
| Whole words | "cat" won't match "category" | Renaming a word without breaking longer words |
| Regular expression | Use patterns instead of plain text | Removing all numbers, reformatting dates |

## Regular expression examples

| Find | Replace with | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `\d+` | (empty) | Removes all numbers |
| `\s{2,}` | a single space | Collapses runs of spaces |
| `(\w+)@(\w+)` | `$1 at $2` | Rewrites email-style text |
| `^` | `- ` | Adds a dash at the start of every line (tick Regular expression) |

In the replacement, `$1`, `$2` and so on insert the text matched by the first, second and later groups in parentheses. If a pattern isn't valid, the tool shows an error instead of changing your text.

## Everyday uses

- Updating a product name, price or date throughout a document.
- Removing tracking codes such as `?utm_source=` from a list of links.
- Turning a comma-separated list into lines, ready for the [sort lines](/tools/sort-lines) tool.

## Private and safe

The text never leaves your browser, so you can use it with contracts, code and other private material.
