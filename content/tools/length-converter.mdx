## How to convert length

1. Type a number in the **Value** box.
2. Choose its unit under **From**.
3. Every other unit updates instantly. Tap **Copy** next to the one you need.

The row for the unit you started with is highlighted, so it's easy to see what you converted from.

## Exact conversion factors

The imperial units in this converter are defined exactly in metric terms by international agreement, so conversions are exact rather than approximate:

| Unit | In metres |
| --- | --- |
| 1 inch | 0.0254 m (2.54 cm) |
| 1 foot | 0.3048 m (12 inches) |
| 1 yard | 0.9144 m (3 feet) |
| 1 mile | 1,609.344 m (1,760 yards) |
| 1 nautical mile | 1,852 m |

## Height in feet and inches

Height is often given in feet and inches, such as 5'7". To convert centimetres to that format, convert to inches, then divide by 12. The whole number is feet and the remainder is inches. For example, 170 cm is 66.93 inches: 5 feet and 6.93 inches, usually rounded to 5'7".

## Screen sizes

Phone, tablet and TV screen sizes are measured diagonally in inches. A 6.7-inch phone screen is about 17 cm corner to corner. Type the size in inches here to see it in centimetres.
