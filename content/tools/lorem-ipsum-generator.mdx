## How to generate lorem ipsum

1. Choose how many items you need and whether they're **paragraphs**, **sentences** or **words**.
2. Decide whether to start with the classic "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet".
3. Tap **Regenerate** for a fresh variation, then **Copy**.

Each paragraph has a slightly different length and each sentence a different word count, so blocks of placeholder text look natural rather than identical.

## Where lorem ipsum comes from

Lorem ipsum is scrambled Latin taken from *De finibus bonorum et malorum*, a work by the Roman philosopher Cicero written in 45 BC. Printers began using it as dummy text in the 1500s, and it became standard in design software in the 1980s. The words look like real language, with a natural mix of short and long words, but they don't form meaningful sentences, so viewers focus on the design rather than reading the content.

## When to use placeholder text

| Use it for | Avoid it for |
| --- | --- |
| Website and app mockups | Live pages that search engines can see |
| Testing fonts and line spacing | User testing, where real content matters |
| Checking how layouts handle long text | Final print or published material |

Real content often differs from placeholder text in length and structure. Headlines get longer, product names don't fit, and paragraphs break differently. Replace lorem ipsum with draft copy as early as you can, so the design is tested against reality.

## Don't forget to remove it

Pages accidentally published with lorem ipsum look unfinished to visitors, and search engines treat them as low-quality content. Before launch, search your site or app for "lorem" to catch any leftovers.
