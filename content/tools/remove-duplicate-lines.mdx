## How to remove duplicate lines

1. Paste your list into the **Input** box, one item per line.
2. Choose your options: **Trim spaces**, **Ignore case** and **Remove empty lines**.
3. The cleaned list appears instantly, with a count of how many lines were removed.
4. Tap **Copy result**.

The first time a line appears it's kept in its original position, and every later copy is removed.

## When duplicates sneak in

Duplicate lines are common in:

- **Email and contact lists** merged from several sources.
- **Keyword lists** gathered from different research tools.
- **Exported data** from spreadsheets, logs and apps.
- **Lists of links** collected over time.

Often the "duplicates" aren't quite identical. One copy might have a space at the end, or a capital letter at the start. That's what the options are for:

| Option | Treats these as the same |
| --- | --- |
| Trim spaces | "apple" and "apple " |
| Ignore case | "Apple" and "apple" |
| Remove empty lines | Drops blank lines completely |

With Ignore case on, the version that appears first is the one that's kept, so put the spelling you prefer at the top.

## Tips

- To sort the cleaned list alphabetically, paste the result into the [sort lines](/tools/sort-lines) tool.
- If your items are separated by commas instead of lines, use [find and replace](/tools/find-and-replace) to replace ", " with a new line first, or tick its regular expression option and replace `,\s*` with `\n`.
- Spreadsheet data can include invisible characters such as non-breaking spaces. Running it through [remove extra spaces](/tools/remove-extra-spaces) first helps duplicates match.

## Your data stays private

The list is processed in your browser and never uploaded, which matters when it contains email addresses or customer names. Close the page and the data is gone.
