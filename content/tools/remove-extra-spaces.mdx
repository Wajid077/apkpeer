## How to remove extra spaces

1. Paste your text into the **Input** box.
2. Choose what to clean: **Convert tabs to spaces**, **Trim each line** and **Collapse empty lines**.
3. Copy the cleaned result.

Double spaces are always reduced to single spaces, and leading and trailing spaces are removed from the whole text.

## What gets cleaned

| Problem | What the tool does |
| --- | --- |
| Double or triple spaces | Replaced with a single space |
| Tabs | Turned into spaces (optional) |
| Spaces at the start or end of lines | Removed (optional) |
| Several blank lines in a row | Reduced to one blank line (optional) |
| Non-breaking spaces | Treated as normal spaces |

Non-breaking spaces deserve a special mention. They look exactly like ordinary spaces but behave differently: they stop text from wrapping and make searches and duplicate checks fail. They often sneak in when copying from websites, Word documents or PDFs, and they're a common cause of mysterious formatting problems.

## When to use it

- Cleaning up text copied from **PDFs or websites** before pasting it into a document or app.
- Tidying **lists and data** before checking for duplicates or sorting.
- Fixing **double spaces after full stops**, an old typewriter habit that looks out of place online.
- Preparing **app descriptions, captions and bios** with character limits, where every space counts.

## Tips

- If your text also has unwanted line breaks, run [remove line breaks](/tools/remove-line-breaks) first, then this tool.
- To check the length after cleaning, use the [word counter](/tools/word-counter), which counts characters with and without spaces.

## Private by design

Everything runs in your browser. Your text isn't uploaded, logged or stored.
