## How to create a URL slug

1. Paste a title into the **Input** box, or several titles, one per line.
2. Choose a separator: **hyphen** (recommended) or underscore.
3. Copy the slugs.

For example, "My Post Title: Here's What's New!" becomes `my-post-title-heres-whats-new`.

## What the generator does

- Converts letters to **lowercase**.
- Removes **accents**, so "Café Münster" becomes `cafe-munster`.
- Deletes **apostrophes**, so "What's New" becomes `whats-new` rather than `what-s-new`.
- Replaces spaces and symbols with your **separator**.
- Removes separators from the **start and end**.

## What makes a good slug?

| Good | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `/app/brave-browser` | Short, readable, describes the page |
| `/tools/qr-code-generator` | Contains the words people search for |

| Avoid | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `/p?id=48213` | Tells people and search engines nothing |
| `/brave-browser-2026` | The year makes the URL go out of date |
| `/My_Post_Title` | Mixed case and underscores are harder to read and share |

Keep slugs short: three to five words is plenty. Remove filler words like "the", "a" and "and" if the slug still makes sense without them.

## Changing a slug later

Once a page is published and linked to, changing its slug breaks links and loses search ranking unless you add a 301 redirect from the old address to the new one. It's worth choosing a good slug before you publish.
