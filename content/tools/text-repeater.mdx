## How to repeat text

1. Type the word, sentence or emoji you want to repeat in the **Input** box.
2. Set **Repeat how many times?** (up to 10,000).
3. Choose how to separate the copies: new line, space, comma or nothing.
4. Tap **Copy result**.

## Choosing a separator

| Separator | Result for "Hi" × 3 | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New line | Hi / Hi / Hi on separate lines | Lists, test data |
| Space | Hi Hi Hi | Sentences, chat messages |
| Comma | Hi, Hi, Hi | CSV-style data |
| Nothing | HiHiHi | Emoji strings, patterns |

## What people use it for

- **Chat messages.** Repeating an emoji or a short phrase for fun with friends.
- **Testing.** Developers and designers use long repeated text to check how layouts, text boxes and apps handle lots of content.
- **Templates.** Creating numbered placeholders or repeated lines to fill in later.
- **Learning.** Writing practice lines for handwriting or spelling.

## Be considerate

Sending very long repeated messages to people who didn't ask for them is annoying and can count as spam. Messaging apps may slow down, limit or ban accounts that flood chats with repeated text. Keep it for fun with friends who are in on the joke, or for testing.

Very large results, such as a long sentence repeated thousands of times, can be too big for some apps to paste. If an app struggles, reduce the count.
