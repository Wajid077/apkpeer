## How to convert weight

1. Type a number in the **Value** box.
2. Choose its unit under **From**.
3. See it in milligrams, grams, kilograms, tonnes, ounces, pounds and stones at once, and copy the one you need.

## Exact definitions

| Unit | In kilograms |
| --- | --- |
| 1 ounce (oz) | 0.028349523125 kg (28.35 g) |
| 1 pound (lb) | 0.45359237 kg (16 ounces) |
| 1 stone (st) | 6.35029318 kg (14 pounds) |
| 1 metric tonne (t) | 1,000 kg |

Because the pound is defined exactly as 0.45359237 kg, conversions between metric and imperial weights are exact.

## Weight in stones and pounds

In the UK and Ireland, body weight is often given in stones and pounds, such as 11 st 4 lb. To convert kilograms: find the weight in pounds, divide by 14 for whole stones, and the remainder is pounds. 72 kg is 158.7 lb, which is 11 stone and 4.7 pounds.

## Cooking and shipping

Recipes from different countries switch between grams and ounces, and the converter makes it easy to follow either. For cooking, note that US recipes often use cups, which measure volume rather than weight, so a cup of flour and a cup of sugar weigh different amounts.

Courier and postal services usually charge by weight bands in kilograms or pounds, so converting before you ship helps estimate costs.

## Ton vs tonne

A metric tonne is 1,000 kg. The US "short ton" is 2,000 lb (about 907 kg) and the UK "long ton" is 2,240 lb (about 1,016 kg). This converter uses the metric tonne.
